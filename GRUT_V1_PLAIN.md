# A Responsive-Vacuum Framing of Gravity, Audited: What Survived

**Abstract.** This document assembles the audited record of GRUT, a research program that models the gravitational vacuum as a responsive medium with finite memory. Its primary product is a method: every claim held in a machine-checked register, tiered by epistemic status, sourced, paired with a named falsifier, and priced by the assumptions it adds — currently net +13, a number the program treats as its honesty metric. The physics output is deliberately bounded: zero novel positive predictions, four derived negative boundaries on what the framework itself can and cannot do, one decomposition of the thermodynamic arrow of time, and one open question dispatched to outside specialists. The record includes the program's retractions — an uncomputed 32σ figure recomputed at ~2σ, a flagship bound demoted by its own audit — and a hash-sealed termination document fixing the meaning of every possible ending in advance. Nothing here is externally validated, and the text says so.

# Part I — What this program is, and the method that is its real product

## 1. The physics premise, stated as a framing under test

GRUT (the "Grand Responsive Universe Theory," a name that overpromises more than the program does) rests on a single physical stance: model the gravitational vacuum not as an inert stage but as a **responsive medium with finite memory** — a medium that reacts to what happens in it and relaxes on a finite timescale — and treat that medium with the standard toolkit of open quantum systems. Concretely, the machinery is the in-in (Schwinger–Keldysh) closed-time-path formalism, influence functionals for the integrated-out environment, Mori–Zwanzig memory-kernel projections, and the fluctuation–dissipation theorem with its KMS (thermal detailed-balance) condition. Those four tools are standard physics, and the program's source register — its maintained list of citations — records each as **verified against the primary literature**. That verification is the foundation, and it is the only thing in the program graded that strongly.

Everything above the foundation is explicitly a **framing under test, not a discovery**. The program's own constitution says so in as many words: *"This is not a theory of everything, and any draft that starts sounding like one has drifted."* The commitment is not "responsiveness explains everything." The commitment is to mark, ruthlessly and in machine-checkable form, where responsiveness is *shown* versus merely *assumed* — and the stated win condition is not zero assumptions but a **short, marked list of them**. As of this writing the program's summary of its physics output reads: zero novel positive predictions, four computed or obstruction-backed *negative* boundaries, and one structural demonstration that a load-bearing ingredient of its own framework was a choice rather than a consequence. That the program counts these among its best results is the point of everything that follows.

## 2. The discipline: every claim tiered, sourced, falsifiable, and priced

The real product of the program is not the physics stance; it is the bookkeeping system the stance is held inside. Every claim lives as a record in a machine-readable register, and a validator — a blocking script run before anything is accepted, called "the gate" below — enforces four requirements on every record:

- **Tiered.** Each claim declares its epistemic status from a fixed vocabulary: `shown` (verified against primary sources), `derived` (follows from the foundation, with the derivation exhibited and checked), `derived-pending` (derived modulo a *named* open input), `assumed` (an underived input, named at the point it enters), or `to-derive` (an open target). No untiered claim enters any document.
- **Sourced.** Each claim cites sources that must resolve in the separately maintained source register.
- **Falsifiable.** Each claim names an "overturning computation" — a concrete calculation that could kill it. A claim nothing could kill is rejected by the gate.
- **Priced.** Each claim carries a signed integer: the number of underived assumptions it adds (+) or genuinely removes (−).

The validator sums those integers blindly across the register. The current total, computed by a script inside the repository, is **net +13** across 45 claims (34 belonging to the physics program proper, 11 pieces of borrowed scaffolding tracked under a separate label). In plain terms: after every derivation the program has managed, its framework stands on thirteen more underived inputs than it has discharged. Among them, named one by one in the register: the split of the world into system and environment, the general-relativity limit imports (area entropy, Unruh temperature), the Born rule, the low-entropy past boundary condition that gives dissipation its direction.

Why would a program *advertise* that number? Because the number is the honesty metric, and its arithmetic is public. The validator prints it on every run with the annotation *"win condition is a SHORT marked list, not zero — every +1 is flagged, not hidden."* The count can move in the uncomfortable direction, and it has: the ledger's only net movement in the program's recorded history was **upward**, +12 to +13, when a shape restriction in the dark-energy sector was discovered to be an input nobody had recorded, and was recorded. A program that hides its assumptions can always claim to have few; a program that prices them in a signed sum a script computes has removed the easiest way to do that. One further disclosure sits on the gate's own printed face: +8 of the +13 is carried under declared waivers (explained next), and the validator itself lists them on every run, so that fact is visible without an audit.

## 3. Anti-laundering: how a "derivation" cheats, and how the gate blocks it

The failure mode the whole apparatus is aimed at has a name in the code: **laundering** — a claim graded as a result (`shown`, `derived`, or `derived-pending`) that carries a *positive* assumption price. That combination is a derivation that quietly expanded the assumption list: the paper-facing sentence says "we derive X," while the fine print smuggles in a new input the derivation needed. In ordinary practice this is invisible, because the grade lives in prose and the assumption lives in a different paragraph. Here the grade and the price are adjacent fields in the same record, and the validator **blocks the build** on the combination.

There is an escape hatch, and it is deliberately expensive. A claim may declare a waiver, converting the block into an openly printed stance — but a waiver without a written justification is itself a blocking violation. The code's comment states the principle: a waiver that costs nothing is a self-granted exemption.

The gate's honesty extends to its own limits, which its docstring states bluntly: it verifies **discipline, not truth**. A physically wrong but well-provenanced claim passes. The blind sum cannot detect a physics error, and it cannot detect a double-count (one narrow double-count risk — a normalization cost that could have been priced twice — is closed by a hand-written check specific to this repository, and the code says exactly that). Even the gate's summary line has a correction history: the top line once said "no laundering," which claimed more than the code enforces; the README records the correction — *"the guard was honest, the narrative had drifted"* — and notes it was the tenth recorded instance of that drift pattern.

## 4. The human–AI structure, and what the guards actually caught

The program is built by AI collaborators generating and checking derivations, calculations, and audits, under a human overseer — and the discipline is designed on the working assumption that the AI collaborators **will overclaim if permitted to**. The constitution names this the directional-optimism rule: the generate-and-check loop reliably errs in one direction, toward strengthening the result, and errs worst exactly where a result is wanted. So any claim that would resolve an open question is presumed overclaimed until an adversarial pre-screen — a review explicitly charged to break the claim, defaulting to "broken" — says otherwise, and every such pre-screen and every tier upgrade is relayed to the human overseer *before* the register accepts it. The overseer verifies **by execution**: running the validator, the gates, and the calculations, not reading summaries of them. Dissents are recorded verbatim inside the claims they dispute — one cosmology claim in the current register carries an overseer-ordered dissent that its favorable tier is "dead in spirit even though alive in letter," left in place precisely so the flattering label cannot quietly outlive the doubt.

None of this would be worth a colleague's time if it were aspiration. What makes it real is the recorded catches — cases where the program's own machinery caught the program. Three, from the close-out audit of the program's second version:

**The significance figure that had never been computed.** The register carried an observational anchor — a measured data point the framework leans on — quoted at roughly 32σ. When someone finally ran the computation, the result was **~2.0σ**, and the mechanism as recorded pointed in the wrong direction. The anchor was retired, the dependent claim was re-anchored to a different observable, and the episode is written into the record as a retraction, not smoothed over.

**The self-test that could not fail.** A calculation producing a load-bearing number shipped with a self-test, as required. A mutation test — rerunning the calculation with its answer deliberately replaced by a wrong value, specifically the one value the calculation's own directional guard forbids — showed the self-test *still passed*. The calculation was rebuilt, and the episode produced a standing, machine-enforced rule: any calculation behind a result-grade number must ship a battery of pre-registered wrong answers, and the battery must demonstrably make the calculation's own self-test fail. The coverage of that rule is partial and the register says so in the open: five batteries cover the current result-grade calculations, and fifteen further register-cited calculations sit on a published owed-list — calculations still owed their battery — that is permitted only to shrink. Installing the rule immediately found more real gaps in calculations whose numbers were already accepted; each is now fixed, each with a dated comment in the code.

**The guard that had to be guarded.** That new rule carried a ratchet — a ceiling meant to guarantee the owed-list only shrinks. The program's own checking then found the ceiling was being **computed from the very list it was supposed to bound**, so the guard could never fail: the exact failure class the rule exists to prevent, reproduced inside the rule itself. It was fixed to a hard-coded constant and verified by execution.

The pattern in all three is the same, and it is the program's honest sales pitch for the method: the errors were generated inside the loop, caught by machinery the loop had been forced to build in advance, and recorded with their repairs. The claim is not that AI collaborators don't err — the record documents that they do, and that the humans do too — but that the discipline converts silent overclaiming into loud, dated, fixable events.

## 5. The method, packaged: claimledger

The discipline engine has been extracted from the physics program into a small domain-free public package, **claimledger** — pure Python standard library, no dependencies, one command: `claimledger audit claims.json sources.json`. It enforces the same five checks (tiered, sourced, falsifiable, priced, not laundered) on any claim set in any field, with a neutral default tier vocabulary (`measured / derived / posited / heuristic / open`), and it prints the waived portion of the net separately, so the headline's dependence on declared stances sits on the report's face. Its README states its limits in the same terms as the parent program's gate: it verifies discipline, not truth; it cannot know whether a tier is earned; its value is that every judgement it cannot make is forced into a declared field a human reviewer can audit rather than living in prose.

The package ships a worked example on a real published result: the claim structure of Du et al. (arXiv:2602.03110, a reported 3.0σ deviation from general relativity in gravitational light deflection), encoded by a third party from the published text. The honest encoding prices the paper's two modeling choices at +1 each and passes at **net +2** — the famous number sits exactly two priced posits deep, *which the paper itself says* (the deviation drops to 2.2σ when one posit is freed; the authors call it a degeneracy). The laundered encoding — the same paper as it tends to get quoted downstream, with the parameterization absorbed into the headline — **fails the gate**. Same science, same numbers; the difference is only in what is declared, and that difference is exactly what the tool measures.

The bounded claim for all of this, stated at its honest grade: what exists is a **competent synthesis** — tiering, sourcing, falsifiability, priced assumptions, adversarial pre-screening, and mutation testing, assembled into one enforced arithmetic and run live for months as the working gate of one small research program, where it blocked real errors written by the AI collaborators it was designed to constrain and by its own authors. It is **externally unvalidated**: no independent team has adopted the tool, audited the register end to end, or reproduced the discipline's value on their own problem. The package's README even discloses its own first bug — a dead-code guard its test suite caught before release, the fix's comment left in the code because "the guard proven to fire found the guard that could not" is the design argument, demonstrated on itself. Whether the method survives contact with an independent team is, in the program's own vocabulary, *open* — and by its own rules it must say so.

*That is the container. Part II reads out what it certified — and what it refused to certify.*

# Part II — The four derived boundaries: what the framework established about itself

## II.0 Orientation: what a "boundary" is here

As Part I stated, the framework models the gravitational vacuum as a responsive open quantum system; concretely, the medium's linearized gravitational response enters cosmology through a kernel of the form K = α·χ·P — a single dimensionless amplitude α, a causal memory function χ, and a projector P selecting which spin components of the metric the medium responds to. The amplitude is adopted as α = a/c = 1/3, the ratio of the two trace-anomaly coefficients of the standard-model fields, conditional on the hypothesis that the metric's conformal mode carries the response. That adoption is marked as an assumption in the program's own records, not presented as a derivation.

Every claim below lives in the machine-checked claims register described in Part I; the grades quoted in this Part — computed in-repo (by rerunnable code in the program's own repository), verified against the published literature, assumed, open — are that register's tier vocabulary at work. The program's chief physics products are **boundaries** — results, most of them negative, about what the framework itself can and cannot do, each computed or backed by a named structural obstruction rather than argued from plausibility.

Four of these are load-bearing, and they are the subject of this Part. A word on selection honesty: the program's own ledger of no-go results lists four "established boundaries," and that list overlaps but does not coincide with the four below. Two of the ledger's entries are dispatched here in one paragraph: gravitational-wave dissipation from the medium's imaginary response is real in the model but computed in-repo to lie 22–62 orders of magnitude below detectability (the same Planck suppression that makes the model solar-system safe); and an "economical" single-parameter evolving dark energy matching the DESI hint is structurally unavailable — a phantom-divide crossing (the dark-energy equation of state passing through w = −1) needs at least two relaxation modes, and the framework's natural cheap candidate cannot supply the second, its stress having the wrong equation of state, w = +1/3. Both results are genuine, both negative, and neither is where the program learned the most about itself. The four below are: the framework's failure to derive its own coupling normalization; the exclusion of the endpoint its own coefficient suggested; the discovery that its central projector is a choice, not a theorem; and the computed refusal of its anomaly action to pin the resulting free parameter.

## II.1 First boundary: the framework cannot derive its own normalization by the route it hoped

**What was hoped.** The kernel's amplitude α = a/c = 1/3 comes from the trace anomaly. The hope was that this was not merely a suggestive number but a derivable normalization: that the anomaly ratio would be shown to fix the coefficient of the transverse-traceless (TT) response, turning the framework's one dimensionless input into an output.

**What was found.** The bridge from the anomaly to the TT normalization is **settled negative on three named obstructions** — a graded verdict the register is careful to distinguish from a formal impossibility proof. The primary obstruction is projector orthogonality, and it is exact algebra: the trace anomaly is a spin-0 object, the TT response is spin-2, and g^μν P^TT = 0 identically. The double trace of the tracefree projector vanishes, so the ratio 1/3 has no shown carrier in the TT channel, and no metric-built map from the scalar sector into the TT sector is known. The secondary obstruction is that the two sectors satisfy independent Ward identities; the tertiary is a UV-versus-IR gap with no renormalization-group protection connecting them. A fourth-looking fact is deliberately *not* counted as an obstruction: the fluctuation–dissipation relation N/Im K = 2coth(ħω/2k_BT) is α-free because α cancels between numerator and denominator — detailed balance fixes the shape and temperature dependence of the noise but cannot rescue the normalization. It does not obstruct; it declines to help. (A later in-repo computation, Section II.4, sharpened the verdict without changing it: each anomaly coefficient turns out to reach exactly one channel — a spin-0 only, c spin-2 only, both at fourth order in momentum — so the *ratio* a/c is the coefficient of nothing. That refinement is computed but was, at the time of the record read here, still held pending the program's internal review step.)

**What survived, and at what grade.** Settled-negative means: no known route, plus a strong structural obstruction, with the escape routes explicitly named — a new metric-built scalar-to-TT intertwiner (a symmetry-respecting map carrying the scalar sector into the spin-2 sector), or a legitimate CFT route in which the Weyl-sector coefficient, which genuinely lives in the spin-2 channel, normalizes the TT two-point function. Impossibility in every extension is *not* claimed. The consequence is booked without flinching: the amplitude c₀ = α is demoted to an **adopted phenomenological parameter** (grade: assumed), and everything downstream that depends on its value is marked conditional on that adoption. The α *value* itself, as a fact about the anomaly ratio, is untouched (grade: verified against the literature); only its hoped-for *role* as a derived normalization died. The failure is recorded as a fixed result — a wall any completion must either breach at a named point or accept as an external input.

## II.2 Second boundary: the endpoint the framework's own coefficient suggested is excluded at the ~4σ class — with a retraction en route

**What was hoped (or rather, feared).** If the medium's response includes a trace (scalar) component with the full strength the conformal coefficient naively suggests, linear cosmological perturbation theory acquires a modified growth parameter μ = 4/3 on large scales — a 33% enhancement of the effective gravitational coupling for structure growth. This is the framework's own would-be signature. The question was whether data exclude it.

**What was computed — including the honest history.** They do, at the joint ~4σ class graded below — but the story of *how* is as instructive as the verdict. The register at one point carried a headline exclusion of roughly 32σ from the integrated Sachs–Wolfe (ISW) cross-correlation channel. That number had been asserted, never computed — this is the first of the three catches recorded in Part I. When the program built the computation — in-repo, and pre-registered, meaning its method and failure conditions were declared before the result was known — three things happened at once. First, the asserted mechanism was found to be **backwards**: μ > 1 strengthens growth, which *suppresses* the decay of gravitational potentials, so the model suppresses the ISW cross-correlation to about 0.57 of the standard template rather than enhancing it. Second, a structural cap was computed: for a signal-suppressing model the cross-channel exclusion can never exceed roughly 9–12σ regardless of inputs, so the 32σ figure was not merely wrong but impossible in that channel. Third, the corrected number is **~2.0σ** (1.97 at central inputs; a pre-registered sweep of the observational inputs moves it across roughly 0.6–4.8). The 32σ claim was retracted, and the retraction is recorded in the same document as the correction. A second, independent audit pass in the same cycle caught a further omitted factor (the lensing-relevant Σ correction) and *strengthened* the corrected verdict slightly.

**What survived.** The exclusion of μ = 4/3 stands, but as a multi-leg case rather than a single kill: the ISW cross-correlation at ~2.0σ (computed in-repo); a DESI lensing-parameter constraint at ~3.5σ (taken from the literature; independent); jointly a ~4σ-class empirical disfavoring; plus a structural consistency argument — a super-horizon adiabatic mode is just a shifted background whose growth the Friedmann equation fixes to δ ∝ a, while super-horizon μ = 4/3 forces δ ∝ a^1.186 — an internal contradiction quantified in a matter-dominated model and graded conditional on adiabaticity and on one assumed identification. The correction had one more consequence worth emphasis: with the ISW channel weakened, the *binding* constraint on the framework's scalar sector shifted from the ISW channel to the DESI lensing bound — with a recorded caveat that the mapping from the survey's parameter to the framework's constant is loose, so the quoted bound is a conservative upper edge, plausibly 2–3 times tighter in truth.

**Grade.** Hybrid: a structural consistency argument (conditional, assumptions named) plus joint empirical disfavoring at the ~4σ class, with the cross-channel leg computed in-repo at ~2σ. Notably, this exclusion does **not** depend on the pure-TT projector assumption of Section II.3 — the program checked, because the alternative would have made the boundary circular. And the flattering converse — "therefore linear cosmology equals ΛCDM, as predicted" — is explicitly *not* credited: that positive-sounding statement rests on the assumed projector and stays at grade open.

## II.3 Third boundary: the central projector is a choice — and what honestly opens when you admit it

**What was hoped.** The framework's kernel uses the transverse-*traceless* projector: the medium responds in spin-2 only, with the scalar (trace) response set identically to zero. The hope — load-bearing for the "linear cosmology = ΛCDM" claim — was that this was forced by symmetry: that diffeomorphism invariance, imposed properly, would leave only the TT structure.

**What was computed.** It is not forced. Imposing the diffeomorphism Ward identity on both branches of the closed-time-path formalism leaves exactly **two** survivors of the six-dimensional space of Lorentz-covariant kernel structures: the spin-2 projector *and* the traceful scalar projector. Diffeomorphism invariance buys transversality; it does not buy tracelessness. The decisive check is that linearized Einstein–Hilbert theory *itself* fails the would-be criterion: its kernel decomposes as k²[−½·(spin-2) + 1·(scalar)] — the scalar coefficient twice the spin-2 one, a ratio of exactly −2, computed in exact rational arithmetic and confirmed by four independent implementations. General relativity's own kernel is not TT-only, so no criterion that GR itself passes can force TT-only. Setting the vacuum's scalar response to zero is therefore a **constitutive assertion, adopted by fiat** — the program's word for its own move is "chosen, not forced." One dynamical escape survives (a vanishing trace correlator in the underlying bath), and the program prices it honestly: taking that route would relocate the assumption into the bath, adding a new entry to the ledger of assumptions rather than discharging one.

**What honestly opens.** Once the scalar sector is admitted as foreclosed by choice rather than by symmetry, the parameter space that choice had deleted must be faced. The program parameterized it: an interior family μ(x) = 1 + xα, with x ∈ [0,1] interpolating from the pure-TT choice (x = 0) to the excluded trace-only endpoint (x = 1). The μ law is exact within the bookkeeping inherited from the TT-only case (computed in-repo); the companion relations η = 1/(1+xα) and Σ = 1 + xα/2 — and with them the identity Σ−1 = (μ−1)/2 — are graded conditional on that bookkeeping's one-sidedness, since a genuine scalar admixture could split them via scalar anisotropic stress (grade: open). The family has a **computed observational ceiling**: the register's accepted edge is x < ~0.59, from the conservatively mapped DESI lensing bound of Section II.2, corresponding to μ−1 ≤ ~0.20.

**The label, stated without softening — because the program states it without softening:** the family has a ceiling and **no floor**. It *allows* deviations up to the edge; it *predicts* nothing. No detection of μ ≠ 1 would confirm the framework, and no null result would refute it, because nothing pins x away from zero. The register grades this as its own category — "constrained to a computed window": parameter space bounded by measurement rather than deleted by assertion. The demotion of the projector from theorem to choice is, by the program's own account, its single most important structural finding — and it is a finding *against* itself.

## II.4 Fourth boundary: the anomaly action was asked to pin the free parameter — computed answer, no pin

**What was hoped.** With x floorless, one in-house route remained that could conceivably pin it: the anomaly-induced effective action. If that action, expanded to quadratic order, assigned x a definite value — the aesthetically hoped-for candidate was x = α² ≈ 0.111 — the family's "allows" would convert into the program's first genuine percent-level prediction.

**What was computed.** An in-repo calculation, in exact rational arithmetic, decomposed every relevant local curvature-squared operator onto the covariant projector basis, with the results mutation-tested: deliberately corrupted answers, including the specific wished-for value x = α², were injected to confirm the test suite catches them (five out of five caught). The verdict is **NO PIN**, in three computed parts:

1. **The anomaly delivers a scale, not a number.** The induced coefficient is a momentum-dependent form factor, x(k) = [1/(3α)]·k²/(k² + M²), with M² set by an R² counterterm coefficient. Its zero-momentum limit is exactly zero, scheme-immune, *in flat space* — and the program declines to extend that claim to curved backgrounds, where two independent in-house power counts disagree; the curved case is recorded as **halted** (stopped without a verdict), not as zero. Cosmology is the curved case, and the record says so.
2. **The wished-for value is structurally unreachable.** Both anomaly coefficients were traced through the two-point kernel: the a-coefficient reaches the scalar channel only, the c-coefficient the spin-2 channel only, both at fourth order in momentum — and fourth-order operators cannot shift a second-order Poisson coupling by a momentum-independent fraction. The net power of α in the dynamical result is **zero, as a computed output**: the ratio a/c is the coefficient of nothing, and x = α² cannot arise by this route. The confirmation-bias trap the calculation was pre-registered against did not spring.
3. **The residual freedom is the finding.** The anomaly's own contribution across the cosmological band is at least 108 orders of magnitude below every recorded observational bound — far enough down that no caveat about comparing across channels matters. But the counterterm coefficient is free and unbounded, and with it the form factor *can* reach the observable window (at coefficient values ~10^110 — ordinary f(R)-dark-energy territory, which DESI actually constrains). The calculation explicitly refuses the flattering summary "the anomaly cannot reach the window": what it establishes is that the anomaly does not *choose*.

The residual burden is localized, at the grade the source assigns it and not above: a scheme-immune normalization would require non-analytic zero-frequency structure in the scalar channel, which the local flat-space sector lacks (by exhaustive enumeration) and which the anomaly's own non-analytic structure — living in the spin-2 channel — cannot supply. That points at one named external computation: the zero-frequency limit of the vacuum's scalar self-energy in de Sitter space, an infrared computation the program has written up as a brief for outside specialists rather than attempted beyond its competence. This localization is graded **argued, explicitly not proven**, and travels with a three-way scope split: the local flat-space enumeration is unconditional; the nonlocal closure is conditional on the conformal-mode hypothesis of Section II.0 and closed only by a dimensional argument; the curved case is halted.

**One more honesty note.** The first build of this calculation is the "self-test that could not fail" episode recorded in Part I: its two most load-bearing algebraic facts lived in print statements rather than in tested code, and a mutation test showed the self-test would have passed with the answer replaced by x = α² — the one outcome the calculation's pre-registered guard against confirmation bias says must be treated as an error. The physics survived an independent reimplementation; the artifact did not, and was rebuilt. (The standing mutation-battery rule that episode produced, and its published owed-list, are described in Part I.) At the time of the record read here, the NO PIN result itself was still held out of the claims register pending the program's internal verification step.

## II.5 The demotion of the flagship bound: the activation-scale result

The story of the four boundaries has a coda that is itself a boundary — this time on the program's own strongest number.

Alongside the ISW work, the program built a second, more powerful observational test of the interior family of Section II.3 — a "gate," in its vocabulary: a computation that turns a data set into a bound on x. The data set was the low-multipole temperature auto-power spectrum, and the gate's history compresses the program's method into one artifact. The **first** frozen version — frozen meaning locked as an immutable record, changeable only by public retraction — was voided within a day by the program's own internal adversarial review: a numerical-recurrence bug made every headline constant noise (the quoted edge moved by a factor of ~8 on a single resolution doubling). The rebuilt, independently cross-validated version was re-frozen with what looked like the flagship result: an **unconditional bound x < 0.358** at the most conservative corner, with all sharper verdicts — including a 6.2σ exclusion of x = α² — explicitly conditional on κ, a filter parameter encoding the scale at which the scalar response activates inside the horizon. The record already stated plainly that cosmological-parameter uncertainties move the edges by under 1% and that **the filter is the entire systematic uncertainty**.

Then, in a later audit dated 2026-08-09, the program demoted its own flagship. Three defects, each computed or proven rather than argued. First, the filter parameter has **no upper bound in any recorded physics**, so "unconditional" held only over the range of κ actually scanned: push κ to 10 and the edge relaxes to 0.55; to 30, and it is 0.90 — reproduced by three independent implementations. Second, the sharp-step filter shape is **provably not the momentum dependence of any causal finite-memory kernel** (a Paley–Wiener-type causality argument), with no licensed replacement. Third — the decisive point — the pipeline contains **no approximation the filter could be excusing**: the line-of-sight integration is exact, so the filter is not a conservative correction to anything but an **insertion of physical content that appears nowhere in the claims register**, sitting exactly on the scales (k/aH roughly 1.7–15.7) where the bulk of the ISW signal lives and the register is silent. The quotable object now is: x < 0.358 *conditional on the declared filter family*; **no unconditional bound on x exists from this gate**; every number it emits is marked as a property of the model-plus-filter; and the only survivors are a floor κ ≥ 1 and a still-owed differential check against a full Boltzmann code before any strong use of the loose-filter regime.

The demotion is the point. A program whose best remaining bound was its own product, and whose audit machinery found that the bound rested on an undeclared assumption — and then *published the demotion against itself* — is exhibiting the one behavior that distinguishes bookkeeping-as-discipline from bookkeeping-as-theater.

## II.6 What the four boundaries jointly establish

Read together, the four results are a framework mapping its own outline from the inside. It hoped to derive its coupling normalization: settled negative on named obstructions, escape routes listed, the coupling demoted to an assumed input (II.1). Its own coefficient suggested an observable endpoint: disfavored at the joint ~4σ class — after retracting, on computing it, a headline number that turned out to be both uncomputed and mechanistically backwards (II.2). Its central structural assumption was interrogated and found to be a choice, not a theorem — opening a one-parameter family with a computed ceiling and no floor: an allowance, never a prediction (II.3). Its last in-house route to converting that allowance into a prediction was computed and returned no pin — a scale, not a number, the wished-for value structurally unreachable, the residual burden localized at argued grade onto one named external computation (II.4). And when its sharpest remaining bound was audited, the program demoted it for resting on an undeclared insertion (II.5).

None of this is new positive physics, and the program says so in its own summary documents: zero novel positive predictions; the falsifiable content is the no-go directions. What the four boundaries are, instead, is a finite and named account of exactly what any completion of this framework must supply: a scalar-to-TT intertwiner or an accepted external coupling; a scalar sector either derived away or priced as an assumption; a pin for x — or, failing that, the admission that the framework is a characterization of the vacuum plus its boundaries, not a predictor; and an activation-scale answer that would make the surviving bounds unconditional. Whether or not the underlying bet about the vacuum is right, that account is the part that was *established* — and most of it was established in the negative.

*The boundaries above are the framework mapping itself. Part III turns to the program's cleanest stand-alone result, its current standing, and its written ending.*

# Part III — The arrow of time, where the program stands, and how it ends

## 1. The arrow of time: existence is intrinsic, direction is imported

The program's cleanest single result is not about gravity at all. It is a decomposition of the thermodynamic arrow of time, developed inside the framework but stated so as to stand independently of it, with a runnable demonstration in plain standard-library Python in the repository (`calc/arrow_origin.py`).

The setting is standard: an open quantum system obtained by integrating out an environment ("bath") from time-symmetric microscopic dynamics, using the closed-time-path (in-in) influence-functional formalism of Feynman and Vernon. The claim splits in two, and the halves carry different strengths of evidence — stated here exactly as the program itself rates them.

**The existence and magnitude of dissipation are intrinsic to the formalism.** For a Gaussian bath, the reduction yields a retarded, causal self-energy that is analytic in the upper half frequency plane, a positive spectral measure, and a positive-semidefinite noise kernel. These are operator identities — proven at the level of the formalism and checked against the primary literature — and they require no assumption about the system's initial state. *That* dissipation occurs, and how strong it is, are genuine outputs. This is, on the program's own assessment, the strongest defensible version of an "intrinsic arrow," and its own document calls it real but narrow.

**The direction of relaxation is never fixed by the dynamics.** Nothing above fixes the *sign* — whether coherence damps or anti-damps. The direction enters through a low-entropy condition on the past boundary, and the program's contribution is to argue that three apparently different places it can enter are one and the same assumption: (i) the convention of specifying the density matrix at the past endpoint of the time contour; (ii) the assumption that the bath is thermal at positive temperature — decisive, because by a 1978 theorem of Pusz and Woronowicz, positive-temperature thermal states are exactly the states from which no work can be extracted even from arbitrarily many copies (the "completely passive" states), and a legal population-inverted (negative-temperature) bath state *reverses* the arrow; and (iii) the assumption that system and bath start uncorrelated — the quantum version of Boltzmann's molecular-chaos assumption, which published work confirms can reverse the arrow for suitably entangled initial states (Partovi 2008). Three guises, one imported input: a passive, low-entropy, uncorrelated past-boundary state. That is the Past Hypothesis, re-entering as a boundary condition.

The exactly solvable demonstration, computed in the repository, makes every step visible: on the pure-dephasing independent-boson model, the decoherence function is even in time (the dynamics pick no direction, to machine precision); with finitely many bath modes the coherence *returns to 1* (again at machine precision) at the Poincaré recurrence time, which grows linearly with the number of modes, so irreversible decay exists only in the continuum limit — itself an assumption; and the decay rate is set entirely by the assumed bath temperature.

Two honesty notes the program insists on, and which should be preserved in any retelling. First, this is **methodological, not a solution**: it locates precisely what must be assumed, and where, for the foundations-of-irreversibility literature — it does not derive the arrow, and the program's own claim ledger grades the overall result only as *plausible/adopted* — its label for a working assumption, not a theorem — with existence at proven grade and direction imported as a primitive. Second, the "no dynamics-only route to a direction" conclusion is **scoped, not a universal impossibility theorem**: it is closed only within the equilibrium and non-equilibrium bath classes actually surveyed, and the program adds a tightening against its own interest — even the "intrinsic" positivity results are established relative to a passive reference state, so the import is more total than it first appears, not less.

One corollary is specific to the gravity program. Its own rules impose a hard admission test on every proposed constitutive law of the vacuum: consistency with the fluctuation-dissipation relations of a positive-temperature equilibrium state. That test is *exactly* the object through which the direction of time enters. So the framework does not bury the Past Hypothesis; it makes it the visible, labeled assumption every claim must pass through. The framework clarifies the question. It does not dissolve it, and it says so.

## 2. Where the physics stands now

Stripped of internal bookkeeping, the current state is one external question, one empirical channel, and a ledger that is deliberately unflattering.

**The one decisive external question.** The framework's single load-bearing structural conjecture is that the gravitational vacuum's tensor response has *finite memory* — technically, that the low-frequency spectral density of the graviton's self-interaction on a de Sitter background is a simple pole (short-memory, "Markovian") rather than a branch cut (long-memory). Settling this is a genuine one-loop quantum-field-theory computation on de Sitter space: the gauge-invariantly assembled low-frequency tensor spectral density of the pure-graviton one-loop self-energy — "assembled" meaning source vertex, observer vertex, and external-leg corrections combined into a gauge-invariant observable, infrared-resummed, then continued to zero frequency. After a deliberate literature sweep, the program's finding is that **this computation does not exist in assembled form**: a 2026 paper (JHEP 04 (2026) 159) achieves the required gauge-invariant assembly at one loop on de Sitter, but only for scalar-probe observables, and the bare gauge-fixed graviton self-energy is gauge-dependent in the relevant sector, so the pole-versus-cut question cannot be read off anything published. An in-house test applied to the best available published object returned *under-determined* — a verdict independently reconstructed and slightly tightened by an external specialist, the program's first logged outside check: the un-resummed secular logarithm establishes neither a cut nor a pole. The program's own written rules forbid settling the question in-house (attempting it is defined as an automatic failure), so it has been posed as a one-page ask to the de Sitter community (`DISPATCH_ONE_PAGE.md`). Either answer is publishable stand-alone de Sitter physics regardless of what it does to this program; what each answer means *for* the program is fixed in advance in the termination document below.

**The one empirical channel, bounded above but not below.** The framework's only live empirical surface is the one-parameter family of modified-growth cosmologies opened in Part II: the growth-of-structure parameter μ — the factor rescaling the Newtonian source term in the growth of cosmic structure — takes the form μ = 1 + x·α, with α = 1/3 fixed by the trace anomaly and x an undetermined parameter between 0 (exactly ΛCDM) and 1. The endpoint μ = 4/3 the program rules out at the significance it states: roughly 2σ in the temperature–galaxy cross-correlation (computed in the repository against published survey numbers), joined by an approximately 3.5σ weak-lensing tension — after the retraction, detailed in Part II, of an earlier ~32σ version of the same exclusion. The current screen: weak-lensing amplitude data bound the family from above at roughly x < 0.59 under central inputs and generous systematic allowances, and an estimate-level (not yet rigorous) calculation of the low-multipole CMB temperature auto-correlation quotes x < 0.358 — a figure the audit recorded in Part II.5 has since demoted to *conditional on the declared filter family*, the unresolved activation-scale question being that gate's entire systematic, so no unconditional bound survives from that channel. From *below*, there is nothing: the program computed whether its own anomaly action pins a value of x, and the answer — checked by mutation-testing the calculation after a first version was found defective — is **no pin**. The anomaly fixes a *scale*, not a number; its own contribution lands more than a hundred orders of magnitude below every observational bound, and the remaining coefficient is a free, unbounded convention choice. That freedom is the finding. So the honest statement, in the program's own enforced wording, is an *allowance, never a prediction*: the one channel **may be fully compatible with ΛCDM (x → 0 is open), and the program cannot yet say** — the family has a computed ceiling but no floor.

**The ledger.** The program's own summary of its first phase — the record it will file as its final "deposit," the archived end-of-program report — is: zero novel positive predictions; four *derived boundaries*, negative results the framework genuinely establishes: gravitational-wave dissipation computed 22–62 orders of magnitude below detectability; the anomaly-normalization bridge settled negative on a specific projector-orthogonality obstruction, with the two possible rescue routes named rather than hidden; an economical one-mode evolving dark-energy equation of state settled negative; and μ = 4/3 excluded at the significances above. The program treats these negatives as its principal output, and separately records that every surviving *positive* statement is structural rather than numerical — with the stated corollary, against its own interest, that structural claims survive partly because they stick their necks out less.

## 3. How it ends

The program carries a written termination condition (`provenance/prereg/PREREG_TERMINATION_2026-08-09.txt`), sealed by hash so its outcomes cannot be renegotiated after results arrive, and — its most unusual feature — **not in force until the human owner signs it**, on the stated reasoning that a stop condition the author never committed to is not one. As of the sealed file, the signature block is empty. The file is written outcomes-first: every live empirical front is enumerated, and what each possible result *means* is fixed before any result exists. The rationale it records is the program's own track record: every result whose interpretation was pre-registered stayed honest under pressure, and every number that traveled without pre-registration eventually needed a retraction.

The four channels, summarized:

1. **The specialist dispatch** (the pole-versus-cut question above). A pole-class answer earns the core assumption at one loop; the program may then continue past its stop date, but only under a *new* signed condition — continuation is a decision, not momentum. A cut-class answer refutes the assumption as stated: a pre-committed demotion of the dependent claims executes with no re-litigation, and the program moves to final write-up saying plainly that its one decisive external question was answered against it. No response within six months of sending, after at least one reminder, is recorded as posed-and-unclaimed: the program files its deposit *without* the answer, stating exactly that, and explicitly may not spin non-response as evidence for either side.

2. **The DESI dark-energy channel.** If the next data release establishes a crossing of the w = −1 line at a severity threshold already frozen in writing, the corresponding limb of the framework dies by its own pre-written clause. If the evidence strengthens short of that, the limb's window narrows and no new adjustable ingredient may be added to rescue it. If the data are consistent with a cosmological constant, the limb survives unused and is reported as an untriggered instrument.

3. **The rigorous version of the CMB temperature calculation** (time-boxed: it has a fixed internal deadline). If the surviving window closes to unobservable at every declared member of the μ-family, the empirical surface is gone, and the final deposit is ontology plus derived boundaries plus method — which the file specifies must be said "without euphemism." If a percent-level window survives, it ships as an allowance; the no-floor ruling above forbids the word "prediction."

4. **The method itself** — the machine-checked claim register described in Part I. If an independent team uses it and it catches a real error, confirmed by someone outside the program, the method graduates by its own pre-written criterion into a stand-alone deliverable, independent of every physics outcome above. If there is no external uptake by the stop date, it ships exactly as its own self-assessment already describes it: a competent synthesis of prior art, validated only on itself — which the program's own rules score as the weakest possible kind of evidence. The claim does not inflate at deposit time.

**The date:** in-house physics calculation stops on 2026-12-31, or on completion of the two remaining time-boxed calculations, whichever comes first — with the sole exception that a pole-class answer arriving earlier re-opens continuation under a new signed condition. After the stop, the program may only *consume* external results against its frozen instruments; no new in-house physics may be started. The final deposit is written promptly after the date, whatever state the channels are in, and must report **every** channel in whichever pre-registered outcome obtains, including "still open" stated as such. The file bars its own misuse in one sentence: reporting the ending on a subset of channels because that subset looks better is exactly the selective reporting the program was built to prevent, applied to a conclusion.

That last clause is why the termination document is the method's closing exhibit rather than administrative boilerplate. A research program that pre-registers the meaning of its own possible endings — including the unflattering ones, including "nobody answered" — is applying its central discipline to the largest claim it will ever make about itself: the claim of when to stop.

## What would make it wrong

The program's falsifiers are its own, written before the answers, and they are concrete. If the assembled low-frequency de Sitter computation returns a branch cut, then in the dispatch's own words "the core assumption is refuted as stated — we retire it and say so." If the DESI-favored phantom-crossing dark-energy shape — currently a 3.1σ preference the program records as the live threat, because the passivity argument of Section 1 forbids exactly that shape — consolidates at the pre-frozen threshold, the program's own state file says of itself, "GRUT dies in this channel if it consolidates." And if the rigorous temperature-correlation calculation closes the one surviving observational window at every member of the family, the framework is left with an ontology, four derived boundaries, and a method — an outcome it has pre-committed, in writing, to describing without euphemism.
