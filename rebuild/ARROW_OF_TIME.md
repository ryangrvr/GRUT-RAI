# Where the arrow of time is imported: an existence-versus-direction decomposition for open-system irreversibility

*A standalone result, hardened out of the GRUT responsive-vacuum program but independent of it. The claim below is about the in-in / Feynman–Vernon treatment of irreversibility in general; GRUT enters only as one labeled corollary in §6. Register claim: `arrow_of_time`. Runnable demonstration: `calc/arrow_origin.py` (pure stdlib, runs in seconds).*

## Abstract

For an open quantum system reduced from time-symmetric microscopic dynamics via the closed-time-path (in-in / Schwinger–Keldysh) influence functional, the thermodynamic arrow splits cleanly into two logically separable parts:

- the **existence and magnitude of dissipation** is **intrinsic** to the formalism — a retarded analytic self-energy, a positive Källén–Lehmann spectral measure, and a positive noise kernel, supplied operator-identically with no low-entropy *system* initial state required; whereas
- the **direction** of relaxation is **state-dependent, never dynamics-intrinsic** — fixed, for equilibrium (KMS) baths, by a low-entropy *past-boundary* condition that appears in three interchangeable guises which reduce to a single initial-condition assumption; and, for non-equilibrium baths, by the chosen reservoir state in the Liouvillian.

The contribution is **methodological, not a solution**: it *locates*, precisely and without laundering, exactly where and in what form the arrow is assumed. It does not derive the arrow. We argue this is a real clarifying statement for the foundations-of-irreversibility problem (Boltzmann; Zeh; Albert; Price; Carroll): *here is the precise object you must assume — a passive, low-entropy past-boundary state — named, not hidden.*

## 1. The problem

Microscopic dynamics — unitary quantum evolution, or time-symmetric Hamiltonian flow — distinguish no direction of time. Yet macroscopic behaviour is irreversible. Every account since Boltzmann closes this gap with a low-entropy boundary condition somewhere (the *Past Hypothesis*), and a recurring failure mode is to let that condition enter disguised as something "natural" or "dynamical" and so escape audit. The question this note answers is narrow and auditable: **in the in-in/Feynman–Vernon reduction of a finite-memory bath, what exactly is intrinsic to the dynamics, and what is imported — and where?**

## 2. The decomposition

**Existence is intrinsic.** The in-in/CTP influence functional of a Gaussian bath yields a retarded dissipation kernel $K_R$ and a noise kernel $N$ with: a retarded, analytic self-energy $\Sigma_R(\omega)$ (causal, upper-half-plane analytic); a **positive Källén–Lehmann spectral measure**; and a **positive-semidefinite noise kernel**. These hold operator-identically — they need no assumption about the *system's* initial state. So *that* dissipation occurs, and its *magnitude* $|\mathrm{Im}\,\Sigma_R|$, are genuine outputs of the formalism. This is the strongest defensible version of the "intrinsic arrow" claim, and it is real but narrow.

**Direction is imported.** None of the above fixes the *sign* of relaxation — they fix $|\mathrm{Im}\,\Sigma_R|$, not which way it runs. The direction is set by low-entropy data on the **past boundary**, in three interchangeable guises that reduce to **one** initial-condition assumption:

1. **the past-endpoint contour convention** — where on the closed time contour the density matrix is specified;
2. **passivity / KMS $\beta>0$ of the bath state (decisive).** By Pusz–Woronowicz (1978), a KMS state with $\beta>0$ *is* passivity *is* "no work extractable" *is* the second law as a property of the state. The sign of $\beta$ in the $\coth(\beta\omega/2)$ factor alone decides damping versus anti-damping; a legal population-inverted ($\beta<0$) KMS state **reverses the arrow**. Spectral positivity never fixes this sign.
3. **factorization $\rho_S\otimes\rho_B$** — the quantum *Stosszahlansatz*. In the Nakajima–Zwanzig construction the closed dissipative master equation is literally the $\mathcal{Q}\rho(0)=0$ *deletion*; irreversibility is generated by that choice.

These are not three independent inputs but three faces of one: a low-entropy, uncorrelated, passive past-boundary state.

## 3. The exactly-solvable demonstration

`calc/arrow_origin.py` makes this concrete on the independent-boson (pure-dephasing) model with the same super-Ohmic finite-memory bath, where the decoherence function is closed-form:

$$\Gamma(t) = \int \frac{J(\omega)}{\omega^2}\,\bigl(1-\cos\omega t\bigr)\,\coth\!\Bigl(\frac{\omega}{2T}\Bigr)\,d\omega, \qquad J(\omega)=\omega^3 e^{-(\omega/\omega_c)^2}.$$

It exhibits, exactly:

- **Time symmetry of the dynamics:** $\Gamma(t)=\Gamma(-t)$ identically (it depends on $1-\cos\omega t$, even in $t$). The equations of motion pick no direction.
- **Reversibility:** with finitely many modes the coherence decays toward zero and **returns to exactly $1$** at the Poincaré recurrence time $t_{\rm rec}=2\pi/\Delta\omega$.
- **The continuum limit as an assumption:** $t_{\rm rec}\propto N$; a monotone, irreversible decay exists *only* in the many-mode/thermodynamic limit.
- **Initial-state dependence:** the decay rate is set entirely by $\coth(\omega/2T)$ — i.e. by the assumed bath state. Different $T$ (different $\beta$), different decay.

So on a model where every step is visible, the arrow lives in (i) the factorized low-entropy initial state and (ii) the continuum limit — both assumptions about the initial condition and the bath, neither a consequence of the dynamics.

## 4. Primary-source grounding

- Feynman–Vernon (1963) assumes a factorized initial state (cf. Aurell et al., *J. Phys. A*, 2020).
- Finite baths recur (Poincaré); irreversibility requires the continuum limit (Caldeira–Leggett, *Physica A* 121, 587, 1983).
- The uncorrelated initial state is Boltzmann's *Stosszahlansatz*: the arrow **reverses for entangled initial states** (Partovi, *Phys. Rev. E* 77, 021110, 2008, "opposite to molecular chaos"); the $\delta\rho(0)=0$ step is the irreversibility-generator in Mori–Zwanzig (te Vrugt 2022, after Zeh).
- **No surveyed derivation escapes** a low-entropy/typicality input — Bogoliubov's weakening-of-correlations and ETH/typicality merely *relocate* it.
- Pusz & Woronowicz (*Commun. Math. Phys.* 58, 273, 1978): KMS $\beta>0 \equiv$ passivity.

## 5. Scope, and the closed ceiling

Asked whether the direction tracks $\mathrm{sign}(\beta)$ *alone*, the honest answer is **no**, and an over-general version of the claim must be demoted to its correct scope:

- **Within the equilibrium KMS class:** yes — detailed-balance direction is fixed by $\mathrm{sign}(\beta)$, and $\beta>0$ is the imported low-entropy input.
- **Outside KMS** (squeezed, driven-Floquet, active, non-thermal Gaussian baths): there is no single $\beta$; $N(\omega)$ and $\mathrm{Im}\,\chi$ decouple, and the dynamics relax to a *nonequilibrium steady state* whose direction is set by the full Liouvillian / stationary state / frequency-dependent rate asymmetry.

**Either way the direction is set by the assumed bath/reservoir state, never by the time-symmetric dynamics.** The "intrinsic ceiling" is therefore closed **within the cases surveyed** (equilibrium KMS and the surveyed non-equilibrium/NESS baths): no dynamics-only or single-scalar route to a direction was found — a scoped result, not a universal no-go theorem. (Anti-laundering tightening: even the "intrinsic" positivity in §2 is established relative to a passive/stable reference state, so the passive-state assumption co-supplies part of what looks dynamics-intrinsic — the import is *more* total, not less.)

## 6. What this is — and one GRUT corollary

**This is not a solution to the arrow of time.** It is a clean decomposition — *existence intrinsic, direction state-dependent* — plus the triangulation showing that three apparently different choices (contour, passivity, factorization) are one initial-condition assumption that no surveyed derivation escapes. The in-in formalism is the right *tool*: it separates intrinsic existence from imported direction and handles equilibrium and non-equilibrium baths alike. It does not make the arrow free.

The one corollary specific to the responsive-vacuum (GRUT) program: there, the equilibrium constraint is enforced as a hard admission gate (the KMS/FDT detailed-balance gate that every constitutive kernel must pass). That gate — built as a *discipline* mechanism — is *exactly* the object where the direction enters ($\beta>0$). So the program does not bury the Past Hypothesis; it makes it the visible, labeled gate every claim passes through. The discipline machinery and the imported assumption are the same gate. (This corollary uses GRUT; §§1–5 do not.)
