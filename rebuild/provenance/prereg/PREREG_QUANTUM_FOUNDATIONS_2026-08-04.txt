Immutable once the manifest records its hash; results go to a separate RESULT_ file that cites it.

THE CLUSTER QUESTION, bounded (an unbounded "node quantum foundations" is the failure shape):
    ARE OUTCOME-SELECTION AND THE PROBABILITY MEASURE ONE INPUT OR TWO?
i.e. does whatever accounts for a single definite outcome occurring also account for the Born
weights, or are those two separate underived inputs?

THE CHEAP DECISIVE TEST, run first (same move as SUSY/unimodular in the vacuum cluster, which
settled that headline in one pass):
    EINSELECTION DISSOCIATION. Environment-induced superselection derives the PREFERRED BASIS from
    the interaction Hamiltonian -- which basis is stable under monitoring -- and demonstrably does
    NOT derive which outcome occurs, nor the weights. If that dissociation holds, the basis problem
    is separable from the outcome problem, and the analysis becomes enumeration rather than
    adjudication.

PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATIONS (stated now, so a later result cannot be read back onto them):
  E1  The einselection dissociation HOLDS: einselection addresses basis selection and leaves both
      outcome-selection and the Born weights untouched. Grade expected: ATTESTED, not derived
      in-house.
  E2  Outcome-selection and the Born measure are TWO inputs, not one, under the criterion --
      because no DERIVED relation from one to the other is exhibited. Expected reading: TRADE at
      best (a posited relation), NEVER a REDUCTION. If a REDUCTION is reported here, the relation
      claimed as derived must be named and it must survive C1, or the result is void.
  E3  The DISSOLUTION class gets its first live exercise. Predicted OUTCOME: dissolution does NOT
      apply between these two. Gleason/Born-from-decision-theory arguments derive the MEASURE GIVEN
      that outcomes occur; they do not falsify the presupposition that outcomes occur. A candidate
      that removes a question rather than answering it is what dissolution is for, and this is
      probably not one. Recording the prediction because the class has never touched anything real
      and a first use that "works" is exactly when a class gets over-applied.
  E4  Expected typed inventory: predominantly POSTULATE (propositions about the world) and
      HEURISTIC, with few or no MEASURED entries. Quantum foundations is a cluster of stances, not
      of fitted numbers. A MEASURED-heavy inventory would signal a mis-declaration.
  E5  Expected cross-cluster recurrence with the vacuum cluster: `semiclassical_sourcing` (the
      source is the expectation value IN THE STATE -- which presupposes a state and a measure) and
      `eft_validity`. These were registered for the vacuum map and were NOT chosen with this
      cluster in view. If they recur, that is the first genuine cross-cluster shared input.

DIRECTIONAL GUARD: "outcome-selection and the Born measure are ONE input" is the flattering horn --
it would be a unification found by the instrument on its first unknown cluster. DEFAULT-BROKEN
TOWARD TWO. A merge requires a DERIVED relation; a posited one is a TRADE and moves nothing.
