PRE-REGISTRATION -- the authority-vocabulary audit. Written and SEALED BEFORE the audit runs.
BLIND-SAFE: yes

WHY THIS IS PRE-REGISTERED AND THE FIRST ONE WAS NOT. The 2026-08-12 "specialist" audit found 49
occurrences across 18 claims and classified them. It was still fatally narrow, and the fatal
property was NOT the regex (that was a separate, second defect). It was that THE AUDITED PARTY
CHOSE THE SEARCH WORD AFTER KNOWING WHAT IT WANTED TO FIND. One word, chosen by the person whose
work the word would indict, is not an audit -- it is a search for a reassuring answer, and it
returned one. A hostile reader greps the obvious synonym and finds an external reviewer catching
errors in the first file he opens.

THE CORRECTION: the term list is written down and hashed FIRST. Whatever it returns, it returns.

Case-insensitive, whole-word where the term is a word. Searched across the ENTIRE repository

since a reader greps the repo, not the register.

  T1  specialist / specialists          (the already-audited term; re-run for completeness)
  T2  referee / referees

  T5  overseer / overseers

                    check" -- NOT "independent variable", "independent of", "independently of")
  T7  verified by / confirmed by / corrected by / answered by / signed off
  T8  expert / experts / consulted / correspondence / colleague

RATIONALE FOR INCLUDING T5 AND T6, stated before the counts are known: the glossary defines
"overseer" as the human author of this program. Every "overseer-verified" string therefore reads
as EXTERNAL VALIDATION to a reader who has not read the glossary -- and readers do not read
glossaries first. "Independent" has the same defect: it is true of the PROCESS (a separate session,
clean context) and false of the PERSONNEL, and the document's own fixed point turns on personnel.

================================ THE CLASSIFICATION SCHEME (frozen) ==============================
Every occurrence lands in exactly one:
  A  PROSPECTIVE     -- a future outside party; asserts nobody has done it. ("reserve for a
                        specialist", "escalate to")
  B  READS-AS-EXTERNAL -- phrased so that a reader reasonably infers an outside party acted.
                        THE DANGEROUS CLASS. Includes self-attributions whose SURFACE reads
                        external ("overseer-verified", "independent specialist", "externally
                        reviewed") regardless of what the glossary says.
  C  GENERIC         -- collective/abstract, no actor implied ("three specialists conflict").
  D  MECHANICAL      -- filenames, paths, field names, code identifiers.
  E  TRUE-EXTERNAL   -- an actual outside human acted, with a logged transmission.
                        *** PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATION: E = 0. If E > 0, fixed point 2 as drafted
                        is FALSE and must be rewritten before publication, not softened. ***
