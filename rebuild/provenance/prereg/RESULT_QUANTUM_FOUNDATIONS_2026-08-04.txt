RULING: TWO INPUTS. Outcome-selection and the Born measure are two, and the default-broken position
survived on POSITIVE evidence rather than absence-of-evidence -- which is more than the
pre-registration asked for.

TWO-DIRECTIONAL NON-DETERMINATION (the ground): (a) outcome-selection does not fix the measure --
pilot-wave out of quantum equilibrium is a consistent construction with exactly one definite
outcome per run and NON-Born statistics; (b) the measure does not fix outcome-selection --
Everettian and ensemble formulations carry the full Born measure while asserting no single definite
outcome. One-directional non-determination is an argument; TWO-directional is a dissociation.

THE WEIGHTS-DERIVATION LITERATURE, met head-on rather than waved past: Gleason/Busch, envariance,
and decision-theory all have the same structure -- MEASURE-GIVEN-STRUCTURE. Each removes the
quadratic functional form as a free choice and each LEAVES a replacement posit (noncontextuality /
envariance-locality+continuity / branching indifference), plus, in every case, the separate question
of why the numbers are observed FREQUENCIES. None derives the measure FROM outcome-selection or
outcome-selection FROM the measure. That directed relation is what a merge requires; it is absent in
all three. Under this criterion each is a TRADE, never a REDUCTION.
