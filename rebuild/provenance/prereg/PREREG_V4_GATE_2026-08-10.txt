PRE-REGISTRATION -- THE GATE FOR TERMINATION-CONDITION VERSION 4. Sealed BEFORE v4 is drafted,
so the rebuild is written AGAINST these conditions rather than toward them.
BLIND-SAFE: yes

THE RULING THIS EXECUTES (overseer, 2026-08-10): not a fourth patch round on the v3
architecture (the guard became exempt from its own guard twice); not the universal-rules-only
rebuild as pitched (both deepest defects lived IN the universal layer; the disease is
complexity accretion, not channel-vs-universal). Instead: ONE short rebuild on the primitives
that survived three hostile reads, with minimal channel anchors -- the goal is not zero
specificity but ZERO UNVERIFIED SPECIFICITY -- then ONE internal adversarial pass, then the
decision. NO ROUND FIVE. Reaching outcome (C) is a first-class finding about the domain, not
a loss: if three rounds establish that a signable complete-partition stop condition is not
achievable for a program of this shape, that banks as a result.

WHAT v4 MUST BE BUILT FROM (pre-committed design constraints):
  D1. The surviving primitives: still-open first-class in every channel with its cause stated;
      thresholds frozen only against PUBLIC events (a release landing, a manifest entry) --
      never a private act such as reading; every received communication logged with its
      operative language quoted verbatim; consequences execute only at their own register
      nodes per those nodes' own text; consumption symmetric and dutiful; ONE deposit-timing
      rule.
  D2. Minimal channel anchors ONLY: source node, its actual predicate quoted verbatim, one or
      two resolved outcomes, the still-open state. No nested refinement trees, no per-channel
      timing rules, no per-channel classification machinery.
  D3. THE FORBID BRANCH of the citation fix: future event-log entries MAY NOT assert what
      register text says, structurally -- every register predicate the condition will ever
      need is pre-quoted in the sealed file itself, so no event entry has occasion to assert
      register text at all. The hole is removed, not patrolled.
  D4. Reply classification carries no reasoning machinery: a reply classifies as a resolved
      outcome ONLY if its own quoted language asserts that outcome unconditionally; anything
      less -- acknowledgment, caveat, condition, conflict between replies -- is still-open
      with the operative language quoted. The document decides nothing a quote cannot decide.

THE FOUR CASES (each must land in EXACTLY ONE home, by the document's text alone):
  K1. The C3 zero-bucket corner: activation-scale unresolved; a reach threshold frozen; the
      calc complete at register grade; the surviving window below the threshold and not
      excluded. (Fell through all five v3 buckets.)
  K2. "Received, will look": a non-substantive acknowledgment at receipt. Must have a home
      that neither classifies it as a resolved outcome nor lets it block or fire any stop
      trigger.
  K3. A register assertion attempted in a future event entry: must be structurally
      impossible under the document's rules (nothing to patrol -- the entry has no licit form
      that asserts register text).
  K4. A threshold frozen against a private act ("before the data are read"): must be
      unwritable -- the document's freeze rule admits only public events.

THE SIZE LIMIT (the burden test's proxy, stated in advance, non-negotiable): v4 in FULL --
header, rules, channels, stop, signature block, everything -- fits ONE PAGE, mechanically
defined as AT MOST 75 LINES AND AT MOST 7500 CHARACTERS. A document that busts the limit has
FAILED regardless of the four cases: the round-3 finding was that operating burden exceeded
what a single signer can execute correctly, and length is the committed proxy.

THE BURDEN TEST: a hostile reader who did not draft v4 must produce the correct deposit line
for each of the four cases PLUS at least three adversarially-invented scenarios of their own,
using only the document, in one read, with no judgment call beyond quoting causes. Any
scenario requiring the reader to re-derive physics, weigh unstated precedence, or choose
between two defensible buckets is a failure.

THE DECISION RULE (pre-committed; executing it is Part 4, re-opening it is forbidden):
  PASS = all four cases land exactly-one; size within limit; burden test passed; no new
  bespoke exception machinery was needed during drafting.
  -> v4 is sealed, manifested, and goes to the external reviewer with the gate record.
  FAIL on ANY condition -> outcome (C): the stop condition is retired with a statement; the
  program proceeds under the two named owner acts plus ordinary register discipline; the
  three-round record and this gate bank as the finding. NO ROUND FIVE, no partial credit,
  no "one more small fix" -- a v4 that needs repair after its one pass IS the (C) outcome.

THE PASS ITSELF: one internal adversarial workflow, run by agents that did not draft v4,
before any external eyes. The external reviewer sees v4 only if the internal pass is clean.
