# Vol 1 — Foundations: the responsive-vacuum ontology and the constrained primitive

## 1.1 The premise

The generative vision behind GRUT is broad — **responsiveness** (the capacity to respond, retain, and scale) as the root of structure. The premise the theory can actually *stand on* is narrower and harder: **the gravitational vacuum is a responsive medium with finite memory, governed by a single in-in action, from which known physics is recovered as limits, and which names the inputs it does not derive.**

## 1.2 The mathematical primitive — a *constraint*, not an absorber

GRUT's primitive is the in-in (Schwinger–Keldysh) influence action **S_IF**, equivalently a response map **R : S → S** on the vacuum's slow sector. **It is presented here as a constrained object, and that is the whole point.** A primitive is scientific only to the degree that it **forbids**; a primitive that "derives everything" has derived nothing — that is the unfalsifiability engine the discipline exists to prevent.

**S_IF is shipped with its axioms** (the constraints that give it teeth):

- **Locality / finite memory** — the influence functional is local with a finite memory kernel (single-pole, the one novel structural commitment; §2.2).
- **Causality** — a background Lorentzian causal structure; the kernel is *retarded* (the existence half of the arrow; §2.5).
- **KMS / FDT** — in equilibrium the noise is locked to the dissipation by detailed balance, enforced as a *hard admission gate* (no kernel that fails it enters the foundation; rung2).
- **Gaussian / linear-response** — a quadratic influence action; the leading constitutive truncation.
- **Positivity** — a positive Källén–Lehmann spectral measure and a positive-semidefinite noise kernel.

**What R cannot do (its value is its no-gos).** Because the primitive is constrained, it *generates* prohibitions — and those prohibitions are GRUT's strongest, most defensible exports (the no-go ledger, §2.4). The primitive forbids: a μ=4/3 linear-cosmology modification (excluded → ΛCDM); an economical single-mode evolving w(z); an observable parameter-free decoherence wedge; an observable GW-dissipation signature; and it **cannot** select the Einstein–Hilbert action or derive the Born measure (both borrowed). A reader should hold the primitive to this standard throughout: *what does it forbid here?* If the answer is "nothing," the statement is ontology or interpretation, not physics. **S_IF is not a flexible history-map that bends to fit any phenomenon — it is fixed by its axioms and audited by what it rules out.**
