> **past hypothesis ≠ arrow of time immediately at t = 0.**

**The O-6 question, as the owner restated it:** whether, **after the
initial slip and before recurrence**, an ensemble-level ordering emerges
robustly from the coupled conservative system:

> uncorrelated initial condition → correlation-building slip → possible
> one-way heat/entropy behavior.

> That is much sharper than asking whether "a conservative system has
> an arrow." It asks whether an arrow can emerge as a property of a
> **restricted ensemble plus system–bath partition**, despite the
> underlying full dynamics remaining conservative.
