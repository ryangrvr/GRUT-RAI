## Part I -- Executive Result

This bridge document accomplishes four things:

1. **Empirical roadmap.** Nine candidate observables are classified across four observational categories (compact-object structure, gravitational waves, strong-field lensing, cosmology). Each observable has explicit support, tension, and falsification criteria. No observable is presented as confirmed.

2. **Cross-sector integration.** The seven GRUT sectors are mapped with explicit dependency structure. The theory is best described as one architecture with multiple limits, unified through the D8 coupled action, but with the cosmology sector not yet integrated at the action level.

3. **Parameter authority.** Twenty-three major parameters and constants are classified into five authority levels: inherited (10), derived (4), matched (3), phenomenological (3), open (3). No open parameter is presented as derived.

4. **Canon hierarchy.** Sixteen theory-wide claims are classified from LOCKED to OPEN. The final-ToE status remains OPEN. Two critical gaps persist: O(3) sector derivation and empirical confrontation.

The strong-field architecture is now sufficiently stabilized to support a serious bridge phase. The theory has a defined observational roadmap, a mapped sector architecture, and an explicit parameter discipline. Some final gaps still remain before complete-ToE language is fully earned.

## Part II -- Why a Bridge Document Is Needed After D12

The D1--D12 program closed the classical strong-field vacuum/source frontier within tested scope. D11 confirmed the exact two-field coupled BVP converges and the Companion architecture survives. D12 classified Q as principled and strongly constrained.

However, the theory still cannot jump from D12 to final ToE language for four reasons:

1. **Mathematical viability is not empirical legibility.** The interior solution is well-defined but has not been confronted with any observational data. A theory without falsification criteria is not yet a physical theory in the empirical sense.

2. **Principled source ontology is not whole-theory integration.** D12 closed the Q question for the strong-field sector, but the cosmology, trigger, and observational sectors are not yet connected to the same ontological foundation at the action level.

3. **A final ToE document requires parameter discipline.** The theory has open parameters (lambda, g_p, xi) that are scanned, not predicted. A credible ToE presentation must make clear which quantities are inherited, which are derived, which are matched, and which remain open.

4. **A final ToE document requires a canon hierarchy.** Different parts of the theory have different authority levels. Presenting them uniformly would misrepresent the state of knowledge.

This bridge document addresses all four requirements.

The roadmap is organized into four categories:

### Notes on individual observables

**Quasinormal modes.** The most promising near-term target. GRUT's modified interior metric changes the effective potential for perturbations. The direction of the QNM frequency shift depends on the balance between Component A and Component B. Current LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA sensitivity may not resolve GRUT-scale corrections, but next-generation detectors (Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer) are plausible probes.

**Love numbers.** Classical black holes have exactly zero tidal Love numbers. GRUT's regular interior with non-zero energy density generically produces non-zero Love numbers. This is a clean discriminator: any measurement of non-zero Love numbers for BH-mass objects would be consistent with GRUT and inconsistent with classical BH vacuum.

**Ringdown echoes.** If the GRUT interior produces an effective cavity between R_eq and R_S, post-merger ringdown may exhibit echo signatures. The echo amplitude depends on the reflectivity of the effective potential barrier, which depends on the interior equation of state. Current upper limits on echoes are not yet constraining for GRUT-scale effects.

**Photon ring and shadow.** Space-based VLBI and next-generation EHT could probe photon ring substructure. GRUT modifications to the metric near the photon sphere produce small deformations of the critical impact parameter.

**Cosmology.** The memory-kernel cosmology sector is the least integrated with the strong-field program. The connection is structural (same kernel form) but not yet action-derived. Any cosmological prediction should be treated with lower authority than strong-field predictions.

## Part IV -- Cross-Sector Integration Map

### Sector taxonomy

The GRUT framework contains seven identifiable sectors:

1. **Gravitational** -- Einstein equations, Schwarzschild solution, curvature invariants
2. **Macro scalar memory** -- Phi(r) field driven by X(r) = M/r^2, Component A energy ~ 1/r^4
3. **Defect triplet** -- O(3) hedgehog vec_Phi^a, Component B energy ~ 1/r^2, topological charge Q
4. **Curvature trigger** -- Kretschner-coupled defect activation, xi coupling
5. **Portal interaction** -- g_p Phi^2 |vec_Phi|^2 cross-sector coupling
6. **Cosmology** -- memory-kernel modification of late-time expansion
7. **Observational** -- downstream phenomenology (QNMs, lensing, tidal)

### Minimal backbone

The minimal GRUT backbone consists of three sectors:

1. **Gravitational** -- provides the universal background (GR, Schwarzschild)
2. **Macro scalar memory** -- provides Component A and the mass function
3. **Defect triplet** -- provides Component B and the topological charge Q

The curvature trigger and portal interaction are structurally present in the D8 action but contribute negligibly on the physical test domain [R_eq, R_ext] = [1/3, 2.0] in D11. They are required for the full action but are not the dominant physical mechanism in the tested regime.

### Is this one theory or a modular assembly?

GRUT is best described as one theory with multiple limits rather than a modular assembly, for the following reasons:

1. The gravitational sector provides the universal background for all others.
2. The macro and defect sectors are unified through the D8 coupled action S_total = S_grav + S_macro + S_defect + S_trigger + S_portal.
3. The cosmology sector inherits the same memory-kernel structure that defines the macro sector.
4. The observational sector is determined by the interior solution, not independently specified.

However, two qualifications are necessary:

- The cosmology-to-strong-field integration is not yet derived from a single cosmological + interior action. The memory kernel appears in both sectors by structural analogy, not by action-level derivation.
- The trigger sector's coupling constant xi remains open. Until xi is derived or constrained, the trigger threshold is effective rather than predicted.

## Part VII -- Remaining Bridge Gaps

Eight gaps are identified, ranked by severity:

### Critical (2)

1. **O(3) sector derivation from GRUT-native structure.** The O(3) triplet is a minimal extension, not a consequence of GRUT's scalar-memory field equations. Deriving its necessity would upgrade Q from principled to derived, and simultaneously ground eta and potentially lambda.

2. **Empirical confrontation with observational data.** No GRUT prediction has been tested against data. The roadmap defines what to look for, but the theory remains empirically untested.

### Significant (4)

3. **Prediction of eta, lambda, g_p from deeper structure.** eta is matched to Phase 6C; lambda is scanned over [5, 200]; g_p is scanned over [0, 2] and found negligible in D11. Predicting any from renormalization or consistency would strengthen the theory materially.

4. **Cosmology-to-strong-field action-level integration.** The cosmology sector shares memory-kernel structure with the strong-field sector but is not yet derived from the same action.

5. **Non-Schwarzschild backgrounds.** The entire D-program is tested on Schwarzschild only. Extension to Kerr (rotating) backgrounds is needed for astrophysical realism.

6. **Linear stability of self-consistent interior.** The static interior solution has not been tested against linear perturbations. Stability is assumed but not demonstrated.

### Minor (2)

7. **Winding number selection (n=1).** n=1 is assumed as the minimal-energy hedgehog. A stability or energetic argument would close this gap.

8. **Dynamical source promotion.** X(r) = M/r^2 is frozen throughout D1-D12. A fully dynamical source would test whether the interior solution is self-consistent under source back-reaction.

1. GRUT is a unified architecture with a defined strong-field interior solution, a principled source ontology, and a falsifiability roadmap.

6. The theory defines explicit observational targets with stated support, tension, and falsification criteria.

7. The major GRUT sectors are connected through the D8 coupled action and share explicit structure.

### What Omni-ToE v3 may not claim

1. GRUT has been observationally confirmed or tested against data.

2. All parameters are predicted from first principles. eta is matched, lambda and g_p are scanned, xi is open.

3. The O(3) defect sector is derived from GRUT-native structure. It remains a minimal extension.

4. The cosmology sector is integrated with the strong-field program at the action level.

5. The interior solution has been tested on rotating (Kerr) or charged (Reissner-Nordstrom) backgrounds.

6. The static interior solution has been demonstrated to be linearly stable.

7. GRUT constitutes a complete, final theory of everything in the traditional sense. Multiple open parameters and untested sectors remain.

8. The winding number n=1 is derived rather than assumed.

### Threshold statement

Omni-ToE v3 should present GRUT as a theory whose strong-field architecture is closed within tested scope, whose source ontology is principled and strongly constrained, and whose observational predictions are explicitly defined but not yet tested. The theory is strong enough to deserve serious empirical confrontation but not yet strong enough to claim completeness.
