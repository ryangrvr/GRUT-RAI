## 4. What the Upper Stack Now Genuinely Contains

The realized upper stack contains, at bridge level:

- **Sequence-bearing heteropolymer chains:** Mixed-monomer chains with order-dependent geometry and 4^N combinatorial diversity (four-class alphabet).
- **Four-class complementarity:** D1↔A1, D2↔A2 selective pairing from bonding-site geometry; complement map is an involution (C₄(C₄(S))=S).
- **Template-directed assembly:** A pre-existing chain guides the construction of a complementary partner through sequential local pairing.
- **Duplex information objects:** Two complementary chains matched along their length; information-redundant storage.
- **Reusable templates:** Non-destructive cycling; templates persist through unlimited rounds.
- **Identity-faithful replication cycle:** Template → complement → duplex → separate → reuse → amplify; 2^n exponential growth.
- **Hereditary lineage families:** Sequence families related by descent; trackable through shared motifs; persistent for ~1/p_sub rounds.
- **Structured mutation spectrum:** Conservative (subclass D1↔D2 or A1↔A2) more common than radical (D↔A); biased, not random.
- **Sequence-dependent differential success:** Different sequences have different replication rates, duplex stabilities, and chain persistences.
- **Competitive lineage dynamics:** Shared monomer pool; faster/more persistent lineages expand at the expense of slower/weaker ones.
- **Replication-rate/stability tradeoff:** D1-A1-rich sequences (stable but slow-cycling) vs D2-A2-rich sequences (fast-cycling but fragile).

## 10. The Defining Unresolved Boundaries

The architecture's current ceiling is sharply defined. Five unresolved boundaries separate proto-selection from full biology:

**Boundary 1: Operational fidelity.** The four-class pairing provides structural identity-faithful copying. Whether the geometric mismatch penalty ΔE_mismatch is large enough relative to thermal/dissipative energy to make copying operationally faithful is uncomputed. This determines whether the high-fidelity regime (heredity works) or the low-fidelity regime (heredity fails) is physically realized.

**Boundary 2: Catalysis.** The architecture has no chain-mediated reaction acceleration. Without catalysis, there is no function-from-sequence, no enzymatic error correction, no metabolism, and no route from structural performance to biological fitness.

**Boundary 3: Function-to-fitness mapping.** Proto-selection operates on structural performance (replication rate, persistence, stability). Full Darwinian selection operates on functional fitness (catalytic activity, regulatory effectiveness, environmental adaptation). The gap between structural and functional selection is the gap between a self-copying molecule and a living cell.

**Boundary 4: Energy flow / metabolism.** The replication cycle consumes monomers from the environment but does not produce them. A self-sustaining system must convert environmental energy into the chemical work of monomer synthesis, bond formation, and cycle maintenance. No such metabolic architecture exists.

**Boundary 5: Compartmentalization.** All replicating chains compete in the same shared pool. Without compartments (cells), there is no unit of selection above the individual chain. Compartmentalization enables group-level selection, internal concentration of catalysts, and protection from parasitic sequences.

These five boundaries are not arbitrary — they are the standard prerequisites for the transition from prebiotic chemistry to biology, identified independently by origins-of-life research. The bridge architecture has reached the prebiotic-chemistry side of this transition. It has not crossed it.

**Fidelity Bounds Audit.** The single most consequential unresolved parameter in the architecture is the geometric mismatch penalty ΔE_mismatch. If ΔE_mismatch ≫ kT, the high-fidelity regime is realized and heredity works. If ΔE_mismatch ~ kT, the low-fidelity regime holds and the architecture remains pre-hereditary despite its structural potential. Bounding this parameter — even qualitatively — would determine which side of the fidelity boundary the architecture occupies and whether the upper-stack thresholds are operationally realized or only structurally available.

In parallel, a **Catalysis Preconditions Audit** should begin, because catalysis is the key to crossing every remaining biological boundary (error correction, metabolism, function-to-fitness mapping). These two audits can proceed independently and would together define whether the architecture can support the transition from prebiotic chemistry to proto-biology.
