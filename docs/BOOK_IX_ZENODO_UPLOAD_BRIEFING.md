## Critical Narrative Arc

Book IX has a distinctive epistemic-deepening structure — not a new-mechanism story but a resolution-of-conditionality story:

**Prologue (W0 — The Foundation):** Program W0, running in parallel with Book VIII, attacked the carrier barrier from below. It identified the loaded carrier state as the (N=2, ℓ=0, S=0) first excited s-wave state of the K=2 gauge-singlet composite — a quantum-mechanically metastable state protected against single-gauge-boson decay by the exact dipole selection rule (Δℓ = ±1 forbids ℓ=0 → ℓ=0 E1 transitions). W0 derived the inequality ΔE₁₂ = (3/16)α_g²M_sk ≥ 28 kT whenever α_g²(M_sk/kT) ≥ 149, showing the robust carrier regime is the generic expectation for thermally stable scaffolds with moderate gauge coupling. W0 reduced carrier debt from "full" to "reduced" — but left two load-bearing items open: the two-gauge-boson decay rate and the dissipation-coupling assessment.

**Act 1 (Alpha — Completing the Job):** Book IX Alpha resolves both open items:

*Open Item 1 — Two-gauge-boson decay:* The (N=2, ℓ=0) state decays to the ground state via two-gauge-boson emission at a rate scaling as Γ(2γ) ∝ α_g⁸ × M_sk. This rate is strongly suppressed in the weak-coupling regime (α_g ≲ 0.02) where the carrier lifetime exceeds the diffusion time by a comfortable margin. The steep α_g⁸ dependence means the suppression improves dramatically as coupling weakens. In the moderate-coupling regime (α_g ≳ 0.05), the two-boson rate is too fast and the carrier fails. The constraint (α_g ≲ 0.02) is tighter than the general weak-coupling requirement for hydrogenic binding (α_g ≲ 0.1–0.3) but lies WITHIN the same weak-coupling regime the scaffold was already designed for.

*Open Item 2 — Dissipation coupling:* The GRUT native dissipation (τ dΦ/dt + Φ = X) couples to the composite's internal dynamics only perturbatively under the scale-separation assumption (ω_composite ≫ γ). This assumption was already required by the matter and gauge bridges since Book IV Alpha ("ω_sk ≫ γ, assumed not derived"). The dissipation-induced decay rate is suppressed by (γ/ω)^n × exp(−ΔE/kT). No new constraint is added.

**Denouement (The Qualitative Shift):** The conditionality undergoes a qualitative transformation:

| Before Book IX | After Book IX |
|---------------|-------------|
| M4 requires ΔG ≥ 28 kT (externally matched parameter) | M4 requires weak coupling + scale separation (structural scaffold assumptions) |
| Barrier was POSTULATED | Barrier is DERIVED from binding energy |

| Dissipation was UNASSESSED | Dissipation is CONFIRMED perturbative |

The M4/D4/L4/A4 cascade is promoted from "conditional" to "stabilized" — functioning under the scaffold's own operating assumptions rather than an externally matched parameter. The carrier debt is upgraded from "reduced" to "strongly reduced." The total cost is unchanged at 15/9/1/6.

This arc — W0 foundation → two-boson containment → dissipation assessment → qualitative conditionality shift → stabilized cascade — is the structural spine of the manuscript.
