## What This Document Is

Single-stage feasibility audit testing five candidate D(r) functional forms against the LVK population integral constraint that killed universal D (Part 2 Zeta). Result: no viable form found.

## Narrative

Five candidates tested:
1. **Gravitational suppression** D ~ f(r) = (1 - r_s/r): suppression factor 2/3 at ISCO. 23 orders short. FAILS.
2. **Exponential cutoff** D ~ exp(-(r_s/r)^n): pure parameter tuning. AD HOC.
3. **FDT-consistent** D ~ tau_local/tau_0: per-source signal reduced ~500x. Still 17 orders above O4a. FAILS.
4. **Curvature-proportional** D ~ K^alpha: peaks near horizon = WRONG DIRECTION. FAILS.
5. **Far-field only** D ~ (1-exp(-r/r_noise)): evades bound but introduces unmotivated scale. AD HOC.

**Structural diagnosis:** The Level-1 tau reduction concentrates constitutive dynamics near compact objects. Any noise coupled to these dynamics radiates maximally from the same near-horizon region. The universe contains ~10^19 such objects. The population sum overwhelms any reasonable D(r).
