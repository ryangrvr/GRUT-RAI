## Critical Narrative Arc

Book VII has a distinctive three-act structure with a dramatic turning point:

**Act 1 (Alpha — The Last Free Lunch):** The scaffold's HIC network can be expanded at zero cost. Two new HIC pairings — P3 (boundary growth) and P4 (catalyst repair) — plus parallel multiplication and cascading amplification push the directed fraction from ~5–10% to ~15–25%. But then: the concerted-mode substrate-competition ceiling appears at ~25–35%. The zero-cost road runs out. After 23 consecutive zero-cost targets spanning chemistry-entry through M3 expanded metabolism, the scaffold hits a structural wall.

**Act 2 (Beta — The Fourth Bridge):** A new bridge must be designed. The minimum carrier is identified: a K=2-scale composite loaded by HIC discharge, diffusing freely through the proto-cell interior, discharging at compatible remote targets. This is the fourth bridge in the program — and the lightest (1 postulate + 2 parameters, 0 new fields). It breaks the fixed-site locality constraint that capped the HIC network. Source and target are spatially decoupled. No more concerted-mode bottleneck.

**Act 3 (Gamma — The Conditional Crossing):** The carrier is stress-tested. A three-regime structure emerges: weak (carrier useless), marginal (straddles the threshold), and robust (carrier works beautifully). In the robust regime (ΔG_barrier ≥ 28 kT), verified directed fraction reaches ~30–34% — the first time directed energy flow potentially dominates over ambient-thermal for key processes. But the crossing is conditional: the barrier height is physically plausible but not derived from first principles. The carrier is provisionally committed.

**Denouement:** Book VII closes with the first organizational inversion in the scaffold's history — but it's conditional. The scaffold has two coexisting energetic states: M3 (unconditional, ~15–25%) and M4-conditional (~30–34%). The D/L/A levels from Book VI similarly split: unconditional (D3/L3/A3) and conditional-under-M4 (D4/L4/A4 approaching). The next move is to exploit this conditional dominance for downstream domain upgrades — the same productive pattern that made Book VI the most efficient phase in the program.

This arc — last free lunch → ceiling → fourth bridge → conditional crossing → dual-state scaffold — is the structural spine of the manuscript.
