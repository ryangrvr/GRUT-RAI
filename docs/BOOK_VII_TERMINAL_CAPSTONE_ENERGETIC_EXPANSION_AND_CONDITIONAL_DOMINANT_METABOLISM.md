**Book VIII handoff:** Downstream domain reassessment under M4. This is analogous to how Book VI exploited the Book V HIC to upgrade D/L/A at zero cost.

### 3.2 Beta: Carrier Bridge Architecture (New Bridge Debt)

**What was earned:**
- Family J (HIC-to-carrier hybrid) identified as minimum viable carrier bridge
- K=2-scale composite with conformational loaded/unloaded switch
- Produced at HIC discharge sites; diffuses freely internally; discharges at compatible remote targets
- Source-target spatial decoupling: eliminates the concerted-mode saturation constraint

**What was NOT earned:** M4 verification. Beta designed the bridge but did not verify the threshold.

**Cost:** 1 postulate (carrier functional class) + 2 parameters (E_carrier energy content, τ_carrier lifetime). Fourth bridge debt.

### 3.3 Gamma: M4 Threshold Verification (Conditional)

**What was earned:**
- Three-regime structure: weak (< 23 kT), marginal (23–28 kT), robust (≥ 28 kT)
- In robust regime: η_carrier > 0.95; verified directed fraction ~30–34%
- M4 dominant metabolism conditionally verified
- Multi-domain consequence: D4-conditional, L4-approaches, A4-conditional
- Organizational inversion for key processes (directed > ambient for replication + fidelity)
- Carrier provisionally committed

**What was NOT earned:** Unconditional M4. The ΔG_barrier ≥ 28 kT condition is physically plausible but not derived from first principles.

**Cost:** Zero (analytical verification of Beta's bridge).

### 3.4 How the Stages Connect

Alpha → Beta → Gamma forms a single progressive arc:

```
Alpha: Zero-cost expansion → M3 (~15–25%) → hits ceiling
Beta: Carrier bridge design → spatial decoupling → M4 projected
Gamma: Carrier stress test → three-regime structure → M4 conditional
```

The transition from zero-cost to bridge-debt occurs at Beta. Alpha is the last zero-cost energetic target. Beta and Gamma are analytical stages (Beta designs, Gamma verifies) that cost 1+2 in bridge debt but produce M4-conditional in return.
