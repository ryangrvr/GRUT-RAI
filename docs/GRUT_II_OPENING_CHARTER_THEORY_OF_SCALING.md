## 2. What GRUT-II Asks

GRUT-I treated tau as a constant (or locally reduced via Level-1). It treated X as externally given. It treated the equation as operating at ONE SCALE — the constitutive relaxation scale.

GRUT-II asks: **what happens when the constitutive dynamics operate across MULTIPLE scales simultaneously?**

The core observation: physical reality spans 60+ orders of magnitude in scale (Planck length to Hubble radius). The constitutive equation tau dPhi/dt + Phi = X has a natural SCALE PARAMETER: tau. The Level-1 rule (1/tau_local = 1/tau_0 + 1/t_dyn) already showed that tau varies with the gravitational environment. But GRUT-I treated this as a background property, not as dynamics.

GRUT-II asks: **is the scale variation itself a dynamical degree of freedom?**

## 3. The Central Hypothesis

In GRUT-I, tau is a PARAMETER that varies according to a rule.
In GRUT-II, tau is a FIELD that evolves according to an equation.

The GRUT-II hypothesis: the constitutive relaxation timescale tau(x, t) is itself governed by constitutive dynamics — it relaxes, it responds to sources, and its spatial and temporal variation produces emergent structure.

This is NOT the same as:
- Adding noise (GRUT-I Part 2: falsified)
- Making D position-dependent (GRUT-I Part 3: no viable form)
- Self-consistent multiplicity (GRUT-I Part 4: artifact)

It IS: promoting tau from parameter to field. A dynamical timescale.

## 4. What This Could Produce

If tau is a dynamical field:

1. **Scale-dependent constitutive response** — different regions of spacetime relax at different rates, determined by the local tau field, not just by the background gravity
2. **Scaling transitions** — as tau crosses threshold values, qualitatively different physics emerges (analogous to phase transitions in condensed matter)
3. **Emergent hierarchy** — the vast range of physical scales (Planck to Hubble) could reflect the tau field's spatial variation rather than being put in by hand
4. **Coupling between scales** — the tau field mediates interactions between physics at different scales, not through forces but through RELAXATION RATE MATCHING
