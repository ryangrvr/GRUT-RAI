## 2. Structural Closure Statement

### (a) Technical closure

Book B establishes the formal scaffold for GRUT's irreversible update architecture in the linear, Markovian, weak-curvature regime. The minimal Markov state is (Φ_t, X_t, F_t), where Φ is the constitutive field, X = β + αR is the curvature-determined equilibrium target, and F is a four-bit regime-validity register. The update operator U is a first-order dissipative map with exponential contraction (eigenvalue −1/τ), stochastic extension via FDT-consistent Gaussian noise (D = k_BT τ/2), and causal directionality. The admissibility functional A classifies paths against seven conditions but does not constrain the dynamics: in this regime, every path generated by U within the flagged domain is automatically admissible, and Model D (diagnostic) is proven identical to Model C (constraining). The residue V_t = (Φ − X)² is a diagnostic observable (not a state variable) that serves as the Lyapunov function in the deterministic/constant-X case. No trajectory-level global monotonic functional exists under general admissible conditions. The derivation linkage is hybrid: the constitutive law and noise structure are derived from the CTP effective action (Iota-Prime); the source coupling X = β + αR, the regime boundaries, and the Markovian closure are appended inputs. Five EFT parameters (τ, D, T, α, β) remain undetermined. Book B does not resolve any of the six unresolved dependencies (UD1-UD6) inherited from Book A.

### (b) Public-facing closure

Book B of the GRUT-III program defines the formal architecture for irreversible constitutive dynamics: a state, an update rule, a residue diagnostic, and an admissibility classifier. The system evolves by a first-order relaxation equation derived from a quantum field-theoretic action principle, with curvature-dependent equilibrium and thermal noise. Within the controlled domain — weak gravity, slow driving, linear response — the architecture is internally consistent, mathematically proven to be contractive and causal, and free of ghosts or instabilities. The admissibility functional classifies trajectories but does not prune them: every trajectory the update rule generates is automatically admissible in this regime. This is a scaffold, not a final theory. It does not predict its own parameters, does not operate at strong curvature, and does not claim status as a theory of everything. It is the minimal irreversible effective field theory consistent with the constitutive structure identified in GRUT-I and the influence-functional derivation completed in GRUT-II.

## 6. ToE Trajectory Checkpoint

### What was strengthened for ToE credibility

1. **The constitutive law has a variational derivation.** Before GRUT-II Theta-Prime, it was postulated. Now it is the classical EOM of a CTP effective action. This is a real structural upgrade: the law has a Lagrangian ancestor, not just phenomenological status.

2. **The USL has a mechanism.** Before Iota-Prime, it was a scaling law. Now it is identified as tree-level gravitational self-energy dephasing in the influence functional — the same formalism that generates the constitutive law. Both are outputs of one action.

3. **The three-sector structure is clean.** Dissipation, noise, and dephasing live in structurally distinct sectors of the CTP action. Their independence (and the conditions under which it breaks) is derived, not assumed.

4. **The extended-body correction is computed.** The regime of validity for the USL is now known exactly, and the catastrophic failure of the point-mass formula at l << R has been identified, corrected, and frozen.

5. **The domain of validity is explicit.** Six axes, three zones each, with a blacklist of ten forbidden extrapolations. This is more rigorous than most EFT programs at comparable maturity.

### What remains missing for ToE viability

1. **No parameter prediction.** Five parameters (τ, D, T, α, β) are inputs. A ToE predicts its own parameters or derives them from fewer inputs. GRUT does neither.

2. **No strong-field regime.** The backbone is Newtonian. A ToE must work at all curvatures. The strong-field regime is UNSAFE/EXCLUDED.

3. **No covariance.** The action is not covariant. A ToE must be generally covariant (or explain why not).

4. **No UV completion.** The EFT has no UV cutoff from the theory itself (the body radius R provides the physical cutoff). A ToE must either be UV-complete or embed in a UV-complete theory.

5. **No dynamical admissibility.** The admissibility functional is diagnostic, not generative. A ToE that claims to explain "why these trajectories and not others" needs constraining dynamics — which GRUT does not have in this regime.

6. **No experimental confirmation.** The USL prediction is testable in principle but hardware-limited (5-15 years). No GRUT prediction has been tested.

7. **No unification of τ and the USL.** They come from different sectors (environmental bath vs gravitational self-energy) with no known connection. A ToE would unify them.

**Forensic summary:** GRUT has upgraded from "postulated equations" to "CTP-derived EFT with a bounded domain." This is genuine progress. It is not ToE-level. The gap is: parameter determination, strong-field extension, covariance, UV completion, constraining dynamics, and experimental test. Each is a separate multi-stage problem.
