At frequencies ω ≳ 1/τ (if τ is short enough), detectable modifications to GW phase/amplitude could appear. This is a specific mechanism (τ-dissipation) in a specific regime (high-frequency GW) with a specific observable (modified GW phase). GR does not contain this dissipation channel.
