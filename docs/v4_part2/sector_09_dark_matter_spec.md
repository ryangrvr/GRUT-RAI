# Sector 9 — Dark Matter: Specification
## Sector name
Dark Matter
## Status
**Open, with exploratory search framework**
## Claim boundary
**What is claimed:** GRUT's constitutive framework may admit hidden-sector or weakly-coupled response excitations as dark-matter candidates. Candidate viability criteria (stability, coupling suppression, relic-compatible phenomenology) are defined as a search framework.
**What is NOT claimed:** No identified GRUT dark-matter candidate. No relic-density calculation. No halo-structure fit. No direct/indirect detection prediction.
## Dependencies
- Sector 2 (electroweak host), Sector 6 (QCD/color), Sector 5 (cosmology infrastructure)
## Closure condition
Closure requires: identification of a specific stable, weakly-coupled candidate; relic abundance calculation; consistency with direct/indirect detection bounds; halo-structure compatibility.
