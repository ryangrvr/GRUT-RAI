# Sector 12 — Quantum Gravity: Specification
## Sector name
Quantum Gravity
## Status
**Open**
## Claim boundary
**What is claimed:** The exact interface between GRUT and quantum gravity is documented. The missing ingredients are enumerated. The CTP structure provides a formal connection point.
**What is NOT claimed:** No graviton. No quantized metric. No backreaction closure. No UV completion. No black-hole information resolution. No emergent spacetime.
## Dependencies
- Sector 4 (gravity), Sector 3 (gravitational decoherence), A0 (CTP)
## Closure condition
Closure requires: a quantized gravitational sector (graviton or equivalent); UV completion; consistent backreaction; black-hole information closure. This is the deepest open gate in the program.
