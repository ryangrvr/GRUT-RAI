# Sector 11 — Coupling Unification: Specification
## Sector name
Coupling Unification
## Status
**Open, with RG diagnostic entry point**
## Claim boundary
**What is claimed:** Running-coupling diagnostics are provided for the three SM gauge couplings. Convergence metrics are defined. The diagnostic framework is inherited from Sectors 2 and 6.
**What is NOT claimed:** No unification result. No GUT-scale prediction. No threshold correction. No predictive coupling relation.
## Dependencies
- Sector 2 (electroweak), Sector 6 (QCD)
## Closure condition
Closure requires: demonstration that couplings converge at a definite scale; threshold corrections; predictive relations between low-energy couplings.
