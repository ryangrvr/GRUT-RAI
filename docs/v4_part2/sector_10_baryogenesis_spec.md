# Sector 10 — Baryogenesis: Specification
## Sector name
Baryogenesis
## Status
**Open, with nonequilibrium entry point**
## Claim boundary
**What is claimed:** GRUT's CTP/nonequilibrium structure is naturally suited to baryogenesis calculations. The Sakharov conditions are framed as a diagnostic checklist. A toy asymmetry-evolution scaffold is provided.
**What is NOT claimed:** No baryogenesis mechanism. No baryon asymmetry fit. No CP-violation derivation. No electroweak/leptogenesis closure.
## Dependencies
- A0 (CTP doubling), Sector 2 (electroweak), Sector 5 (cosmology)
## Closure condition
Closure requires: a concrete CP-violating mechanism within the constitutive framework; a freezeout/sphaleron history calculation; a quantitative baryon-to-photon ratio matching eta_B ~ 6e-10.
