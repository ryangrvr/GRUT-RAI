# Sector 7 — Flavor / Masses: Mathematical Scaffold

## 1. Flavor sector as inherited from Sector 2

Fermion masses are generated by the Higgs mechanism:

    M_f = y_f v / sqrt(2)

where y_f is the Yukawa coupling (FREE) and v = 246 GeV is the Higgs VEV. The mass matrix in each charge sector (up-quarks, down-quarks, charged leptons) is a 3x3 complex matrix diagonalized by bi-unitary transformation:

    M_diag = U_L^dag M_Yukawa U_R

## 3. CKM mixing

The CKM matrix V_CKM = U_L^{up,dag} U_L^{down} relates up- and down-type quark mass eigenstates. Parameterized by 3 angles + 1 CP phase:

    |V_us| ~ 0.225 (Cabibbo angle)
    |V_cb| ~ 0.041
    |V_ub| ~ 0.004

Unitarity: V^dag V = I (exact). Status: diagnostic utility implemented.
