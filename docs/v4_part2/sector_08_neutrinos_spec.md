# Sector 8 — Neutrinos: Specification

## Sector name
Neutrinos

## Status
**Open, with exploratory entry point**

## Claim boundary

**What is claimed:**
- Neutrinos fit within the electroweak host structure (Sector 2)
- Dirac and Majorana mass entry points are documented
- PMNS mixing-matrix diagnostic utilities are provided
- Oscillation observables (mass-squared differences, mixing angles) are scaffolded

**What is NOT claimed:**
- No derived neutrino mass-generation mechanism
- No derived PMNS matrix
- No resolution of Dirac vs Majorana question
- No mass-ordering (normal vs inverted hierarchy) prediction
- No CP-phase prediction

## Dependencies
- **Sector 2:** Electroweak host
- **Sector 7:** Generic flavor/mass structure

## Closure condition
Closure would require deriving the neutrino mass mechanism (Dirac, Majorana, or novel), the PMNS mixing angles, mass ordering, and CP phase from the GRUT constitutive structure.
