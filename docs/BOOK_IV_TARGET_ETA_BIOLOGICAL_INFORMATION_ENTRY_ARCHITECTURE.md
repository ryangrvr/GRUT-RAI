## 1. Executive Overview

The Biological Information Entry Architecture is the first upper-stack architecture document in Book IV. It consolidates eighteen prior audits spanning five target branches into a single reference platform that reaches from topological soliton matter through gauge-mediated force through chemistry-entry through reaction grammar through polymer-like chains to sequence-bearing material structures.

The architecture has crossed the biological-information-entry threshold at bridge level. It supports: persistent material chains composed of multiple distinct monomer-like composite types, in which the sequential ordering of monomers is a physical property that determines the chain's geometry, bonding pattern, and structural behavior. The combinatorial diversity of sequences scales as k^N (exponentially in chain length), providing the material substrate for information storage without limit in principle.

Biological information entry means that the structural prerequisites for information-bearing material organization are in place. It does not mean that coding, replication, heredity, metabolism, cellular organization, or life have been derived. The architecture provides a material information substrate — a class of physical objects that can carry, preserve, and distinguish sequential ordering — not an information-processing system. The gap between substrate and system is the entire domain of molecular biology, and it remains uncrossed.

This document serves two functions: it freezes the pre-biological material platform as a bounded reference architecture, and it defines the handoff to later upper-stack programs that would need to construct coding, replication, and eventually observer-relevant information organization on top of this substrate.

No new postulates, parameters, fields, or degrees of freedom are added. The biological-information-entry threshold is crossed as a free consequence of the lower-stack investments, exactly as chemistry-entry was.

**Classification:** Bridge-level upper-stack entry. Information substrate established. Biological function absent. Handoff defined.

## 5. Material Information Grammar

### Table 3 — Material Information Grammar

| Feature | Meaning | Present? | Caveat |
|---------|---------|----------|--------|
| **Monomer alphabet** | Set of distinct unit types available for chain construction | **YES** | At least 3–5 types (K=3, 5, 6, 7, 8) with distinct bonding characters |
| **Chain length** | Number of monomers in a sequence | **YES** | Limited only by stability and scale-separation condition |
| **Sequence order** | Specific arrangement of monomers along the chain | **YES** | Order is physical: different sequences → different geometries |
| **Branching grammar** | Rules for where branch points can occur | **YES** | K=7 (trivalent) creates branch points; K=6 (divalent) extends linearly |
| **Termination grammar** | Rules for how chains end | **YES** | K=3, K=5 (monovalent) terminate chains; closed-shell composites do not bond |
| **Geometric encoding** | Sequence information readable through spatial structure | **YES** | Branch-point spacing, chain angles, and topology all sequence-dependent |
| **Stability persistence** | Sequence preserved as long as bonds persist | **CONDITIONAL** | Requires bond energy > thermal/dissipative scale |
| **Combinatorial capacity** | Number of distinct possible sequences | **YES** | k^N for alphabet size k and chain length N; exponential |
| **Distinguishability** | Different sequences are in principle physically distinguishable | **YES** | Different geometries, bonding sites, and branch patterns |

### 5.1 The Information Is Material

The information grammar defined above is not abstract. Every feature has a physical realization:

- **Alphabet:** Different K-value composites with distinct bonding properties (linker, brancher, terminator).
- **Sequence:** The order in which these composites are bonded along a chain backbone.
- **Readability:** The spatial geometry of the chain encodes the sequence — a reader could in principle reconstruct the sequence from the chain's shape.
- **Writability:** A chain is assembled monomer by monomer; the assembly process determines the sequence.
- **Persistence:** The sequence is stored in the covalent-like bond pattern, which is stable as long as the bonds persist.

This is material information in the most literal sense: physically embodied, spatially extended, structurally consequential, and combinatorially diverse.

## 6. Sequence-Bearing Object Taxonomy

### Table 4 — Sequence-Bearing Object Taxonomy

| Object type | Defining feature | Example | Status |
|------------|-----------------|---------|--------|
| **Linear homopolymer** | Single monomer type; repeated; no branches | K6–K6–K6–K6 chain | Simplest polymer; no sequence information (all units identical) |
| **Linear heteropolymer** | Mixed monomers; no branches; sequence matters | K6–K7–K6–K8–K6 chain | First information-bearing object; sequence encodes branch-point and bonding-site pattern |
| **Branched heteropolymer** | Mixed monomers; branch points at trivalent nodes | Chain with K7 nodes connecting multiple linear segments | Network-like; richer information content; 2D/3D structure |
| **Terminated chain** | Chain with monovalent end caps | K3–K6–K6–K7–K6–K5 | Finite, well-defined object with start and end |
| **Ring / cyclic chain** | Chain closing on itself with no free ends | K6–K6–K6–K6 (cyclic) | Topologically distinct from linear; no termini |
| **Inert closed-shell composite** | Not part of the information-bearing set | K=2, K=10 (noble-like) | Do not bond; do not participate in chains |

### 6.1 The Hierarchy

The simplest information-bearing object is the terminated linear heteropolymer: a finite chain with distinct end caps and a mixed-monomer sequence along its backbone. Its information content is the sequence itself — the ordered list of monomer types from one end to the other.

More complex objects (branched heteropolymers, cyclic chains, multi-chain assemblies) carry richer information — topology, branching pattern, and three-dimensional spatial arrangement — on top of the linear sequence. These higher-order information modes require the trivalent K=7 composite as a structural enabler.
