## Critical Narrative Arc

Book X has a three-act structure that exactly parallels the bridge-building pattern established in Books V and VII:

**Act 1 (Alpha — The Boundary Wall):** The scaffold has stabilized internal energetics (M4-stabilized) but zero boundary-crossing work capability. Five zero-cost transport route families are tested: carrier-biased pore transit, boundary-gated conformation switching, repair-coupled retention/release, directed incorporation with transport consequence, and pseudo-transport. ALL FIVE FAIL. The root cause is structural: internal energetics terminate at internal scaffold discharge pockets; the boundary mesh is passive K=6/K=7 cross-linking with structural pores; no mechanism connects carrier/HIC discharge to boundary-state switching or trans-boundary material movement. This is the third time the program hits this pattern — zero-cost routes exhausted, structural gap localized, new bridge required (previously: Book V coupling gap → HIC; Book VII M4 ceiling → carrier).

**Act 2 (Beta — The Fifth Bridge):** The minimum boundary-crossing bridge is designed. Four candidate families evaluated: Family F (Carrier-Coupled Boundary Gate / CCBG), Family G (carrier-driven shuttle/importer), Family H (boundary work cycle, subsumed into G), and Family I (overbuilt pseudo-pump, disqualified). Family F survives as the minimum viable fifth bridge: a boundary-embedded scaffold whose backbone conformation controls local pore accessibility, switched by carrier discharge. Cost: 1 postulate + 1 parameter for T2 gated permeability, +1 parameter for T3 directional binding-pocket extension. The formal cycle is defined: carrier docks at gate pocket → discharges → backbone flips (closed→open) → species transits through open pore → gate resets spontaneously → cycle repeats. Family G is reserved for future T4 needs but is not required.

**Act 3 (Gamma — Verification and Commitment):** The CCBG is stress-tested. T2 is robustly verified: carrier-coupled pore switching provides controlled exchange timing and — the signature result — waste export of large degradation products that are permanently trapped at T1 (too big for passive pores, small enough for open gated pores). This is a genuinely new capability absent from the entire prior scaffold. T3 is conditionally verified for large species near the pore-size threshold, where gate-directed import exceeds passive inward flux. For small species, T3 fails (passive bidirectional flux overwhelms gate-directed import). The CCBG is provisionally committed as the fifth bridge at 1P + 2p.

**Denouement (Terminal — The Sector Transition):** Book X closes the biology-side scaffold at a natural stopping point. Five bridges installed. Stabilized M4/D4/L4/A4 organization. T2 robust + T3-conditional transport. Cost: 16/11/1/6. The remaining biology-side boundaries (broad T3, T4, full metabolic regulation, innovation, ecology, life) each require substantial new bridge investment with diminishing structural leverage. The highest-leverage next sector is the gravitational/cosmological program — where the GRUT native equation (τ dΦ/dt + Φ = X) faces precision observational data (Hulse-Taylor binary pulsar, gravitational wave detection, cosmological expansion) for the first time.

This arc — boundary wall → fifth bridge → conditional crossing → biology-side freeze → sector transition — is the structural spine of the manuscript.
