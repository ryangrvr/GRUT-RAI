The scaffold reaches the prebiotic-chemistry side of the origin-of-life transition. Five biological boundaries remain:

### 11.1 Full Material-Cycle Closure

The proto-cell imports individual solitons from the environment. It does not create matter. Full metabolic autonomy would require either environmental self-sufficiency (processing all needed materials from available precursors) or a fundamental matter-energy conversion mechanism beyond the current architecture.

### 11.2 Energy-Flow Organization

No energy currency (ATP-like molecule) exists. No mechanism couples energetically favorable reactions to drive unfavorable ones. Reactions proceed on ambient thermal energy only. Bioenergetic coupling is absent.

### 11.3 Homeostasis

Internal conditions (monomer concentration, template/catalyst ratio, production rates) fluctuate with external supply and are not self-regulated. No feedback loops, no sensor/actuator chains, no regulatory architecture.

### 11.4 Regulated Growth and Division

Division is mechanical (pressure-driven fission), not regulated (no internal timing or content-distribution control). Daughter quality is variable and statistically determined. No cell-cycle analogue exists.

### 11.5 True Darwinian Adaptation

Proto-selection operates on structural and catalytic performance. Full Darwinian evolution requires functional fitness landscapes, open-ended adaptation, and ecological dynamics — none of which is present.
