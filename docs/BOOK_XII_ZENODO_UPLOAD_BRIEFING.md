## Critical Narrative Arc

Book XII has a distinctive three-act structure where each act TESTS a claim and NARROWS the frontier:

**Act 1 (Alpha — The Cosmological Reckoning):** Gate 1 tests whether T^Φ_μν coupled to FRW Einstein equations produces viable cosmological dynamics with a real GRUT-native surplus. The result is devastating for the original claim but illuminating for the program: the "native dark-energy replacement" claim COLLAPSES. The equilibrium T^Φ has negative energy density (ρ_eq = −X²/(2τ²) < 0) with w = −1 — this is anti-accelerating, not accelerating. The static Yukawa screening (Appendix W-F) does not translate to FRW as dark-energy replacement. However, a different, genuine surplus emerges: the GRUT dynamical cosmological regulator — a three-regime expansion-history modification controlled by H·τ (fast expansion: ρ_Φ > 0; transition at H·τ ~ 1; slow expansion: ρ_Φ < 0). This is structurally GRUT-native and distinct from GR + Λ. Gate 1 survives conditionally with a revised surplus.

**Act 2 (Beta — The GW Disappointment):** Gate 2 tests whether the GGB produces a beyond-GR GW-sector surplus and constrains τ. The result is a clean failure: the tensor sector IS standard GR (from installed Einstein-Hilbert). The scalar admixture exists formally but is perturbatively small (α_mix ≪ 1) and observationally invisible. τ is not constrained by GW data because the mixing amplitude depends on both τ AND X (the GRUT source value) — a τ-X degeneracy that prevents individual τ determination. Critically, Gate 2 was supposed to constrain τ enough to sharpen the Gate 1 cosmological regulator (by predicting the transition epoch H ~ 1/τ). Since τ is unconstrained, this leverage does not materialize. Gate 1 is not sharpened. Gate 2 fails as an independent surplus.

**Act 3 (Gamma — The Consistency Rescue):** Gate 3 tests whether binary-pulsar timing is spoiled by τ. The result is reassuring: τ ~ 10⁻⁵ s (the compact-interior scale) satisfies the binary-pulsar constraint by NINE orders of magnitude (τ/P ~ 10⁻¹⁰). The constraint is weak (any τ < 60 s works). Crucially, this τ simultaneously preserves the demonstrated singularity-resolution surplus (which operates at exactly this scale) and confines the cosmological regulator to the early universe (transition at H ~ 1/τ ~ 10⁵ s⁻¹, corresponding to T ~ 10¹² K — the QCD-transition era). The frontier is NOT trivialized. Gate 3 survives.

**Denouement (Terminal — The Renewed Decision):** With all three gates tested, the surplus portfolio is frozen: 1 demonstrated (singularity resolution), 1 conditional/narrowed (early-universe regulator at T ~ 10¹² K), 0 GW surplus. The renewed commitment decision: Option B (retain two-tier stance, refined). The portfolio (1+1+0) does not justify the cost of a full metric-sector bridge (+1P +1p +1F +2DOF). The singularity-resolution result can be studied within the existing GR + T^Φ framework (Phase 4 xAct) without formal commitment. The frontier is active (demonstrated result warrants continued exploration) but uncommitted.

This arc — cosmological claim killed → GW surplus fails → τ consistency rescues the core → narrowed but honest frontier → commitment deferred — is the structural spine of the manuscript.
