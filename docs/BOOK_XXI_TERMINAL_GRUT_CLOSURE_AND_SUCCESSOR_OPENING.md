## 2. What GRUT Cannot Do

GRUT cannot derive probability from its constitutive grammar. The obstruction is structural:

- **Unique attractor:** All initial conditions converge to one point. No multiplicity.
- **No branching:** Monotone contraction destroys the splitting needed for probability.
- **No measure:** The invariant measure on the attractor is a delta function. Trivial.
- **No chaos:** The coupled nonlinear system (including CTP doubling) produces runaway, not bounded strange attractors.
- **No SRB:** Without bounded chaos, no physical probability measure emerges from dynamics.

This is not a gap that further analysis will close. It is a consequence of what the equation IS: a linear, first-order, scalar, deterministic, contractive ODE.
