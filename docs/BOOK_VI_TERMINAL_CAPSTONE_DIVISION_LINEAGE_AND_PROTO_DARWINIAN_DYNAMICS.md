## 1. Executive Verdict

**Global verdict: (A) — Book VI closes with meaningful but bounded state advance; Book VII handoff is determined.**

Book VI earned three genuine structural advances beyond Book V, all at zero cost:

1. **Supplementary regulated division (D3)** (Alpha): A connected A+C+D control package — content-load-responsive timing (A), P2-quality-dependent timing bias (C), spatial-correlation partition bias (D) — reduces nonviable-daughter production from ~10–30% to ~3–8%.

2. **Supplementary lineage robustness (L3)** (Beta): A five-route connected robustness package (redundancy + partition amplification + quality culling + timing completeness + HIC catch-up) produces ~3x improvement in lineage half-life, with self-reinforcing partition quality across generations.

3. **Supplementary proto-Darwinian dynamics (A3)** (Gamma): A low-dimensional selection landscape with 3+ directional trait axes (HIC quality, division threshold, assembly efficiency) produces cumulative multi-generation enrichment above the drift/filtering baseline, driven by the P1+P2 compounding loop, converging to a bounded optimum in ~5–15 generations.

These three advances form a connected hierarchy: D3 regulated division → L3 lineage robustness → A3 proto-Darwinian dynamics. Each stage depends on and amplifies the previous. Together, they produce the first integrated reproductive-selection system in the GRUT architecture: proto-cells that divide with improved quality, persist as robust lineages, and evolve toward better-performing variants on a convergent selection landscape.

**What Book VI did NOT achieve:** Inheritance-robust reproduction (L4), dominant metabolism, active transport, strong adaptive evolution (A4), open-ended evolution (A5), or life. All remain explicitly fenced.

**Cost:** Zero new postulates. Book VI added zero bridge debt. The three stages are the twenty-first through twenty-third zero-cost upper-stack targets. Total cost remains 14/7/1/6 (unchanged from Book V Delta).

**Realized identity after Book VI:**

> A self-limiting, reproducing proto-cell with supplementary proto-metabolic coupling, supplementary regulated division, supplementary lineage robustness, and supplementary proto-Darwinian dynamics — converging toward a bounded multi-trait optimum on a low-dimensional selection landscape.

**Book VII handoff:** The next structurally load-bearing boundary is the gap between supplementary and dominant energetic/metabolic organization. The current scaffold achieves ~5–10% directed energetic fraction. Moving toward dominant organization (~30%+) would require either many more HIC variants or a fundamentally different (diffusible) coupling mechanism. This is the most consequential remaining gap because all other remaining boundaries (inheritance robustness, strong adaptation, ecological structure) become easier once metabolic organization is dominant rather than supplementary.

## 3. The Alpha-Through-Gamma Arc

### 3.1 Alpha: Supplementary Regulated Division (D3)

**What was earned:** Three zero-cost routes form a connected division-control package:
- Route A: Content-load-responsive division timing (assembly catalysts produce boundary material proportionally to content)
- Route C: P2-mediated quality-dependent timing bias (higher-quality proto-cells accumulate functional content faster)
- Route D: Spatial-correlation partition bias (K=7 mesh topology + functional-object clustering → coherent-cluster inheritance)

**Key result:** Nonviable-daughter rate improves from ~10–30% (D1) to ~3–8% (D3). Still statistical, not guaranteed.

### 3.2 Beta: Supplementary Lineage Robustness (L3)

**What was earned:** Five zero-cost robustness routes form a connected package:
- Route A: Redundancy-assisted retention (P1 acceleration → more copies → lower P(missing type))
- Route B: Partition-bias amplification (spatial clustering self-reinforces across generations)
- Route C: Quality-linked lineage filtering (P2 quality filter enriches population for high-quality lineages)
- Route D: Timing-linked completeness bias (division at content-complete threshold)
- Route E: HIC-linked catch-up (under-equipped daughters recover faster with HIC acceleration)

**Key result:** Lineage half-life improves ~3x (from ~6 gen to ~16 gen). Branching lineage is resilient and self-reinforcing. Still not inheritance-robust (~4% per-gen failure persists).

### 3.3 Gamma: Supplementary Proto-Darwinian Dynamics (A3)

**What was earned:** A low-dimensional selection landscape with directional enrichment:
- HIC-P1 quality: → geometric optimum (compounding via P1+P2 loop)
- HIC-P2 quality: → fidelity plateau (self-reinforcing)
- Division threshold: → optimal value (convergent)
- Assembly efficiency: → substrate ceiling (selected)

**Key result:** Population enrichment above drift/filtering baseline for ~5–15 generations. Convergent to bounded optimum. Not open-ended. Not innovative.

### 3.4 How the Stages Connect

The three stages form a reinforcing hierarchy:

```
D3 (division quality) → fewer nonviable daughters → L3 (lineage persistence)
L3 (lineage persistence) → multi-generation variant tracking → A3 (directional selection)
A3 (selection enrichment) → better HIC/threshold/assembly → D3 (even better division)
```

This is a **closed improvement loop**: division quality → persistence → selection → better division quality. The loop converges (because the selection landscape has a bounded optimum), but while it operates (~5–15 generations), it produces genuine adaptive improvement.

### 8.2 Recommended Book VII First Target

**Dominant Metabolism / Energetic Expansion Audit.**

Justification:

1. **Highest leverage:** The supplementary energetic organization (~5–10% directed) is the controlling bottleneck for every remaining biological boundary. Division quality, lineage robustness, and adaptive dynamics are all supplementary *because* the HIC network is supplementary. If directed flux were dominant (~30%+), the HIC network would organize the proto-cell's operation rather than merely enhancing it.

2. **Structural accessibility:** Two routes are visible:
   - **Route A (more HIC variants):** Additional HIC pairings (beyond P1 and P2) would increase the directed fraction. Each new pairing requires a new HIC sequence variant but not a new postulate (the HIC functional class is already postulated).
   - **Route B (diffusible coupling):** A mobile energy-carrying intermediate that distributes coupling energy throughout the proto-cell (not just at fixed HIC sites). This would require a new postulate (a new type of object: a diffusible activated molecule) and is the path toward an ATP-like currency.

3. **Load-bearing position:** Dominant metabolism is upstream of: inheritance robustness (energy-driven segregation could guarantee partition), strong adaptation (metabolically organized proto-cells can maintain more complex functional networks), and active transport (energy-driven boundary pumps require energy distribution).

4. **Direct continuity with Book V:** The Book V energy-flow arc (Beta → Gamma → Delta → Epsilon → Zeta) established the HIC bridge and the supplementary network. Book VII naturally continues this arc by asking whether the supplementary network can become dominant.
