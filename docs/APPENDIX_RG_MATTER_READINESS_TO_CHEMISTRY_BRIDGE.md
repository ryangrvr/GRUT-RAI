## 10. Whether Chemistry/Life Bridge Is Honest

The matter program is **complete as a pre-chemistry ledger**. It provides
an honest foundation for future chemistry/life work:

- What GRUT matter HAS: bosonic scaffold (localization, persistence,
  composition, exchange), pre-atomic grammar, fermionic route inventory
- What GRUT matter LACKS: stable objects, binding, fermions, probability,
  shells, multi-constituent matter
- What chemistry NEEDS: all 6 bridge conditions satisfied
- What is MAPPED: every gap, every route, every boundary

The bridge is MAPPED but NOT CROSSED. Crossing requires building the
6 bridge conditions, each of which is a specific, actionable extension.
