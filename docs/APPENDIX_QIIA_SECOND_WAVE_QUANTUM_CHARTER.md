# Appendix Q-II.A: Second-Wave Quantum Charter

GRUT Quantum Program -- Phase Q-II.A

---

## 1. Program Objective

Appendix Q, Part II extends the first-wave quantum foundations (Q-B through
Q-F) by investigating composite state structure, entanglement, richer
observable algebra, field excitation structure, probability/determination
mechanisms, and quantum matter readiness.  Each sector is audited to its
natural depth without forcing derivation.

### Not in Scope

- Deriving the Born rule from first principles
- Deriving entanglement as native canon
- Deriving outcome selection or measurement collapse
- Deriving many-body matter at quantum level
- Deriving field quantization in full
- Reopening closed first-wave audits except to classify inheritance
- Promoting any first-wave result above its locked Appendix P class
