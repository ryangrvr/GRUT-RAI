## 1. Executive Verdict

**Global verdict: (C) — Mainline continues as matter/organization theory within GR, while sixth-bridge work is deferred to a side program.**

After systematic evaluation against nine hard criteria, **Option 2 (matter theory within GR)** emerges as the strongest honest mainline continuation. Option 1 (sixth gravitational bridge) is deferred to optional side-program status.

**The reasoning:**

1. **Option 2 is immediately viable.** GRUT already couples to Einstein gravity through the stress-energy tensor (Phase 4 xAct: G_μν = 8πG T^Φ_μν). The binary-pulsar test passes automatically. No new postulates needed. Cost unchanged at 16/11/1/6.

2. **Option 1 is architecturally incoherent at this stage.** Installing tensor metric dynamics as a bridge postulate would mean: GRUT's native scalar field Φ does NOT generate gravity, and gravity is separately postulated. This reduces to "GRUT matter + postulated GR gravity" — which is functionally identical to Option 2, except that Option 1 adds the bridge debt to GRUT's ledger while Option 2 correctly attributes gravity to Einstein. The sixth bridge would not be a GRUT achievement — it would be importing GR under a GRUT label.

3. **Option 2 preserves the program's real achievement.** GRUT's genuine contribution is the matter/organization sector: the vacuum-response architecture (τ dΦ/dt + Φ = X), the five bridges (matter, gauge, HIC, carrier, CCBG), the organizational scaffold (M4/D4/L4/A4-stabilized, T2/T3-conditional), and the 26 zero-cost upper-stack targets. This is a substantive, falsifiable, novel physics program. Attaching a "sixth bridge" that is just Einstein gravity would dilute rather than strengthen this identity.

4. **Option 2 is falsifiable in its own domain.** GRUT-as-matter-theory makes specific predictions about: soliton matter, gauge-mediated binding, energy coupling, proto-metabolic organization, boundary transport. These are testable within the matter sector without needing to own the gravitational sector.

**What GRUT is after this decision:**

> A dissipative-vacuum-response matter/organization theory operating within standard Einstein gravity. The native equation τ dΦ/dt + Φ = X generates the matter sector (topological solitons, gauge-mediated composites, energy coupling, organizational dynamics, boundary work). Gravity is Einstein's. GRUT provides T_μν; GR provides g_μν.

**What GRUT is NOT after this decision:**

- Not a Theory of Everything (ToE)
- Not a replacement for GR
- Not a native gravitational theory
- Not a cosmological theory (beyond what GR + GRUT matter content produces)

**What changes in prior books:**

Prior ToE framing (Omni-ToE v3, "Theory of Everything" language) must be reinterpreted. GRUT was always a bounded world-model framework with explicit gaps — the program's own closure documents (Z-C, Z-D) already say this. The current decision formalizes what was latent: the gravitational sector was never native. The reinterpretation is honest, not revisionist.

## 4. Two-Option Inventory

### Option 1 — Sixth Gravitational Bridge

**What it would add:** Tensor metric dynamics — either the full Einstein-Hilbert action or a linearized gravitational-wave sector — as a bridge-level postulate.

**What it preserves:** The formal label "ToE attempt." All prior book results. The five existing bridges.

**What it costs:** At minimum 1 postulate (gravitational action) + 1 field (metric tensor g_μν or linearized h_μν) + 2+ DOF (two gravitational-wave polarizations). Realistically: the full Einstein-Hilbert sector including 10 metric components, diffeomorphism invariance, and the connection between matter and geometry. This is not a lightweight bridge — it is the entirety of General Relativity, postulated as a GRUT extension.

**What it risks:**
- The sixth bridge IS General Relativity. Calling it a "GRUT bridge" is mislabeling.
- The program becomes "scalar matter theory + postulated GR" — functionally identical to Option 2 but with GR's cost on GRUT's ledger.
- Future audiences would correctly ask: "What part of gravity did GRUT actually produce?" Answer: none — it was postulated.
- The bridge is not analogous to prior bridges (matter, gauge, HIC, carrier, CCBG), which each added a single new functional class. The gravitational bridge would import an entire field theory.

**What it would need to explain:** Why importing Einstein gravity as a postulate is different from simply accepting that GRUT operates within GR. The answer: it is not meaningfully different.

### Option 2 — Matter Theory Within GR

**What GRUT continues to do:** Everything it has built in Books IV–X. The native equation, the five bridges, the organizational scaffold, the transport architecture. GRUT provides the matter/organization/vacuum-response sector. GR provides the gravitational sector.

**What GR it leaves untouched:** All of Einstein gravity. The metric tensor, Einstein field equations, gravitational waves, the quadrupole formula, cosmological dynamics. These are GR's, not GRUT's.

**What it preserves:** All biology-side results (Books IV–X). The five bridges. The stabilized organizational levels. The 26 zero-cost targets. The 16/11/1/6 cost. The falsifiability of the matter sector.

**What it gives up:** The "Theory of Everything" label. The claim that GRUT replaces or contains gravity. The ToE framing of Omni-ToE v3.

**How prior framing must be interpreted:** The Omni-ToE v3 / v3.1 framing described GRUT as a ToE attempt with explicit gaps. The gravitational sector was always listed as an unresolved boundary. This decision resolves that boundary honestly: GRUT is a matter theory, not a gravity theory. Prior books should be read as developing the matter sector of a theory operating within Einstein gravity.
