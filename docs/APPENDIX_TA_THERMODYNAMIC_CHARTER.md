## 10 Mandatory Distinctions
1. Dissipation vs thermodynamics
2. Time asymmetry vs irreversibility
3. Irreversibility vs entropy increase
4. Relaxation scale vs temperature
5. Balance law vs conservation law
6. Late-time relaxation vs equilibrium
7. Entropy-like monotone vs thermodynamic entropy
8. Effective analogy vs native derivation
9. Nonequilibrium description vs statistical mechanics
10. State-function language vs phenomenological monotonicity

## 8 Forbidden Failure Modes
FFM-T.1 through FFM-T.8: entropy from dissipation alone, entropy increase
from time asymmetry, temperature from tau, ensemble without probability,
free energy without state variables, equilibrium from fixed point,
FDT without derivation, statistical mechanics from decoherence.
