## 1. Executive Verdict

**Global verdict: (B) — M4 conditionally upgrades adaptive dynamics to A4-conditional, but unconditional A4 remains absent.**

Under M4 backing with D4-conditional division and L4-conditional lineage persistence, the adaptive landscape undergoes three genuine structural changes beyond the A3 baseline:

1. **Landscape broadening:** The carrier bridge introduces **at least three new heritable trait axes** (carrier-loading efficiency, carrier-target compatibility, HIC-carrier coupling quality) that are entirely absent at A3. Combined with the four A3 axes, the effective landscape dimensionality increases from 3–4 to **6–7 interacting axes**. This is not a relabeling — carrier-related traits are genuinely new selectable features whose fitness impact is M4-dependent.

2. **Gradient amplification:** At A3, selection gradients operate on a supplementary energetic fraction (~5–10% directed). At M4, directed flux rises to ~30–34%. The fitness differential between better and worse HIC variants is amplified by **~3–5×** because HIC quality now affects ~30% of internal operations rather than ~5%. Selection can discriminate smaller quality differences, producing finer-grained optimization.

3. **Enrichment-window extension:** At A3, active enrichment lasts ~5–15 generations before the population converges to the bounded optimum. Under M4, three factors extend this window: (a) the optimum is shifted outward (carrier-related axes are not yet optimized), (b) L4-conditional lineage persistence provides ~50–140 generations for selection to operate (vs ~16 at L3), and (c) the multi-domain coupling between energetic, division, and lineage fitness creates deeper combinatorial optimization that takes more generations to explore. Estimated active enrichment window: **~15–40 generations** before the extended plateau.

**Why A4-conditional and not A4 unconditional:**
- Every landscape change depends on M4. If the carrier fails, carrier-related trait axes vanish, gradients revert to A3 levels, and enrichment window contracts to ~5–15 gen.
- Innovation remains absent. The scaffold optimizes existing functions (HIC quality, carrier quality, division timing, assembly efficiency) but does not generate qualitatively new functional classes through mutation + selection alone.
- Ecological structure remains absent. All proto-cells compete in a shared monomer pool with no niches, no environmental gradients, and no frequency-dependent dynamics.
- The dynamics are still convergent — they plateau at a higher, broader optimum, but they plateau.

**Why not merely A3+:**
- Carrier-related trait axes are genuinely new. They are not restatements of HIC quality or assembly efficiency — they require the carrier bridge to exist and to have fitness consequences.
- The multi-domain coupling (energetic × division × lineage → fitness) creates a richer optimization target than A3's single-domain (quality → replication rate) landscape.
- The enrichment window extension is structural (from L4-conditional persistence), not just parametric (faster convergence to the same point).
- The gradient amplification is a consequence of the directed-fraction inversion (M4: directed > ambient for key processes), which qualitatively changes the selection environment.

**Cost:** Zero new postulates. Twenty-sixth zero-cost upper-stack target.

## 6. M4-Backed Adaptive Route Families

### Family A — Stronger Gradient Reinforcement

**Concept:** M4 backing amplifies existing trait-success gradients. HIC-quality variants that were barely distinguishable at A3 (where HIC affected ~5% of events) become clearly distinguishable at M4 (where HIC affects ~30% of events).

**Mechanism:** The fitness differential between HIC-P1 variants with quality q₁ and q₂ scales approximately as:

Δ_fitness ∝ (q₁ − q₂) × f_directed

At A3: f_directed ≈ 0.05–0.10. At M4: f_directed ≈ 0.30–0.34. Gradient amplification: **~3–5×**.

**Consequence:** Finer-grained selection. At A3, only coarse quality differences (near-optimal vs poor) were selectable. At M4, subtler quality differences (slightly-better vs slightly-worse among good variants) are selectable. This extends the active enrichment period: instead of converging after removing poor variants, the population continues to optimize among good variants.

**Is this new?** NO — this is A3 Route B (HIC-quality enrichment) with stronger gradients. The mechanism is the same; the strength is amplified.

**Verdict:** Real gradient amplification. Strengthens A3 but not independently A4-qualifying.

### Family B — Plateau Shift / Expanded Optimum

**Concept:** At A3, the optimum was limited by the supplementary energetic fraction — improving HIC quality beyond a certain point yielded diminishing returns because most operations were ambient-thermal. At M4, the optimum shifts outward because improvement now affects ~30% of operations.

**Mechanism:** The A3 HIC-quality plateau occurs when the marginal fitness gain from better HIC quality drops below the noise floor (mutations degrade quality faster than selection can maintain improvement). At M4, the marginal fitness gain from better HIC quality is ~3× larger → the plateau is reached at a higher quality level.

Additionally, carrier-related trait axes (loading efficiency, target compatibility, coupling quality) are entirely new dimensions. Their optima have not been approached. The population starts far from the carrier-related optima and has room to optimize.

**Is this new?** PARTIALLY — the plateau shift on existing axes is strengthened A3. The carrier-related axes being far from optimum IS new — it opens a genuine new optimization frontier.

**Verdict:** The plateau shift on existing axes is A3-strengthened. The new carrier-axis frontier is genuinely new and A4-qualifying.

### Family C — Multi-Domain Coupling Deepening

**Concept:** At A3, fitness was approximately separable: HIC quality, division threshold, and assembly efficiency contributed nearly independently to lineage success. At M4, fitness depends on coordinated multi-domain performance — the coupling between energetic infrastructure quality, division regulation, lineage persistence, and adaptive potential creates a richer optimization landscape.

**Mechanism:** Under M4, a proto-cell's fitness depends not just on individual trait quality but on how well its traits coordinate:
- An excellent HIC with poor carrier-loading geometry underperforms
- An excellent carrier with poor target-site geometry underperforms
- A well-tuned division threshold with poor pre-division content quality underperforms

The optimal configuration requires **coordinated optimization** across all trait axes simultaneously. This creates an optimization problem that is inherently richer than single-axis optimization: the population must find a good point in a 6–7-dimensional space with non-trivial interactions, not just climb four independent hills.

**Is this new?** YES — the multi-domain coupling at this depth is M4-dependent. At A3, the coupling was weak (HIC quality and division timing interacted modestly). At M4, energetic × division × lineage coupling is strong and creates genuinely non-additive fitness interactions.

**Verdict:** Genuinely new multi-domain coupling. A4-qualifying.

### Family D — Repair-Backed Retention of Beneficial Variants

**Concept:** Under M4, carrier-driven repair and post-fission recovery reduce the rate at which beneficial variants are lost to degradation or bad partition. High-quality configurations persist longer, allowing selection to act more effectively.

**Mechanism:** At A3 with L3, a beneficial variant has ~4% per-gen chance of being lost by partition failure. After ~16 generations (L3 half-life), the beneficial variant has a ~50% chance of being gone. At M4 with L4-conditional, the per-gen loss is ~0.5–1.5%. After ~50–140 generations (L4 half-life), the beneficial variant has a ~50% chance of being gone. The retention window for beneficial variants is ~3–9× longer.

**Consequence:** Selection has ~3–9× more generations to act on beneficial variants before they are lost to drift/partition failure. This directly translates to: (a) higher probability that a beneficial variant spreads through the population before being lost, and (b) ability to select for smaller fitness advantages (because the variant persists long enough for the small advantage to compound).

**Is this new?** PARTIALLY — L3 already provided some retention. L4-conditional provides much longer retention. The mechanism is the same (lineage persistence); the magnitude is increased.

**Verdict:** Real retention improvement. Strengthens selection capacity. Supporting role, not independently A4-qualifying.

### Family E — HIC/Carrier Co-Optimization

**Concept:** Under M4, the energetic infrastructure itself (HIC scaffolds, carrier molecules, discharge-pocket geometry) becomes a rich adaptive target. At A3, HIC quality was the primary energetic trait. At M4, the carrier adds multiple new optimization targets: loading efficiency, carrier energy content, discharge selectivity, carrier lifetime. Optimization of these traits is a genuinely new adaptive program absent at A3.

**Mechanism:** Carrier-related traits are heritable (encoded in HIC scaffold sequences and target-scaffold sequences). Variants with better carrier-loading geometry produce more effective carriers → more remote directed events → higher fitness. Variants with better discharge-pocket geometry at target sites receive more carrier energy → more efficient remote processes → higher fitness.

The carrier-related optimization landscape is multi-dimensional (at least 3 new axes) and coupled to the existing HIC-quality landscape (better HIC + better carrier = non-additively better). This creates a genuinely richer adaptive target.

**Is this new?** YES — carrier-related optimization is entirely absent at A3. The carrier bridge opened these trait axes; M4 made them selectable.

**Verdict:** Genuinely new adaptive target. A4-qualifying.

### Family F — Pseudo-Upgrade / No Real A4

**Concept:** The apparent improvement is only faster A3 convergence — the population climbs the same A3 hill faster (stronger gradients) and stays on top longer (L4 persistence), but the hill itself is unchanged.

**Test against Families B, C, E:**

- Family B (plateau shift + new carrier axes): The landscape IS changed — new axes are present. The population has a genuinely new frontier to explore, not just a faster climb of the old hill. **Family F FAILS for B.**
- Family C (multi-domain coupling): The fitness function IS structurally different — non-additive interactions create a richer optimization problem. **Family F FAILS for C.**
- Family E (carrier co-optimization): The adaptive target IS new — carrier-related traits didn't exist at A3. **Family F FAILS for E.**
- Family A (gradient amplification): THIS IS faster climb of the same hill. **Family F APPLIES to A.**
- Family D (retention): THIS IS longer stay on the hill. **Family F APPLIES to D.**

**Verdict:** Family F applies to A and D (stronger/longer A3) but not to B, C, E (genuinely new landscape structure). A4-conditional is NOT purely pseudo-upgrade.

## 7. Multi-Generation Enrichment Reassessment Under M4

### 7.1 Phase 1: Initial Rapid Convergence (Generations 0–10)

Same dynamics as A3 but faster. The A3 trait axes (HIC quality, threshold, assembly) converge toward their optima ~2–3× faster due to amplified gradients. Filtering removes non-functional variants. Compounding P1+P2 loop drives HIC-quality enrichment.

**Difference from A3:** Faster convergence (stronger gradients). Same qualitative dynamics.

### 7.2 Phase 2: A3 Plateau Approach + Carrier-Axis Exploration (Generations 10–25)

At A3, the population would plateau here. Under M4, two things happen:

**2a.** Existing A3 axes approach their (shifted) M4 optima. The plateau is slightly higher than the A3 optimum because stronger gradients enable finer optimization. But this is diminishing returns on existing axes.

**2b.** Carrier-related trait axes begin to be selected. The population starts exploring the carrier-loading, carrier-target, and HIC-carrier coupling landscape. This is a genuinely new optimization frontier with room for directional improvement.

### 7.3 Phase 3: Multi-Domain Coupled Optimization (Generations 25–40+)

As carrier-related traits approach their individual optima, the interaction effects between domains become the dominant selection target. Proto-cells with well-coordinated energetic + division + lineage packages outcompete those with individually-good but poorly-coordinated traits.

This is the richest adaptive phase — the population optimizes not just individual traits but their coordination. The fitness landscape in this phase is more complex than in Phase 1 or 2 because it involves multi-dimensional interactions.

**Duration:** Estimated ~15–25 additional generations of active multi-domain optimization before the population converges to the M4-backed multi-trait optimum.

### 7.4 Phase 4: Extended M4 Plateau (Generations 40+)

The population converges to the M4-backed optimum. Further enrichment is minimal. The dynamics plateau — at a higher, broader optimum than A3, but still plateau.

**No open-ended dynamics.** Innovation is absent. No new functional classes emerge. No ecological structure develops. The plateau is a genuine ceiling, not a temporary pause.

### 7.5 Enrichment Summary Table

| Phase | Generations | Dominant process | New vs A3? | Landscape region |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1: Rapid convergence | 0–10 | A3 axes converge (faster) | Faster A3 | Existing axes |
| 2: Carrier exploration | 10–25 | Carrier-axis optimization begins | **Genuinely new** | New carrier axes |
| 3: Multi-domain coupling | 25–40+ | Coordinated multi-domain optimization | **Genuinely new** | Interaction space |
| 4: M4 plateau | 40+ | Convergence to M4 optimum | Extended A3 ceiling | Full landscape converged |

**Total active enrichment:** ~30–40 generations (vs ~5–15 at A3). Extension is ~2–3×, driven by new axes and multi-domain coupling.

### 7.6 Is This Just Longer A3 or Genuinely Richer Dynamics?

**Phases 2 and 3 are genuinely richer.** They involve optimization of trait axes that don't exist at A3 (carrier-related) and multi-domain coupling that is structurally absent at A3 (energetic × division × lineage interactions). The dynamics are not "climbing the same hill for longer" — they are "climbing a bigger hill with more dimensions."

**Phase 1 is just faster A3.** Phase 4 is just longer plateau. The A4-conditional claim rests on Phases 2 and 3.

### Classification Decision

**A4-conditional is justified** because:

1. **Multi-component improvement (criterion A):** Gradient strength (~3–5×), landscape breadth (+3 axes), enrichment window (~2–3×), multi-domain coupling (new fitness-function structure), beneficial-variant retention (~3–9×). Five components improve.

2. **Beyond A3 bounded regime (criterion B):** The landscape is genuinely broader (6–7 vs 3–4 axes), the fitness function is structurally richer (multi-domain coupling vs approximately additive), and the enrichment involves genuinely new territory (carrier-related trait axes that don't exist at A3). This is not just faster convergence to the same A3 optimum — it is convergence to a higher, broader, structurally different optimum.

3. **Persists across many generations (criterion C):** L4-conditional provides ~50–140 gen for selection. Active enrichment extends to ~30–40 gen through three distinct phases (rapid convergence, carrier exploration, multi-domain optimization). The improvement is not explained by stronger culling alone — Phases 2 and 3 involve genuine optimization of new trait axes.

4. **Best explained by M4 organizational support (criterion D):** Carrier-related trait axes (Families B, E) exist only because M4 installed the carrier bridge. Multi-domain coupling at M4 strength (Family C) exists only because the directed fraction is ~30%. Gradient amplification (Family A) exists only because M4 raised the directed fraction. These are M4-dependent structural changes, not relabelings of A3 effects.

5. **Conditional on M4 (criterion E):** All landscape changes disappear if the carrier fails. Carrier-related axes vanish. Gradients revert to A3 levels. Multi-domain coupling weakens to A3 levels. The scaffold returns to A3 unconditionally.
