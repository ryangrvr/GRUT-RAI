## 1. Executive Verdict

**Global verdict: (A) — Book V closes with meaningful but bounded state advance; Book VI handoff is determined.**

Book V earned three genuine structural advances beyond Book IV:

1. **Homeostasis preconditions** (Alpha): Three intrinsic negative feedback mechanisms — substrate-depletion cap, template-catalyst ratio self-correction, growth-division size regulation — produce a bounded operating window for sustained multi-cycle reproduction. The proto-cell is passively self-limiting.

2. **Energy coupling at bridge level** (Delta–Epsilon): The Hybrid Intercepting Catalyst (HIC), at a cost of 1 new postulate + 1 new parameter, provides Level 5 local energy coupling — genuine mechanical transduction of favorable-reaction energy to drive unfavorable processes. The HIC operates in concerted mode (direct transducer, not energy-storage device).

3. **Supplementary proto-metabolic subnetwork** (Zeta): Two HIC pairings (P1: replication acceleration; P2: fidelity enhancement) form a connected benefit loop with cross-generational compounding. The network is heritable, selectable, and self-reinforcing — but supplementary (~5–10% of total flux), not dominant (~90–95% remains ambient-thermal).

Book V also produced three important negative results:

1. **Zero-cost energy coupling is impossible** (Beta–Gamma): The zero-cost scaffold has no high-energy intermediate. All favorable processes thermalize through delocalized channels. The energy-coupling gap is structurally irreducible without a new postulate.

2. **The HIC is a transducer, not a battery** (Epsilon stress test): Store-and-wait mode fails because thermal leak dominates diffusive target encounter. The HIC works only in concerted mode with both substrates pre-positioned. This narrows its capability to synchronous transduction.

3. **The proto-metabolic network is supplementary, not dominant** (Zeta): Directed fraction ~5–10%. The system remains overwhelmingly ambient-thermal. The network enhances but does not organize the system's operation.

**Realized identity after Book V:**

> A self-limiting, reproducing proto-cell with bridge-level homeostasis preconditions and a supplementary proto-metabolic subnetwork providing ~5–10% directed energetic flux through concerted HIC-mediated coupling, operating predominantly on ambient thermal energy within a bounded multi-cycle operating window.

**Total cost:** 14 postulates, 7 free parameters, 1 new field, 6 propagating DOF. Book V added 1 postulate + 1 parameter (the HIC bridge). All other Book V results (homeostasis, network organization) are zero-cost.

**Book VI handoff:** Regulated growth and division. The proto-cell reproduces through uncontrolled mechanical fission. Regulated reproduction — controlled timing, content distribution, and daughter quality — is the next structurally load-bearing boundary.

## 3. The Alpha-Through-Zeta Arc

### 3.1 Alpha: Homeostasis Preconditions

**What was earned:** Three intrinsic negative feedback mechanisms producing a bounded operating window.
**What was NOT earned:** Active regulation, set-point homeostasis, sensor-actuator circuits.
**Key insight:** The reproducing proto-cell is passively self-limiting — not because it has regulatory machinery, but because diffusion limitation, competitive growth, and mechanical pressure create natural bounds on internal variables.
**Cost:** Zero.

### 3.2 Beta: Energy-Flow Threshold (NOT CROSSED)

**What was tested:** Whether favorable processes can systematically drive unfavorable processes.
**What was found:** Level 4 only — energetic asymmetry exists (favorable/unfavorable classes) but no mechanism connects them. All energy thermalizes before any structure intercepts it.
**Key insight:** The scaffold has catalysis (barrier lowering), concentration advantage (compartment retention), and directionality (bond hierarchy), but none of these constitutes energy coupling (Level 5).
**Cost:** Zero (analytical).

### 3.3 Gamma: High-Energy Intermediate Search (NEGATIVE)

**What was tested:** 31 candidates across 5 search classes against 7 hard criteria.
**What was found:** 0 candidates pass. 0 pass even 6 of 7.
**Key insight:** The root cause is structural: every favorable process releases energy through delocalized channels (gauge radiation, thermal motion) that thermalize faster than any existing structure can intercept. There is no "energy interceptor."
**Cost:** Zero (analytical).

### 3.4 Delta: Energy-Coupling Bridge Architecture

**What was found:** The Hybrid Intercepting Catalyst (HIC) — a scaffold whose pocket mechanically deforms during an in-pocket favorable reaction, trapping energy as backbone strain and discharging it at a separate site to drive a distinct unfavorable process.
**Key insight:** The HIC is a functional-capability postulate (a new thing existing objects can do), not a structural postulate (a new type of object). It introduces no new fields or DOF.
**Cost:** 1 postulate + 1 parameter (η_couple). This is the first upper-stack bridge debt and the lightest of all three bridges.

### 3.5 Epsilon: HIC Stress Test and Threshold Reassessment

**What was found:** The HIC survives stress testing but only in concerted mode. The store-and-wait mode fails (thermal leak dominates). The HIC is a direct transducer, not an energy-storage device.
**What was earned:** Level 5 local coupling — genuine, conditional.
**Key revision from Delta:** The HIC's loaded state is transient (~femtoseconds in concerted mode), not persistent. The HIC cannot store energy for later asynchronous use.
**Cost:** Zero (analytical).

### 3.6 Zeta: Proto-Metabolism Re-Evaluation

**What was found:** P1 (soliton assembly → duplex separation) and P2 (monomer assembly → mismatch removal) form a connected benefit loop. The loop compounds across generations: P1 feeds P2 (more substrates), P2 improves P1 (better scaffolds).
**What was earned:** Level 5 networked supplementary — a connected, heritable, selectable, compounding energetic subnetwork. Directed fraction ~5–10%.
**What was NOT earned:** Dominant metabolism (Level 5+ or L6). Active transport. ATP-like currency.
**Cost:** Zero (network emerges from multiple HIC instances).

### 8.2 Recommended Book VI First Target

**Regulated Growth and Division Preconditions Audit.**

Justification:

1. **Structural necessity:** The current proto-cell divides by uncontrolled mechanical fission. Daughters receive random ~50/50 content partitions. ~10–30% of daughters are nonviable (missing essential functional sequences). This is the single largest source of waste in the reproductive cycle.

2. **Direct leverage:** If division timing and content distribution could be even partially regulated — e.g., an HIC variant that drives content segregation or boundary constriction at a specific moment — the nonviable-daughter rate would drop, reproductive efficiency would increase, and lineage persistence would strengthen.

3. **Load-bearing position:** Regulated division sits upstream of inheritance robustness (which requires controlled partition) and Darwinian dynamics (which requires reliable reproduction). Fixing division unlocks both downstream targets.

4. **Minimal distance from current scaffold:** The scaffold already has HIC-mediated coupling (from Delta), scaffold catalysis (from Pi), and compartment structure (from Tau). A division-regulating mechanism might be achievable as another HIC application — coupling a favorable internal process to boundary constriction or content segregation. This is speculative but structurally accessible.

5. **Ordering logic:** Dominant metabolism expansion (more HIC variants) is valuable but does not fix the division problem. Function-to-fitness mapping is important but premature without reliable reproduction. Adaptive dynamics requires everything else first.
