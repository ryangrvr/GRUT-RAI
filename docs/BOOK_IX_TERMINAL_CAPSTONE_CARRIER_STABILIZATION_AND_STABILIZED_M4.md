## 3. The W0 + Alpha Arc

### 3.1 Why W0 Mattered

Book VII committed the carrier provisionally, with ΔG ≥ 28 kT as an unproven plausibility argument. The entire Book VIII downstream reassessment (D4/L4/A4-conditional) depended on this single unproven parameter. W0 asked: can the lower-stack architecture provide derivational support?

**W0 delivered:**
- The loaded state identified as (N=2, ℓ=0, S=0) — the first excited s-wave state of the K=2 composite
- Metastability mechanism: dipole selection rule (Δℓ = ±1 forbids single-gauge-boson decay)
- Derivable inequality: ΔE₁₂ = (3/16)α_g²M_sk ≥ 28 kT when α_g²(M_sk/kT) ≥ 149
- The robust regime shown to be the generic expectation for thermally stable scaffolds with moderate coupling
- Carrier barrier upgraded from "matched parameter" to "lower-stack-supported"
- Debt reduced but NOT erased — two open items remained

### 3.2 What IX Alpha Resolved Beyond W0

W0 left two load-bearing open items:

**Open Item 1 — Two-gauge-boson decay:** IX Alpha computed the scaling: Γ(2γ) ∝ α_g⁸ × M_sk. The rate is strongly suppressed in the weak-coupling regime (α_g ≲ 0.02) where τ(2γ)/τ_diffusion > 10. This CONTAINS the dominant leak channel without new assumptions — the weak-coupling regime is the scaffold's pre-existing operating condition for hydrogenic binding.

**Open Item 2 — Dissipation coupling:** IX Alpha assessed the GRUT native dissipation pathway. Under the scale-separation assumption (ω_composite ≫ γ) — already required by the matter and gauge bridges since Book IV Alpha — the dissipation-induced decay rate is suppressed by (γ/ω)^n × exp(−ΔE/kT). This adds NO new constraint.

### 3.3 Why the Carrier Is Now Stabilized Rather Than Merely Conditional

The conditionality has undergone a qualitative shift:

| Before (Book VIII) | After (Book IX) |
|-------------------|----------------|
| M4 requires ΔG ≥ 28 kT — an externally matched parameter with no derivational support | M4 requires weak coupling (α_g ≲ 0.02) + scale separation (ω ≫ γ) — structural properties already assumed for the scaffold to function |
| ΔG could in principle be any value; the program CHOSE 28 kT | The barrier height is DERIVED from the K=2 binding energy; the lifetime is BOUNDED by two-boson scaling |
| The loaded state was POSTULATED as a "conformational switch" | The loaded state is IDENTIFIED as the (N=2, ℓ=0) quantum-mechanically metastable excited bound state |
| The leak rate was ASSUMED to follow Arrhenius kinetics | The leak rate is COMPUTED (scaling) and shown to be sufficiently suppressed in weak coupling |

The word "stabilized" is chosen deliberately: the carrier functions under the scaffold's own structural assumptions, not under an external parameter match. It is NOT "unconditional" because α_g remains a free parameter — but the required α_g regime is the one the scaffold was already designed for.

### 3.4 Why Debt Is Strongly Reduced But Not Erased

**Strongly reduced because:**
- Barrier height derived from binding energy (not matched)
- Metastability mechanism confirmed (selection rule + 2γ containment + dissipation assessment)
- Lifetime quantitatively bounded (scaling known)
- Loaded state structurally identified ((N=2, ℓ=0))

**Not erased because:**
- The carrier POSTULATE (existence of a carrier functional class) is retained — the K=2 composite's ability to be loaded, diffuse, and selectively discharge at targets is still a bridge-level functional assertion
- The carrier PARAMETERS (E_carrier, τ_carrier) are strongly supported but α_g is free
- The α_g ≲ 0.02 constraint, while within the weak-coupling regime, is tighter than previously recognized
- The scale-separation assumption (ω ≫ γ) is inherited from Book IV Alpha and was never independently derived

### 8.2 Handoff Decision

**Recommended Book X first target: A — Active transport / boundary-crossing mechanism.**

Justification:

1. **Highest structural leverage among achievable targets.** Active transport is the next decisive biology-side boundary. Without it, the proto-cell cannot: import resources selectively, export waste products, maintain compositional homeostasis against environmental fluctuations, or interact directionally with its environment. Every subsequent boundary (full metabolism, ecological structure, life) depends on some form of active transport.

2. **Natural continuation from stabilized proto-currency.** The carrier distributes energy internally. The question is whether a variant of this mechanism — or a new mechanism building on the carrier architecture — can move molecules across the compartment boundary. The stabilized carrier provides the energetic infrastructure; active transport asks whether that infrastructure can be extended to boundary-crossing work.

3. **Clearly load-bearing.** The scaffold's remaining gaps are: active transport, full metabolic regulation, innovation, ecological structure, open-ended evolution, life. Of these, active transport is the most concrete, the most directly testable, and the most likely to be achievable (or definitively shown to require new bridge debt) within a single Book stage.

4. **Honest about likely cost.** Active transport almost certainly requires a new bridge postulate — a mechanism by which the carrier (or a carrier-derived object) transduces energy into directed boundary-crossing work. This is analogous to the HIC bridge (Book V Delta) and the carrier bridge (Book VII Beta): a new functional class postulate at minimum cost.
