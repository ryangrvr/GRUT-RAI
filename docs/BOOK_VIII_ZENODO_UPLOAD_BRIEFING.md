## Critical Narrative Arc

Book VIII has a clean three-act structure that exactly mirrors Book VI's exploitation of the HIC bridge:

**The pattern:** Book V built the HIC bridge → Book VI extracted D3/L3/A3 at zero cost. Book VII built the carrier bridge → Book VIII extracts D4/L4/A4-conditional at zero cost. The Book V→VI pattern is repeated one organizational level higher.

**Act 1 (Alpha — Division):** The existing D3 division-control package (Routes A, C, D from Book VI Alpha) is reassessed under M4. Two genuinely new M4-dependent capabilities emerge: full-boundary directed conditioning (carrier-driven K=6/K=7 incorporation covers the entire compartment boundary, not just P3-proximal sites) and post-fission carrier-driven recovery (under-equipped daughters receive system-wide directed repair). Combined with strengthened existing routes, the nonviable-daughter rate drops from ~3–8% (D3) to ~1–3% (D4-conditional). D4-conditional is verified through a six-family route search with hard-criteria evaluation and false-positive screening. The D4 threshold (~5% nonviable) is crossed.

**Act 2 (Beta — Lineage):** The D4-conditional improvement is traced across multiple generations. The dominant driver is M4-backed copy-number deepening: carrier-driven P1 replication system-wide raises pre-division copy numbers from ~6–8 to ~8–12 per essential type, exponentially reducing per-type missing probability. Combined with post-fission carrier-driven recovery (a genuinely new lineage-level capability), per-generation essential-type loss drops from ~4% (L3) to ~0.5–1.5% (L4-conditional). Single-lineage half-life improves from ~16 generations to ~50–140 generations. Crucially, individual daughters become individually robust: a single non-branching lineage survives 100 generations with ~55% probability at mid-range M4. This is the qualitative distinction between L3 (branching-dependent persistence) and L4 (individual-daughter robustness). The HIC subtype copy number is identified as the rate-limiting class.

**Act 3 (Gamma — Adaptive Dynamics):** The adaptive landscape is reassessed under M4/D4/L4. Three structural changes emerge: landscape broadening from 3–4 to 6–7 trait axes (three new carrier-related axes: carrier-loading efficiency, carrier-target compatibility, HIC-carrier coupling quality), gradient amplification ~3–5× (directed fraction ~30% vs ~5%), and enrichment-window extension from ~5–15 to ~30–40 generations through three distinct phases (rapid convergence on existing axes → carrier-axis exploration → multi-domain coupled optimization). The fitness function shifts from approximately additive (A3) to multi-domain coupled (energetic × division × lineage interactions). Innovation and ecological structure remain absent; dynamics remain convergent. A4-conditional is verified.

**Denouement:** Book VIII closes with the full dual-state scaffold frozen:

```
M4-conditional / M3-unconditional
D4-conditional / D3-unconditional
L4-conditional / L3-unconditional
A4-conditional / A3-unconditional
```

All four conditional levels depend on the single condition ΔG_barrier ≥ 28 kT. This conditional cascade is the defining structural feature of the post-Book-VIII scaffold. Three zero-cost targets are added (26 total since Book IV Epsilon). Zero new bridge debt.

This arc — D→L→A reassessment under M4 → full dual-state scaffold → conditional cascade frozen — is the structural spine of the manuscript.
