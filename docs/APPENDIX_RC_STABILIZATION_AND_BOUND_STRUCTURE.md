## 6. Dissipation vs Stability (Track D)

- Dissipation SLOWS collapse (viscous drag on dynamics)
- Dissipation does NOT PREVENT collapse (no restoring force)
- Metastability: extended lifetime but eventual collapse
- Dissipation + Skyrme WOULD = dissipative stable soliton
- Without Skyrme: metastability only

Verdict: **dissipation_supports_metastability_only**
