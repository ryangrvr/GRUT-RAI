## Critical Narrative Arc

Book VI has a distinctive narrative shape — different from Book IV's steady climb and Book V's drama of hitting a wall then finding a bridge:

**Book VI is the zero-cost harvest.** Every single result — D3 regulated division, L3 lineage robustness, A3 proto-Darwinian dynamics — emerges at zero additional postulate cost from the scaffold that Books IV and V built. The narrative is: existing structure, when properly interrogated, produces far more organizational richness than initially apparent. The three stages don't invent anything new — they discover that the existing scaffold already contains regulated division, lineage robustness, and adaptive dynamics as structural consequences.

**Act 1 (Alpha — Division):** The uncontrolled fission from Book IV turns out to already contain the seeds of regulation. Three zero-cost routes form a connected package: content-load-responsive timing, P2-quality-dependent timing bias, and spatial-correlation partition bias. Nonviable daughters drop from ~10–30% to ~3–8%.

**Act 2 (Beta — Lineage):** The improved division, when tracked across many generations, produces self-reinforcing lineage persistence. Five zero-cost robustness routes compound across generations. Lineage half-life triples. The proto-cell lineage becomes resilient — not because any new mechanism was added, but because the existing mechanisms interact across time.

**Act 3 (Gamma — Selection):** The persistent lineages, carrying heritable variation, produce directional enrichment on a genuine selection landscape. The P1+P2 compounding loop from Book V becomes the engine of proto-Darwinian dynamics. The population shifts toward better HIC quality, better division timing, and better assembly efficiency over ~5–15 generations. Then converges to a bounded optimum.

**Denouement:** Book VI closes at the convergent optimum. The dynamics are real but bounded — supplementary, not dominant; convergent, not open-ended; low-dimensional, not ecologically complex. The next boundary is not more regulation or more robustness — it's dominant metabolism. The scaffold is supplementary in every domain; dominant organization requires expanding the energetic substrate.

This arc — zero-cost division improvement → zero-cost lineage emergence → zero-cost adaptive dynamics → convergent plateau → metabolic bottleneck identified — should structure the manuscript.
