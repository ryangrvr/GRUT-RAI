## 1. The reframe (the owner's)

The GR-2 campaign asked questions at the level of the universe we
observe — why matter couples this way, why propagation is universal,
why geometry responds to energy, why gravity looks spin-2. **Those are
potentially downstream questions.** The more fundamental question:

> **What rules make a universe capable of having those properties at
> all?**

Those are not necessarily the same rules. The theory splits into two
layers:

- **Level I — generative / pre-universe rules (the ontology layer):**
  what has to exist before space, time, particles, fields, energy,
  causality, quantum states, observers, or gravity can be meaningfully
  discussed. The objects the program has been using — locality, state,
  response, memory, composition, distinguishability — may themselves
  need to be derived or reformulated here. Asking for Einstein's
  equation at this level is premature: g_μν, t, x, c, G are not yet
  earned quantities.
- **Level II — emergent universe rules:** given a particular
  universe/state, what effective laws does it obey — quantum mechanics,
  effective spacetime, matter, gravity, cosmological evolution. **This
  is the level GR-2 attacked.**

The crucial possibility: **Level I does not look like physics as we
know it.** It produces the conditions from which Level-II physics
emerges.

**Why GR-2 came back UNDERDETERMINED, re-read at this resolution:** the
campaign tried to derive gravity inside experiments that already
supplied nearly all the gravitational prerequisites — a time parameter,
spatial substrate, propagation, locality, excitations, probes, causal
ordering. It legitimately answered "can gravity emerge *within an
already existing spacetime-like universe*?" (no, not from the earned
core) — it never touched "why does a universe with spacetime-like
behavior exist at all?" The adjudicated dependency boundary {cone |
coupling | spin | reach} is precisely the boundary between what the
supplied substrate gives and what the substrate *is*.

## 2. Three levels of "rules" (kept distinct)

> **rules of a universe** ≠ **rules for universe formation** ≠
> **rules that make any universe possible.**

GR describes: given spacetime and stress-energy → how spacetime
evolves. QM describes: given Hilbert-space structure → how states
evolve. The present open-system core describes: given a local
microscopic ontology → continuum + memory + effective response. None
explains why its own given exists. **The first arrow of any deeper
hierarchy is not assumed temporal** — the underlying rule may be
logically prior rather than temporally prior; time is not smuggled
into the pre-time level. (Operational form: a Level-0 instrument
contains no `t` variable; any ordering must be derived and labeled.)

## 4. The corrected question (standing; the owner's)

Level 0 does **not** ask "why is there something rather than nothing" —
"nothing" is not a physical state unless a theory defines it as one,
and no operational experiment exists for it. The tractable question:

> **What is the minimal structure required for the observed emergence
> class?**

Only after the minimal structure is known may one ask whether it has
any interpretation as a rule for universe formation. Minimality is
always **relative to a declared background mathematics** — every
Level-0 record carries that scope declaration on its face (the GR-2
"tested classes" proviso, one level down).
