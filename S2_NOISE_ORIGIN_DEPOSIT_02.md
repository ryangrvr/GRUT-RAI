## Accepted results

1. **The linear-Gaussian S-1 equivalence survives at its old scope.** Under linear canonical drift,
   ongoing martingale noise is invisible to the frozen mean-response object (Theorem LD).
2. **Nonlinear C-B breaks same-state-space initial-uncertainty equivalence.**
   m₁^𝒮 − m₁^𝒟 = −12βT₁a·t² + O(t³).
3. **Arbitrary heavy tails do not restore it.** HT-B shows that no preparation-independent hidden
   initial law on the same state space reproduces the C-B retained mean-response map (β > 0, T₁ > 0).
4. **An unrestricted deterministic enlargement reproduces C-B exactly.** The standard path-space
   (Wiener-shift skew-product) realization exists by general theorem. It is HB-U: the hidden state
   is the driving path. This is a representation theorem, not an origin theorem.
5. **No declared physical Hamiltonian bath reaches or formulates the C-B discriminator.**
   - O-6 fails P-4.
   - S5-1 C₀ fails P-6.
   - S-1 FKM is general-theorem-only (fails P-7).
   - Sz.-Nagy, Koopman, KvN and the cotangent lift are HB-U or representational.
   - CTP, FDT and KMS records declare no parent.
   - No declared bath reaches even the β = 0 R-2 control class.
6. **Therefore the physical origin of the noise remains unresolved.**

> **The current GRUT record can distinguish ongoing noise from initial uncertainty on the same
> nonlinear state space. It does not yet distinguish fundamental stochasticity from a suitably
> enlarged deterministic physical environment, because no such physical parent has been declared and
> tested.**
