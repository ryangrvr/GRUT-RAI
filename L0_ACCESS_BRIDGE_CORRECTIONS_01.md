**BC-9 · LF-5/LF-6 and ħ (mandatory distinction).**
- **(a)** An abstract noncommutative CCR/CAR lift can be written after choosing a normalization and
  statistics, **without** deriving the physical value of ħ. CAR contains no ħ.
- **(b)** Identifying that algebra with physical quantum mechanics requires the quantum action
  scale, which GRUT has not derived (P-2).
- **The bridge question is whether the *physical* lift is forced.**
- **Groenewold–van Hove** is imported with its hypotheses. It does not bite on the quadratic linear
  core; it bites on mixed p–q terms (the cotangent lift of L0-1c).
- **LF-6:**
  - the real-space bosonic functor is **commutative** (Mehler/OU), and noncommutativity needs a
    **supplied complex structure**;
  - real-space CAR is a Clifford algebra, noncommutative with none needed;
  - **the classical contraction data do not select the statistics** (identical one-particle
    dynamics), which supports NONUNIQUE-LIFT absent further structure;
  - "Output algebra: noncommutative" is **choice-dependent.**
