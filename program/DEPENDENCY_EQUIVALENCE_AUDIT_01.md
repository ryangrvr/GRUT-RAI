    D1 = D2          same construction

## 3 · EDGE RECORD AND STATUSES

Every proposed relationship: `(X, Y, premises, map, inverse?, observable_preserved?, status)`.

**Permitted statuses only.** **EQUIVALENT** — explicit equivalence/invertible map **and** declared
observables preserved. **DEPENDENT** — one determines the other under explicit premises; inverse not
demonstrated. **RELATED** — documented relationship, neither determines the other. **INDEPENDENT** —
the record **demonstrates** one can vary while the other and the physical setup are held fixed.
**UNRESOLVED** — insufficient evidence.

> ### Absence of an equivalence proof is **NOT** evidence of independence.
> Different terminology is **NOT** evidence of independence. Shared use in a calculation is **NOT**
> evidence of dependence. A common unresolved prerequisite does **NOT** make two choices one choice.

**Four distinct failure modes, all reported as UNRESOLVED rather than INDEPENDENT:** objects are not
the same type · the map is undefined · the map exists but does not preserve the observable ·
comparison requires a further unresolved input.
