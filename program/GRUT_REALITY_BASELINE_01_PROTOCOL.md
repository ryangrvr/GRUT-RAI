## The eight categories (owner's; every entry carries a source pointer and a quotation)

A. **Established results** — demonstrated by calculation, proof, experiment, or
   source-level verification. B. **Established constraints** — what surviving models must
   satisfy because of A. C. **Effective/model-level results** — work within a defined
   model, not shown fundamental. D. **Unresolved questions**. E. **Failed
   hypotheses/routes** — explicitly tested and rejected, with the reason. F. **Imported
   physics** — inputs, not derivations. G. **Open physical hypotheses**. H. **Genuine
   predictions** — EMPTY unless a quantitative result satisfies the existing prediction
   gate (`GRUT_PREDICTION_GATE_GAMMA_T.md`; REALITY_CHECK_01: PREDICTED = EMPTY).

**Inflation ban (owner's, binding):** a result is NOT "established" merely because it is
mathematically attractive; reproduces a known result; is compatible with GR; is
compatible with QFT; an AI considers it plausible; it was repeatedly discussed; it appears
in GRUT documentation; or it follows after adding an unstated assumption.
