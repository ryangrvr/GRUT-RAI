## 3. METHOD

**Evidence rule:** use ONLY evidence already in the repository/corpus, unless a document explicitly
points to a derivation already contained there. **Do not supply missing theory from general
knowledge.** Do not silently import standard QFT assumptions.

**For every ordered pair (X, Y)** determine separately: can `X → Y` be demonstrated? can `Y → X`?
what premises are required? does either direction leave residual freedom?

**Then classify** — and `UNRESOLVED` is a **first-class result**, not a failure to answer:

- **EQUIVALENT** — an explicit derivation/proof that the two represent the same structure under stated premises.
- **DEPENDENT** — one determines the other under explicitly stated premises; not full equivalence.
- **RELATED** — a documented relationship, but neither determines the other.
- **INDEPENDENT** — the record **demonstrates** one can be varied while the other is held fixed, both objects remaining well-defined.
- **UNRESOLVED** — evidence insufficient for anything stronger.

**The co-determination test** (the criterion that killed H). X and Y are *the same decision* only if,
holding all other declared inputs fixed: **(i)** declaring X fixes Y with **no residual freedom**,
**AND** **(ii)** declaring Y fixes X with no residual freedom. If either direction leaves residual
freedom, classify **no stronger than DEPENDENT/RELATED**.
