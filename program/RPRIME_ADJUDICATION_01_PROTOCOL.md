## The six items the adjudication must address (owner's list, verbatim in substance)

1. **Local gauge status** — already established/strongly supported by Wall-A; restate,
   do not re-derive.
2. **Global behavior** — ζ ~ x, so the transformation is not automatically trivial on
   the slice-wide problem; characterize exactly what "not trivial" means for the R³
   continuum the contract uses.
3. **Boundary condition** — what exact asymptotic condition does the Tier-3 problem
   impose? Does it identify configurations related by this transformation? (Expected
   on the sources: none is declared spatially — the adjudication must confirm or
   refute that by reading, and say what the mode-normalization and measure
   declarations *implicitly* assume about the slice.)
4. **Relation to Declaration 5** — may its coincidence prescription be adopted as a
   new contract-scope boundary prescription for the *TT-bath* residual family, or is
   that an impermissible transfer from the matter-side construction (K_R charter
   STEP 10: only *"VALIDATED MACHINERY"* and the sign dictionary carry)? Note the D2
   ruling already runs Declaration 5 *"verbatim as frozen (unfixed vs synchronous)"* at
   contract scope for the *synchronous* family — is the TT-bath residual family the
   same family, a subfamily, or a different one? (Answer by construction, not by name.)
5. **Observable meaning** — if the mode is quotiented, what observable equivalence does
   that imply (which pairs of configurations are declared indistinguishable, for which
   class of observables — patch-localized? slice-wide?)? If retained, why is a locally
   pure-gauge mode physically distinguishable under the declared conditions (what
   observable distinguishes it — the O(k²)/tidal gradient content? correlation with
   short modes?)? Standard physics on large diffeomorphisms / adiabatic tensor modes
   may be CITED for orientation (Weinberg-type adiabatic modes; soft-graviton
   consistency relations) but NO conclusion may be imported from it — the contract's
   own declarations decide.
6. **No IR calculation** — the repaired loop is not computed; no small-q class is
   read; no statement about finiteness under R′ is made.

## Pre-registered outcomes (owner's)

- **R′_ADOPTED** — the Tier-3 boundary conditions *independently* establish the linear
  mode as gauge-equivalent (the conditions must be found ON THE RECORD, not supplied).
- **R′_REJECTED** — the mode is retained as physical/boundary data under the Tier-3
  problem as declared.
- **R′_CONDITIONALLY_ADOPTED** — quotienting is valid only after an explicitly declared
  additional boundary prescription (named precisely; its price stated; whether it
  relocates an assumption stated).
- **R′_UNRESOLVED** — the contract does not contain enough information to decide (the
  missing declaration named precisely).

**Standing:** R′_ADOPTED ⇏ IR finite. R′_REJECTED ⇒ the divergence is retained as
physical under the declared contract and GRUT takes the hit — this outcome is fully
available and not disfavored.

## Anti-optimization

R′_ADOPTED is the framework-favorable trap ("it's a diffeomorphism ⇒ quotient it ⇒ the
divergence disappears" — the owner named this the rescue to prevent). R′_UNRESOLVED is
the deference-comfortable outcome and must be demonstrated by an exhaustive read of the
declarations, not assumed. R′_REJECTED is deflation-comfortable. None preferred. The
adjudication is about what the CONTRACT declares and implies, not about what would be
convenient for any later computation.
