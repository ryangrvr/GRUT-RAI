## Principal open arrow (one sentence each)

- **GRUT:** The declared construction choices (system/bath, projection P, cutoff/separation, gauge prescription, response object) do not chain to a closed dynamical construction.
- **String/M:** Consistent construction does not select a unique physical vacuum (landscape/compactification ambiguity).
- **AdS/CFT:** The duality is established for AdS-type backgrounds; the bridge to our de Sitter-like cosmology is not established.
- **Asymptotic Safety:** The UV fixed point does not, at current truncation level, uniquely fix the full observed matter sector.
- **LQG:** The quantum-geometric representation does not yet chain to 4D dynamics and GR recovery — the Hamiltonian constraint "remains illusive despite a large amount of effort" (Ashtekar–Lewandowski, gr-qc/0404018).

## Cross-candidate pattern test

**Finding:** the same unresolved-arrow *pattern* appears in unrelated frameworks, but at **different interfaces** — not a shared one. GRUT stops at construction-choice closure; String/M at vacuum selection; AdS/CFT at the de Sitter bridge; Asymptotic Safety at matter-sector uniqueness; LQG at 4D dynamics. The principal open arrows do not coincide.

**Not interpreted as:** "all frameworks are equal" or "GRUT is vindicated." The observation is: different quantum-gravity programs fail to close at different interfaces between formal construction and physical realization.
