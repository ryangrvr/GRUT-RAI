and interface-specific:

  **dynamical** arrow open — quoted directly from Ashtekar–Lewandowski.

  mapping, unique prediction) because its seven construction choices are declared

Asymmetry, not symmetry, is the strongest sourced result:

1. **GRUT is uniquely worse on closure than every other candidate** on the audited
   record: it is the only candidate with *three* UNRESOLVED rows and zero CLOSED rows
   on the audited primary-source record. It does not close an arrow that any other
   candidate leaves open.

2. **AdS/CFT is the only candidate with a CLOSED observable-mapping arrow** on the
   audited record — within its established AdS regime. It does not, on this record,
   extend to a cosmological statement.

3. **Asymptotic Safety is the only candidate producing a PARTIAL unique-prediction
   status** (fixed-point signatures). No other candidate achieves that on the audited
   record.

4. **String/M and LQG are symmetric in a specific sense:** both close the
   quantum/kinematical layers cleanly, and both stall at the same class of arrow
   (unique physical realization). But they stall at *different* points — String/M at
   vacuum choice, LQG at the Hamiltonian constraint.
