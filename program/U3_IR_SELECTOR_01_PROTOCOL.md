## Pre-registered outcomes

- **UNIQUE-SELECTOR** — exactly one completion survives S1–S7, with the excluding
  argument exhibited per excluded candidate. Consequence: compute A₀ and the ω⁰ slot
  under it; state the observable consequences (η's class, χ_static's IR segment, the
  k = 0 thermality) as CONDITIONAL on the selector's premises being themselves
  derived-or-declared; owner ratifies any register consequence.
- **PARTIAL-SELECTOR** — the selectors exclude some candidates but ≥ 2 survive; the
  residual freedom is named precisely (which selector would decide, and whether it is
  computable in-corpus).
- **NO-SELECTOR** — every consistency condition is satisfiable by ≥ 2 candidates and the
  microscopic dynamics is silent; the fork is preserved as an independent physical
  input, its price stated, its observable spread computed (the "ask nature" route:
  which measurement distinguishes the survivors).
- **SELECTOR-EXCLUDES-ALL** — no corpus completion survives; the white-floor arm (3) is
  thereby *forced* rather than chosen — recorded as the framework needing reformulation
  at O(H²).

## Anti-optimization

framework-favorable AND aesthetically-favorable direction — the owner named this trap
explicitly ("Aha! the covariant prescription is obviously right"). NO-SELECTOR is the
deflation-comfortable direction. SELECTOR-EXCLUDES-ALL is the drama-favorable
direction. None preferred. **A selector counts only if its excluding argument is
exhibited as a computation or a theorem on the completed object; "candidate X is
uglier" is not a selector; "candidate X is what U2 found convenient" is not a selector.**
