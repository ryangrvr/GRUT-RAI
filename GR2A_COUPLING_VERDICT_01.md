   members, the measured class equals the frozen coefficient law:
   membership of the ω⁷ class is decided by $c_1=0$ and $c_2+c_3=0$ — the
   coupling's soft amplitude vanishing on the cone — and by nothing else.

   structure, which the record grades **SUPPLIED** (CC-1: conservation not
   derived, reduced to the supplied massless probe; CP-1: the Weyl
   selection conditional on supplied structure).
3. **The +4 locus is strictly bigger than the stress tensor.** The pure
   higher-derivative potential (member 6), the **purely kinetic** gradient
   coupling (member 7 — no tracelessness narrative available), and mixed
   members 8 and 14 all land in class 8. Reaching ω⁷ does not certify
   stress-tensor physics; this re-demonstrates CP-1's plane in a broader
   family.
4. **The locus is unstable in exactly the earned-admissible direction.**
   Adding 0.3 × mass modulation — the coupling CC-1 found passing *every*

   8 to slope 0.0015 (class 0, four *below* base). The couplings the
   earned structure most cleanly admits are the ones that destroy the
   cancellation.
5. **Cancellation depth is purchasable by tuning.** Member 9 reaches
