## 1 · THE RRT-0 NO-GO, AND EXACTLY WHICH ASSUMPTIONS CAUSE IT

Closed dynamics ρ_{t+1} = UρU†; intervention Δρ(τ) = U^τ[E(ρ)−ρ]U^{−τ} (canonical Option-1
E, propagation separate). Verdict: `FULLY_REDUCIBLE_IN_LINEAR_UNITARY_MODEL_CLASS`,
Δ_residual ≡ 0.

The reducibility is forced by a **conjunction of four properties**, and it is important that
no single one of them alone is the culprit:

- **(R1) LINEARITY** of the update in ρ — Δ propagates as an independent linear image.
- **(R2) INVERTIBILITY** of the update (U unitary ⇒ U^{−τ} exists) — the intervention can be
  *exactly un-propagated*, so the response carries no information the map U and the supplied
  E did not already contain.
- **(R3) EXTERNALLY SUPPLIED, ρ-INDEPENDENT E** — the intervention is a fixed affine map, not
  generated by the dynamics.
- **(R4) FIXED READOUT** in a fixed operator basis.

**The load-bearing one is R2 (invertibility), not "unitarity" per se.** A linear *invertible*
non-unitary update would reduce identically the same way (Δ = M^τ[E(ρ)−ρ]M^{−τ} for any
invertible M). This is the audit's first correction to the naive framing: **"nonunitary" does
NOT imply "irreducible."** The route to irreducibility runs through loss of invertibility of
the update *as it acts on the accessible operator data* — equivalently, through the update
being a genuinely non-invertible (information-losing) map on the observed algebra.

## 2 · CANDIDATE EXTENSIONS

For each: RRT-0 assumption removed · new structure supplied · arrow built in? · is stable
organization merely inherited? · can reducibility be re-derived cleanly? · advantages · risk ·
cost · RRT-1-suitable?

**A. CPTP / Lindblad semigroup 𝒯_t = e^{t𝓛}.**
Removes R2 (generically non-invertible for t>0). Supplies: a generator 𝓛 = −i[H,·] +
Σ(L_aρL_a† − ½{L_a†L_a,ρ}), i.e. the operators {L_a}, an implied environment, a Markov
assumption, and a semigroup *direction*. Arrow built in? **YES** — the semigroup is one-sided
(t≥0) by construction; entropy is monotone. Inherited organization? **YES, and this is the
danger**: the fixed-point algebra Fix(𝓛) and the decoherence-free subspaces are determined by
{H, L_a} alone (Baumgartner–Narnhofer; Lindblad structure theory). Reducibility re-derivable?
Cleanly, and it will likely say "reduces to the {L_a}-generated structure" (see §7).
Advantages: maximally controlled, complete theory. Risk: **it supplies a very powerful
structure — a preferred basis, sectors, and a direction — so "emergence" is nearly guaranteed
to be restatement.** Cost: cheap (d=4). Suitable? Usable, but NOT to be adopted by default (§8).

**B. General quantum-channel iteration ρ_{n+1} = Φ(ρ_n), Φ CPTP.**
Removes R2 (Φ generically non-invertible) WITHOUT supplying a continuous generator or a
Markovian semigroup — the arrow is only the discrete iteration order. Supplies: one channel Φ
and its Kraus set {K_i}. Arrow built in? **WEAKER** — a discrete non-invertible step has no
continuous-time direction and no imposed entropy monotone (a channel can be unital/bistochastic
with no relaxation). Inherited organization? The asymptotics reduce to the **peripheral
spectrum** and fixed points of Φ (Wolf–Cirac; the structure of the "phase" subspace) — supplied,
but a *smaller* supplied object than Lindblad's full {L_a} algebra. Reducibility re-derivable?
Yes, against Fix(Φ) / the peripheral automorphism. Advantages: **removes invertibility with the
fewest new ingredients** — closest to "minimal escape." Risk: lower. Cost: cheap. Suitable?
**Strong candidate — the minimal invertibility-breaking extension.**

**C. Open-system / influence-functional (environment retained, not replaced by a generator).**
Removes R2 *and* the closed-system assumption, but keeps the total dynamics UNITARY on
system+environment; irreversibility appears only after tracing out E. Supplies: an
environment Hilbert space, a system–environment coupling, and — critically — an **initial
system/environment split and a factorized initial state** (the observer/world choice the RAI
line already flagged). Arrow built in? Only through the initial-condition choice (the Past
Hypothesis in miniature) — NOT through the dynamics. Inherited organization? The reduced
dynamics is exactly as organized as the coupling and the split make it. Reducibility
re-derivable? Yes, but now against the *supplied split*, which reintroduces exactly the u3
question. Advantages: honest about where irreversibility comes from (a state choice, not a
law) — this is the most RAI-consonant option. Risk: the split is a heavy declared input; and
this is nearly the RRT-0→RAI residue in disguise. Cost: higher (environment dimension). Suitable?
Valuable as the *conceptual* control, expensive as the primary instrument.

**D. Coarse-grained relational dynamics (irreversibility from a declared reduction map).**
Removes R2 by composing unitary evolution with a fixed coarse-graining projection 𝒫 (a
conditional expectation onto a subalgebra). Supplies: the subalgebra / projection 𝒫 itself.
Arrow built in? Through the repeated application of a non-invertible 𝒫. Inherited organization?
**MAXIMALLY** — 𝒫 *is* the decomposition; discovering "the subalgebra 𝒫 projects onto" is
pure tautology. This is the Mori–Zwanzig setting the RAI campaign already showed is
identity-not-derivation. Reducibility re-derivable? Trivially reduces to 𝒫. Advantages: none
beyond illustrating the trap. Risk: **circular by construction.** Suitable? **NO** — pre-inserts
the answer.

**E. Minimal invertibility-breaking WITHOUT a preferred-structure supply.**
The class-defining question this audit raises: is there an extension that breaks R2 while
supplying *neither* a preferred basis (as Lindblad's L_a do) *nor* a preferred subalgebra (as
D's 𝒫 does)? Candidate: a **random/generic CPTP channel** (Kraus operators drawn from a Haar
or GUE-adjacent ensemble, analogous to RRT-0's own GUE H) — non-invertible, but with no
*designed* fixed-point structure. Arrow built in? Discrete-order only. Inherited organization?
This is precisely what would be *tested* rather than supplied — the generic-channel analogue of
"does the frozen family have any structure," now for the fixed-point/peripheral algebra.
This is option B with the sharpest possible honesty setting, and it is the recommendation (§8).

## 3 · THE MINIMUM ESCAPE CONDITION

Distinguishing the candidate properties, with logical relations stated:

- `NONUNITARITY` — **NOT sufficient** (a linear invertible non-unitary map still reduces, §1).
- `NONREVERSIBILITY` / **LOSS OF INVERTIBILITY on the accessible algebra** — **THIS is the
  minimal escape condition.** It is exactly the negation of R2. Everything below is either a
  way of achieving it or an extra ingredient beyond it.
- `CONTRACTION` (‖Φ(ρ)−Φ(σ)‖ ≤ ‖ρ−σ‖, strict on some subspace) — a *sufficient* route to
  non-invertibility, not equivalent: a channel can be non-invertible without being a strict
  contraction, and unital channels contract distinguishability without a unique fixed point.
- `SEMIGROUP PROPERTY` — **independent and NOT required**: it adds continuous-time structure
  and a generator, i.e. *more* supplied structure. Escaping the no-go does not need it (option B).
- `ENVIRONMENTAL ELIMINATION` — a *mechanism* for non-invertibility (partial trace is
  non-invertible), correlated with it but carrying the extra baggage of a split (option C).
- `COARSE-GRAINING` — another mechanism (a conditional expectation is idempotent, hence
  non-invertible), but it supplies the decomposition outright (option D).
- `FIXED-POINT STRUCTURE` — a *consequence* to be analyzed, not an input to escape; every CPTP
  map has one.
- `LOSS OF INFORMATION ACCESS` — **logically equivalent to loss of invertibility on the
  observed algebra** (an invertible map on the accessible data loses no accessible information).
  This is the same condition as NONREVERSIBILITY, stated information-theoretically.

**Verdict: the minimum structural property absent from RRT-0 and required to escape its no-go
is non-invertibility of the update as it acts on the accessible operator data — nothing
smaller, and in particular NOT the semigroup/dissipation machinery, which is one specific
(heavily-structured) way of achieving it.**

## 4 · THE RRT-1 IRREDUCIBILITY QUESTION (made precise)

Not "does it relax?" (a supplied generator relaxes by construction). The strongest defensible
residual question:

> **After subtracting all organization explicitly encoded by the supplied irreversible
> mechanism, does a stable relational structure remain?**

**"Subtracting" made precise.** Let the supplied structure be the algebra
𝓐_supplied generated by the mechanism: for Lindblad, 𝓐 = the algebra generated by
{H, L_a, L_a†} together with its induced Fix(𝓛) and decoherence-free decomposition; for a
channel Φ, the fixed-point algebra Fix(Φ) plus the peripheral (phase) automorphism algebra.
The RRT-1 statistic must be evaluated on the **quotient / complement** of 𝓐_supplied — the
observable organization NOT in the commutant, fixed-point set, or peripheral algebra of the
supplied channel. Concretely: any candidate sector, attractor, or preferred operator structure
is admissible as "residual" **only if it is not a function of 𝓐_supplied** — tested by the
same deletion logic RRT-0 used (remove the mechanism's own algebra from the inputs; if the
structure survives, it is a candidate; if it collapses, it was supplied).

| Hamiltonian H | `DECLARED_INPUT` | reversible part | no arrow by itself |
| irreversible mechanism (L_a / Kraus set / Φ) | `DECLARED_INPUT` | breaks invertibility | **may encode a preferred basis, sectors, and direction** |
| environment / coarse-graining reading | `UNRESOLVED` unless a microscopic model is supplied | meaning of the non-invertibility | must NOT be silently identified with "the universe" |

**Firewall (Section 5 of the order): a supplied one-sided update direction may never be
reported as an emergent arrow of time.** Every row labeled `DECLARED_INPUT` above is barred
from appearing in any RRT-1 positive claim as `DERIVED`.

## 6 · RRT-1 MIRROR DESIGN (before any experiment)

The RRT-0 mirror (antiunitary conjugation, bit-exact PASS) **does not automatically transfer** —
that PASS was a theorem of the *unitary* class. For RRT-1 the mirror must act on the mechanism:
- **Mirrored:** H → H*, and each Kraus/Lindblad operator L_a → L_a* (equivalently the channel
  Φ → Φ̄, its complex-conjugate channel), states and readout conjugated as in RRT-0.
- **Must remain invariant:** any statistic that is genuinely orientation-blind — and *whether
  such invariance holds is now a question, not a theorem*, because Φ and its time-reverse
  Φ† (the dual/adjoint channel) are generically different maps. This is the sharp new feature:
  in RRT-0 the mirror was forced invariant; in RRT-1 a difference between Φ and its conjugate/
  dual is the candidate genuine orientation-sensitivity.
- **Allowed to transform:** cluster labels (relabeling), and any quantity carrying an explicit
  covariant index.
- **Pipeline defect indicator:** a statistic that SHOULD be conjugation-invariant (built from
  |Tr[...]| of conjugation-covariant inputs, as in RRT-0) differing beyond tolerance — same
  diagnostic as the RRT-0 control.
- **Genuine-structure indicator:** an orientation-sensitive statistic that (i) distinguishes Φ
  from Φ̄/Φ†, (ii) survives matched random-channel nulls, and (iii) is NOT a function of
  𝓐_supplied. Only that conjunction could be a non-trivial RRT-1 result — and it must clear
  the §4 subtraction first.

## 7 · FIRST THEOREM BEFORE FIRST SIMULATION (the RRT-1 reducibility gate)

Cheapest analytic/no-go test, to run before any numerics:

> **Does every asymptotic partition, attractor, and preferred operator structure of the
> supplied irreversible map reduce to Fix(𝒯) (the fixed-point algebra), the peripheral/phase
> algebra, and the decoherence structure generated by {L_a}/Comm{L_a, L_a†}?**

This is the exact non-unitary analogue of the RRT-0 reducibility gate, and the operator-algebra
theory says the answer is **generically YES**: for a faithful (relaxing) Lindbladian the
asymptotics are governed entirely by Fix(𝓛), which Baumgartner–Narnhofer decompose explicitly
from {H, L_a}; for a general channel, by the peripheral spectrum and Fix(Φ) (Wolf–Cirac). So the
predicted RRT-1 first-gate outcome, **stated as an expectation, not hard-coded**, is:
`ASYMPTOTIC_STRUCTURE_REDUCES_TO_SUPPLIED_ALGEBRA` for the generic/faithful case — RRT-1 would
then have demonstrated organization *encoded in its declared mechanism*, not emergent. **A
NON-reducible residual can only appear in a non-generic regime** (degenerate peripheral
spectrum, non-faithful/decoherence-free sectors, or transient — pre-asymptotic — organization
not captured by the fixed-point algebra). **Isolating exactly that regime, with matched
controls, is the only place RRT-1 could produce a non-trivial positive result** — and it is
where the design should aim, rather than at generic relaxation.

## 8 · RECOMMENDED CLASS

**`RRT-1-GENERAL-CHANNEL`** (option B / its generic-Kraus sharpening E) — **not Lindblad by
default.** Justification on the order's stated criterion (minimum new assumptions × maximum
falsifiability, not convenience):
- it breaks the *exact* minimal escape condition (invertibility, §3) with the *fewest* new
  inputs — one channel Φ, no continuous generator, no imposed entropy monotone, no environment
  split, no coarse-graining projection;
- it therefore supplies a *smaller* 𝓐_supplied to subtract (Fix(Φ) + peripheral, not a full
  {L_a} algebra), so a surviving residual is harder to dismiss as inherited;
- its mirror question (Φ vs Φ̄/Φ†) is genuinely open rather than theorem-forced (§6);
- Lindblad remains available as a **controlled sub-case** (𝒯_1 = e^𝓛 is one particular Φ)
  and open-system (C) remains the conceptual honesty-control — but adopting Lindblad as the
  ontology would supply the most structure and make emergence hardest to distinguish from
  restatement, which is exactly the trap RRT-0 taught us to avoid.

Cost estimate: identical class to RRT-0 (d=4 density matrices, Kraus sums) — microseconds per
condition; the first-theorem gate (§7) is pure linear algebra on 𝒯's fixed points, cheaper
than the reducibility gate already run.

## 9 · WHAT RRT-1 CANNOT CLAIM (carried forward, binding)

No arrow of time derived from a supplied one-sided update; no emergence of any structure that
is a function of 𝓐_supplied; no physical dissipation, decoherence-as-measurement, geometry,
locality, observer, or 1Space; no transfer of RRT-0's reducibility PASS or mirror PASS as if
they held in the new class (both must be re-derived); nothing about the RAI half-line/KMS
residue (still inexpressible — RRT-1's finite type-I channels do not host type III₁/hsm
structure any more than RRT-0 did). The maximum permitted positive result remains
MODEL-CONDITIONAL and only if a residual survives §4 subtraction, §6 mirror, §7 first-gate, and
matched null controls.

## HANDOFF SUMMARY

- RRT-1 admits an invertibility-breaking (channel) structure that RRT-0 did not contain.
- That structure is initially a **declared input**, not a derived arrow of time.
- The minimal escape condition is **loss of invertibility on the accessible algebra** — not
  dissipation, not a semigroup, and NOT merely non-unitarity.
- The first RRT-1 question is whether **any stable organization survives after all
  fixed-point, peripheral, and supplied-channel structure has been subtracted** — and the
  operator-algebra prior says that for the generic/faithful case it does not, so RRT-1 must
  aim at the non-generic/transient regime or return a clean negative.
- Recommended class: **RRT-1-GENERAL-CHANNEL**, Lindblad as a controlled sub-case only.
