# L0-1 FLOOR PROGRAM — TERMINATION CONDITION (DRAFT FOR OWNER ADOPTION)

**Status: DRAFT. NOT IN FORCE.** This condition takes effect only when
the owner adopts it by a recorded ruling. It was drafted to the shape
the owner specified (`L0_1_FLOOR_REGISTRY_RULINGS_01.md`, last
section): structural, not "until GRUT succeeds"; a finite set of
obligations, each ending as **discharged, falsified, class-split, or
unformulable with a documented reason**. Every clause below is a
proposal. The owner may strike or change any of them.

## T0 · What this governs

It governs when the **floor program** (D-HERM, D-DET, D-ORD) is
complete. It does not govern, limit, or block the broader Level-0
exploration, the autonomous exploratory phase, or anything outside
the obligation list. Its only job is to separate *a finished floor*
from *an agenda that can be extended forever*.

## T2 · The terminal labels (exactly four, the owner's)

Each obligation ends in **exactly one** of these, assigned by owner
ruling:

- **DISCHARGED:** formulated, batteries mapped, and certified lines
  recorded at a declared scope.
- **FALSIFIED:** the obligation's frozen hypothesis failed a clean
  gate. This is a completion, not a failure.
- **CLASS-SPLIT:** the property holds in one declared subclass and
  fails in another, both certified (e.g. trees vs cycle-bearing
  substrates).
- **UNFORMULABLE-WITH-REASON:** a documented reason is recorded,
  either an impossibility theorem (cited) or the specific
  presupposition that no available battery can avoid, named. "Not
  yet attempted" is never a reason.

HALT, VACUOUS, and PARTIAL are **non-terminal** run outcomes.

## T3 · The retry budget (what makes the floor finite in practice)

Each chartered obligation gets **at most one re-charter** after a
non-terminal outcome. A second non-terminal outcome ends the
obligation as UNFORMULABLE-WITH-REASON, with the recorded instrument
limitation as the reason, unless the owner rules CLASS-SPLIT from the
record. With six obligations needing runs or documents (O-1 … O-6),
the floor is bounded at **at most ten chartered runs** (five run
obligations, two attempts each), **one theorem document** (O-5), and
**one synthesis** (O-7).

## T4 · The list is closed

No obligation may be added to the floor after adoption. Questions the
floor raises go on a recorded **successor list**. They are preserved,
not executed under floor authority. The owner may change T1 only by an
explicit recorded ruling that states it is a scope change.

## T5 · Completion

The floor program is **COMPLETE** when O-1 … O-6 each carry a terminal
label accepted by owner ruling, and the O-7 synthesis is recorded and
ruled on. At completion, the floor ledger is filed as a deposit, like
the v4 deposit: one document, the terminal label for each obligation,
and the successor list.

## T6 · Independence from success

Nothing in this condition refers to GRUT succeeding. FALSIFIED and
UNFORMULABLE-WITH-REASON complete an obligation exactly as DISCHARGED
does.

## T7 · Execution rules (carried from v4 R2/R4)
