## Q1 — What has been calculated on the one-loop graviton self-energy in dS?

A mature literature. Tsamis & Woodard computed the one-loop graviton
self-energy on a locally de Sitter background in 1996 (hep-ph/9602317; PRD 54,
2621), with the flat-space limit agreeing with Capper's gauge-matched result.
Tan, Tsamis & Woodard (arXiv:2103.08547; CQG 38, 145024) computed the graviton
self-energy *from gravitons* in cosmology — the same diagram class as the
present contract, and already cited inside the program's own contract
documents as the declared source of the Tier-3 object. Leonard & Woodard-type
representation work (arXiv:1403.0896; PRD 90, 024032) develops the structure-
function representation (four functions for matter loops, nine for graviton
loops, on the cosmological patch). Scalar-loop contributions during inflation:
arXiv:1101.5804. Recent gauge work continues (e.g. arXiv:2511.13660, JHEP
2026: dS graviton propagator in a one-parameter gauge).
**Positioning consequence: the existence and computation of this object is
NOT novel and must not be implied to be. The manuscript's §2 must cite TW
1996 and TTW 2021 as the established line its declared contract instantiates.**

## Q2 — Absorptive/imaginary self-energy information in existing papers?

Yes, as a category. Spectral-function work exists for gravitons (e.g.
arXiv:2507.22169, Lorentzian quantum gravity, spectral function from the
imaginary part of the retarded propagator), and absorptive phenomena on dS are
an established subject: Bros–Epstein–Moschella proved particle decay
processes forbidden in flat space occur on dS (Ann. Henri Poincaré 11 (2010);
arXiv:0901.4223; the lifetime papers), and Boyanovsky et al. developed
dynamical-renormalization-group treatments of relaxation and decay in dS,
including nonstationary self-energy effects. Perturbative (in)stability of dS
QFT: JHEP 05 (2011) 047.
**Positioning consequence: "absorptive response on dS" as a phenomenon is
established; the manuscript's contribution must be framed at the level of
the specific certified coefficient structure and its representation tests.**

## Q3 — Has Wigner/average-time dependence of this kind been derived before?

The Wigner two-time method (center time / relative time; stationarity ⟺
T-independence) is completely standard in nonequilibrium QFT (Kadanoff–Baym;
kinetic theory, e.g. arXiv:1910.10979, arXiv:1108.3688) and has cosmological
applications in transport contexts. This pass found **no** application of the
Wigner-resolved presentation to the dS graviton self-energy's absorptive part
with H-graded (u_b, ω) coefficients. The adjacent dS work (TTW; the
effective-field-equation line) is position-space/structure-function based.
**Status: method standard; this application not found in this pass —
requires the deeper pass before any claim.**

## Q5 — Is the stationary-dressing no-go new, or an existing field-redefinition argument?

screening computations suffer gauge artifacts (arXiv:0708.1471-line) versus
Tsamis & Woodard's reply (arXiv:0708.2004; PRD 78, 028501), and the
Tanaka–Urakawa gauge-invariance/IR-regularity program (e.g. arXiv:1402.2076)

artifacts. That is *related but not the same statement* as the manuscript's
certificate, which is an intra-contract, coefficient-level inconsistency

from non-analyticity. This pass found no existing argument of that specific
form — an internal search result only, NOT evidence of absence: the paper
must never convert this into "no existing argument exists." An equivalent
argument could be expressed in entirely different terminology
(field-redefinition invariance, wave-function renormalization,
time-coordinate redefinition, adiabatic basis transformations, secular-log
resummation, slicing transformations, structure-function representations,
spectral/absorptive invariants, retarded kernel stationarity, average-time
dependence) — Pass 02's Q5 searches exactly that space. **However, the manuscript MUST engage this debate in positioning: its
own limitations (R′ unresolved; temporal-only boundary declarations; the
declared-contract conditionality) are exactly where a Garriga–Tanaka-type
critique would press. Recommended: a short related-work paragraph

secular effects is contested, and situating the no-go as a statement about
the declared object.**
