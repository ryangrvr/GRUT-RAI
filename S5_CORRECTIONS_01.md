## SC5-1. ≃_G quotients positive clock rescalings of the generator

**What §1 said:**

> "a map that changes the K-functional counts as **a different class on the same K**"

That wording also caught an overall positive scalar rescaling of the generator. Such a rescaling is
a change of clock unit, not a different kind of time evolution.

**Amended ≃_G:** in addition to the §1 intertwining maps, ≃_G quotients an overall **positive scalar
rescaling of the generator / evolution parameter**:

  𝒜₂ = c𝒜₁, c > 0, with the flows related only by t′ = ct,

provided no dimensionless structural invariant or observable class changes.

**Precisions (owner wording):**
- **K is not freely rescaled.** The quotient acts on the generator's overall rate (the clock), not
  on the static substrate.
- **Examples:**
  - the CA-1 twin phonon (only an overall frequency scale differs) stays in G-W;
  - −K and −γK (γ > 0) are the same first-order generator kind.
- **Still NOT quotiented:**
  - a nonlinear functional change K ↦ f(K) (for example K vs √K);
  - added noise;
  - added phase-space variables;
  - complexification;
  - statistics;
  - **a sign flip (c < 0)**, which reverses the orientation that O-5 identifies as supplied
    generator data.

**Effect:** none on the S5-0 terminal (GENERATOR-IRREDUCIBLE/SUPPLIED). No elimination in §5
depended on the old wording.
