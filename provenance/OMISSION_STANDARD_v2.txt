================================ THE STANDARD (unchanged from v1) ================================

    A proposition is an OMISSION if some banked node's discharge PRESUPPOSES it -- you cannot
    discharge that node without it -- and it is NOT ITSELF BOOKED.

CONJUNCT 1 is DISCHARGE-BLOCKING: suppose the candidate false or absent -- can the node's content
  still be "ESTABLISHED, STATED, AND USED"? *** ALL THREE VERBS. See R4. *** A presupposition is
  typically INVISIBLE in the node's text; if it APPEARS in the statement it is a SPLIT question.
CONJUNCT 2: the proposition is not itself booked -- see R3 for the scope of "booked".
THE MEMBERSHIP FENCE: a candidate counts only if a NAMED POSITION could deny it AND the denial would
  change something the cluster says. See R1 -- the fence does more work than v1 gave it credit for.
THE MULTIPLICITY SIGNAL: presupposed by several nodes and booked by none is the highest-confidence
  shape. See R5 -- the register must be able to COMPUTE this, not merely assert it in prose.

================================ R1 -- THE FENCE DECIDES; DO NOT ADD A CONVENTION ================
A sweep proposed a new convention to settle whether a MEASURED node's discharge is indexed to its
NUMBER or to its DECLARED PIPELINE. *** THAT DICHOTOMY IS REFUSED. *** Both horns are bad:
value-indexed hides model-dependence; pipeline-indexed opens every prior in a likelihood and blows
the volume guard. The standard's own device already decides, and gradedly:

    AN INPUT IS SOMETHING A NAMED POSITION DENIES.

  * FLATNESS PASSES -- non-flat cosmologies are named positions AND the denial changes what the
    cluster says. vc_flatness_in_reduction stands as banked.
  * A GENERIC INSTRUMENTAL CALIBRATION FAILS -- nobody denies it. It is a RIDER on the value's
    scheme_tag, not a node.
  * Most priors in a likelihood have no named denier, so the inventory does not explode.
A candidate is tested against the fence SPECIFICALLY, on the physics, and never ruled dead by fiat
because a convention would have been tidier.

================================ R2 -- EVERY RAISED CANDIDATE GETS A DISPOSITION ==================
*** "ABSORBED INTO A COMPOUND" IS A DISPOSITION, NOT A REASON TO DROP. ***
Mechanized in sweep_ledger.py and pinned in test_sweep_ledger.py: raw candidates raised must equal
the sum over dispositions, absorption must POINT at a canonical that itself carries a terminal
disposition, and a sweep must additionally answer for the candidates ITS OWN PRE-REGISTRATION named
("the sweep did not reach it" is a finding about the sweep and must be stated). A remembered rule is
not a rule; this one is now a broken build.

================================ R3 -- CONJUNCT 2 IS PER-CLUSTER =================================
"Booked" means booked in THIS CLUSTER'S inventory. An input shared across clusters is booked in
each, and that is not a double-count. Two reasons, both structural rather than convenient:
  (i)  WE NEVER SUM ACROSS CLUSTERS -- already ruled, and reaffirmed by the w=-1 edge note. There
       is no global total for a repeated booking to corrupt.
  (ii) THE MEMBERSHIP FENCE IS CLUSTER-INDEXED, so a proposition deniable in one cluster and not in
       another is LEGITIMATELY an input in one and not the other. Worked instance: the
       individual-vs-ensemble reading of the expectation value was DROPPED by the fence in the
       vacuum cluster (Ballentine/QBism change no vacuum-cluster statement) and PASSES it in
       quantum foundations, where the ensemble reading is a rival account of outcome-selection.
       *** A DROP LIST IS NEVER INHERITED ACROSS CLUSTERS. Re-run the fence every time. ***
Per-cluster is correct throughout, not only for conjunct 2.

================================ R4 -- ATOMISE BEFORE APPLYING THE CONJUNCTS =====================
*** CONJUNCT 2 IS NOT DISTRIBUTIVE. *** A COMPOUND candidate can pass it AS A WHOLE while containing
a part that is already booked. That is the mirror of the failure conjunct 2 exists to prevent,
smuggled in as a conjunct -- and it happened on the first run: a candidate carrying "one Omega_k",
a straight re-book of vc_flatness_in_reduction, passed conjunct 2 because the candidate was tested
whole. Atomise FIRST, then apply both conjuncts to each part. Never "note the atomicity debt on
entry" and ship the conjunction.
NOTE THE SHAPE OF THIS FIX: it moves the count in BOTH directions, which is what distinguishes a
repair from a tightening.

================================ R5 -- WIRING IS PART OF BANKING AN OMISSION =====================
When an omission is banked, THE NODES THAT PRESUPPOSE IT MUST GAIN depends_on EDGES TO IT. The
standard leans on multiplicity and the register could not compute it: of 20 vacuum-cluster nodes,
ONE had a non-empty depends_on, and all three omissions were banked with none -- including
vc_flatness_in_reduction, which was FOUND AS A LINK IN vc_rho_lambda's CHAIN while vc_rho_lambda
kept depends_on: []. *** THE REGISTER FIXED AN INSTANCE OF EDGE-BLINDNESS WITHOUT RECORDING THE
EDGE. *** Unwired, the signal stays prose and is re-derived from scratch every wave -- which is
exactly how detection went blind to S1. Pinned: no omission node may have zero inbound edges.

================================ THE CALIBRATION CORRECTION (carry this forward) ==================
The builder OVER-PREDICTED FINDING COUNTS TWICE RUNNING, both high, both toward "more findings":
compounds 3-5 predicted / 2 found; omissions 3-6 predicted / 2 found. Two in a row in the same
direction is a fact about the estimator, not noise. THE NEXT PRE-REGISTRATION STATES THIS
CORRECTION EXPLICITLY AND ADJUSTS, rather than discovering it a third time. Note the direction: it
is the same pull toward finding things that this program polices everywhere else -- now visible in
the builder's own priors rather than in a result.

================================ THE STANDING LIMIT (unchanged) ==================================
Both conjuncts are C3-class judgements: non-mechanizable, analyst-supplied. v2 mechanizes the
BOOKKEEPING around them (dispositions, edges) and mechanizes none of the judgement. It makes the
CLAIM checkable, never the answer correct.
