WHY A SWEEP AT ALL. The compound-split re-audit found three omissions INCIDENTALLY -- while looking
for something else -- and two of the three had the same shape (a presupposition carried by several
nodes and booked by none). Two same-shape hits found by accident is the signal that a systematic
pass finds more. The three already-found omissions are now BANKED (universal_metric_coupling,
gravitational_uv_ceiling, flatness_in_reduction), so this sweep looks for FURTHER ones and must not
re-report them.

THE STANDARD (frozen, not restated loosely): a proposition is an OMISSION if some banked node's
discharge PRESUPPOSES it -- you cannot discharge that node without it -- and it is NOT ITSELF BOOKED.
Both conjuncts must be shown. Cluster membership is the fence against returning all of metaphysics:
a candidate counts only if a NAMED POSITION could deny it and the denial would change something the
cluster says.
