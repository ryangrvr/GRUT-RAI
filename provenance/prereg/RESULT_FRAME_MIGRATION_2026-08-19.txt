*** WHAT I GOT WRONG, ON THE RECORD ***

    I reported a gapped tower of quasinormal poles, with 96 modes "exhibited". The ODE-residual and
    polynomial-truncation checks were sound. The boundary-condition check was not: it verified that
    the hypergeometric factor is non-zero at the horizon, which does not establish pure
    outgoingness, because the peeled factor (1-r^2)^(-i w/2) = cosh(x)^(i w) is itself not purely
    outgoing when i*w is a positive integer. That is the Gamma(-i w) pole colliding with the
    1/Gamma(alpha) zero -- 0 x infinity -- which the calculation's own PART 2 printed, which I
    noted as needing care, and which I then did not carry through. The exact counterexample:
    c=0, l=2, i*w=3 gives psi = sinh^3(x) with outgoing amplitude 3/16 and ingoing amplitude
    -3/16, decomposition residual ~1e-163.

    I also reported the graviton's m^2 = 2H^2 as a substantive finding. The frame-shopping audit
    showed it is a Regge-Wheeler substitution artefact: V = f[l(l+1)/r^2 + m^2 + f'/r] with
    f'/r = -2H^2 identically, so m^2 = +2H^2 exactly cancels that term and carries no invariant
    content. That correction is the audit's, not mine.
