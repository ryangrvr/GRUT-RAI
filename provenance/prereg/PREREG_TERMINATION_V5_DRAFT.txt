This draft exists because the seven-outcome enumeration in CLASS_C_DISPATCH_SPEC.md
produces results that V4's C1 channel cannot resolve: five of seven leave the stop
clock open under V4's rules. V5 closes that gap by enumerating every outcome class
from the frozen manifest and mapping each to a resolution path.

R2 NODES DECIDE: consequences execute only at the named register node.
R3 PUBLIC EVENTS ONLY: thresholded outcomes fire only against thresholds frozen
   before the public event they judge.

R5 ONE CLOCK: in-house physics STOPS at the earliest of:
   (i) 2026-12-31;
   (ii) all channels below reach RESOLVED or RETIRED status;
   (iii) any logged reply or computed result asserting an outcome class flagged
        FIRES STOP in channel C1 (the stop, once fired, stands even if later
        replies contest the channel line).
   After the stop: consume external results (all of them) before deposit.

WHAT CHANGED FROM V4 AND WHY

V4's C1 channel resolved on exactly two outcomes: POLE or CUT. The frozen
dispatch spec enumerates six permitted outcome classes plus a seventh catch-all
("another structure justified by the calculation"). Five of these — ladder,
secular/nonstationary, no-long-memory, ill-posed-after-assembly, and other —
could not fire V4's R5(iii), meaning the program structurally could not recognise
five of its own endings. V5 fixes this by enumerating every outcome class from
the frozen manifest and mapping each to a resolution path, with C1.c, C1.d, and
C1.e explicitly firing the stop.

ADDITIONAL CONSEQUENCE: four outcome classes (ladder, secular/nonstationary,
no-long-memory, and another-structure) potentially contradict rung1_inin_action's
"finite memory" clause at tier shown. If any of these fires, the rung1 consequence
assessment must evaluate whether the contradiction requires re-tiering rung1 or
whether the finite-memory clause survives as an approximation valid in the
regime probed.
