## 1. Purpose

RRP was created at the closure of the GRUT program (L-22). GRUT's audit ended with a
distinctive-theory claim refuted, an empty prediction ledger, and one certified
standard-QFT result — and with a set of structural questions the closure exposed but did
not answer: why every audited description consumed inputs it did not generate, and whether
that pattern was physics or bookkeeping. RRP was chartered to reverse the direction of
inquiry: not "does this theory work," but "what does the record actually require."

## 2. Starting Question

"What does reality require?" was made operational as: build an adversarially verified
corpus of established physics; extract candidate structural requirements from it under a
frozen protocol; attack every candidate with kill-by-default refuters; and keep only what
survives, at recorded strength, with formulation-relativity declared and deflationary
outcomes pre-registered as legitimate.

## 3. RRP-00 — Effective Requirements

From a 13-domain, ~380-entry corpus (all records adversarially verified; zero fabricated
citations found), 26 candidate requirements were extracted and attacked: 15 were refuted,
the rest survived only narrowed. What survived (L-1–5): one O-grade structural containment
(the universally coupled helicity-2 EH+Λ effective sector in the tested regime); two
consistency conditions that constrain without fixing (anomaly freedom by some declared
mechanism, with the observed generation-wise cancellation as data; the corrected
Weinberg–Witten named-exit condition); the derive-or-price meta-requirement with
predictively contentful selections; and the priced-input inventory (G, Λ, the operational
probability rule and branch weights, time-asymmetric boundary data). What was rejected
(L-6, L-7): every substrate-level structural claim tested — substrate locality, substrate
Lorentz invariance, Hilbert-space and tensor structure, degree-2 probability — each either
analytic in its own formulation class or counterexampled. Headline, at audited strength:
observation pins effective structure and prices inputs; on this record it does not force
substrate structure.

## 4. RRP-01 — Law, State, and Supplied Content

The strongest cross-domain pattern (L-7) — laws fix magnitudes and kinds; reference-state
data fix asymmetries and selections — was made the phase question. Three candidate
explanations (invariance argument; conservation of priced input; one-preparation reading)
were attacked: the first split (its existence-half demoted to bookkeeping, its
location-half surviving on empirical anchors), the second strengthened into per-map typed
form after its own audit showed no global measure exists (L-8), the third narrowed. The
smallness question then ran through four candidates (K-I–III, K-U); all four were refuted
as stated, and the blinded kernel test produced the corrected relation **priced ⊂ kernel**
on the tested cases (L-9) — with the instrument's own category-leak defect disclosed and
seven showcase rows discounted — plus the quotient-kernel/flow-residue distinction (L-10).
Stage 1–2 formalized the survivor as the factorization form F′ (L-11) and, across every
audited interchange and selection mechanism, found suppliedness conserved — relocated and
rebooked, never eliminated (L-12). Every thread terminated at one upstream point: what
fixes the specification space S.

## 5. RRP-02 — Specification-Level Recursion

The S-question was audited against the six strongest available S-derivation candidates,
steelman-first, source-verified. Result (L-13): zero eliminations. One type-local
compression (SO(10): real rep-content compression and coincidences-become-theorems, paid

confirming the derived/priced split from the other side). Refinement: conservation of
suppliedness **up to type conversion** (L-15's precursor). The same architecture appeared
one level up: law-derived structure on specification space, supplied specification point.

## 6. RRP-03 — The Recursion Invariant

One question, with Branch 1 (bookkeeping/architecture) as the explicit null hypothesis:
is the conservation-with-type-conversion pattern a genuine invariant of a defined map
class, or does it recur because each framework introduces a new specification space by
construction? Branch 2 required both a surviving precise invariant *and* a discriminant
separating physics from generic formal systems. Four instruments ran: a formalization
attack, a logic null model, an engineered-systems null model, and a discriminant hunt with
the burden of proof on the discriminant.

## 7. The Formal Result

The candidate invariant (II″) — absence of strictly reducing maps under a certificate-based
typed order on supplied-content ledgers — was **successfully formalized and is
non-vacuous** on the program's audited record (L-15): 19 map-connected ledger pairs, 19/19
comparable; 9 certified equivalences (including the Hartle–Hawking contour↔saddle↔Robin
conversion triangle, verified in the participants' own words); 9 strict increases; 0
strict reductions; stable under enumeration variants (N = 17–21); one TYPE-BLOCKED pair
(CPR) where the verdict holds by refusal, disclosed. Two relativities are part of the
result: the order exists only relative to the audits' declared ledger-closure and
record-preservation conventions, and reduction-detection is asymmetrically harder than
increase-detection. This is a result over the audited map class and record — **not a
universal theorem over all possible theories.** And its most branch-relevant feature is
internal: all nine strict relations point toward *more* suppliedness in the deeper
framework — precisely the direction the bookkeeping null predicts.

## 8. What the Null Models Establish

The same recursion holds outside physics (L-16, L-17). In pure logic it holds at theorem
grade: second-order categoricity eliminates "which model" exactly insofar as the full
semantics is supplied from the metatheory, and withdrawing that supplied item provably
restores nonstandard models; Löwenheim–Skolem is a theorem-grade instance of a law failing
to pin its point; Lindström bounds the available trades; conservativity eliminates only
relative to a theorem class, the precise analog of EFT decoupling. No genuine unscoped
specification-elimination was found **in the surveyed classes** of mathematics.
Engineered specification stacks reproduce the trichotomy (redundancy quotient / scoped
reduction / relocation; no strict reductions) and carry their own forced dimension
theorems. The discriminant hunt (L-18) then failed in the strong sense: D1 — the
load-bearing candidate, that physics uniquely derives the size and stratification of its
supplied spaces — was killed by three independent null-side theorem families
(parametricity, Böhm–Berarducci, Shelah's Main Gap); D2–D4 failed their pre-registered
kill conditions; and three further candidates generated from the program's own data were
killed in the same hunt. **The null hypothesis did not win because the discriminant search
was weak; the search generated candidates and eliminated them.** Verdict (L-19), at
recorded strength: the recursion is the architecture of formal description.

## 9. What Was Actually Learned

Three layers, kept separate:

**A. Effective physical requirements (physics, as audited):** the RRP-00 core — the
helicity-2 EH+Λ containment; the anomaly-consistency and Weinberg–Witten conditions; the
derive-or-price form with contentful selections; the priced-input inventory (L-1–5).

**B. Structural regularities of description (architecture, not physics-specific):** the
law/state split's existence and conservation behavior; the factorization form; the
recursion through specification levels; (II″) as a formalized, non-vacuous, falsifiable
regularity of the audited record — all now known to be exhibited equally by logic
(provably, in surveyed classes) and by engineered specification systems (L-11, L-15–19).

**C. Empirical anchors (the irreducibly physical remainder):** the verified record itself
and its measured values — which every audited framework organizes progressively better and
none converts into consequences (L-20). The residual question — why this record, these
values, this realization — remains an empirical and theoretical physics question. Nothing
in this program establishes that it is unanswerable.

## 10. What Was NOT Learned

RRP does not establish: any theory of everything or candidate substrate; the derivation of
any constant, coupling, or initial condition; that physics can never explain its empirical
anchors; that the universe is fundamentally underdetermined; any metaphysical claim about
reality's nature; a universal theorem of suppliedness-conservation (the result is over the
audited map class); the impossibility of a future discriminant; verdicts on unaudited
derivation programs; or the rehabilitation — or refutation at its own claim point — of
GRUT's responsive-vacuum hypothesis. Where the record is silent, this synthesis is silent.

## 14. Appendix: RRP Vocabulary

- **Supplied content** — information a description consumes without deriving: selections,
  parameter values, boundary/state data, model-class declarations, measures/contours.
- **Priced input** — supplied content explicitly declared and carried in a ledger rather
  than left implicit.
- **Derive** — obtain as a consequence of stated structure, with the derivation's own
  inputs ledgered.
- **Relocate** — move supplied content to another representation, level, or booking
  (law↔state, object↔metatheory) without reducing it.
- **Scoped reduction** — eliminate an item's relevance relative to a stated observable or
  theorem class only (EFT decoupling; conservativity), the item persisting in the full
  description.
- **Strict reduction** — a map to a description whose complete supplied ledger is smaller
  under a type-honest correspondence with the payment ledger closed. None found on the
  audited record.
- **Type conversion** — certified rebooking of supplied content across types (discrete ↔
  continuous ↔ model-class ↔ measure), size comparisons across types being refused.
- **Architecture** — a property of formal description as such, exhibited by non-physical
  specification systems (logic, engineered stacks), as opposed to a property of physics.
- **Empirical anchor** — the measured, presentation-absolute record that pins the audited
  descriptions and constitutes the program's irreducibly physical remainder.
- **Class-4 gate** — the standing criterion that a claimed derivation of supplied content
  counts only as a strict reduction: forcing without relocation, retyping, or scope
  restriction. Open; never yet passed.
