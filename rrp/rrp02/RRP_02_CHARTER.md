# RRP_02_CHARTER — S-Selection

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Authority:** owner opening ruling, following RRP-01
(`rrp/rrp01/STAGE2_SYNTHESIS.md`: every thread terminated at content/specification
selection). **One question:**

> **Can the specification space S itself be derived from a more primitive theory, or is S
> necessarily an irreducible part of physical content?**

## 1. The two criteria, fixed at birth

**Falsification condition (hard):** one genuine strict-reduction example in which
suppliedness is *eliminated* ⇒ principle (II) of the Stage-3 formulation fails. This
phase actively hunts that example — finding it would be the most instructive outcome.

**Positive criterion (equally hard):** a proposed derivation of S does not count because S
is embedded in a larger mathematical structure. It must show the supplied content is
**forced by the deeper dynamics without moving the specification one level upward.** The
regress S₀ → S₁ → S₂ → ⋯ teaches nothing except how to relocate unexplained content, and
detecting it is this phase's core instrument.

## 2. The regress detector (the instrument)

Every candidate S-derivation is audited by a **per-map typed ledger** (never a global
integer — NOT-FORMALIZABLE-TYPE-MIXED stands): what specification content does the deeper
theory T′ consume (its S′: group choices, field/rep content, breaking sectors, manifold
and flux data, measures, contours, model classes, declared symmetries), versus what of S
it forces. Verdict classes, pre-registered:

- **ELIMINATION** — S-content forced with strictly less supplied content in S′, same-map,
  type-honest. (The class-4 gate at specification level; II fails if found.)
- **COMPRESSION** — strictly fewer independent supplied items at honestly compared type
  (e.g. one irrep choice replacing many rep choices), with the payment ledgered. Partial
  pressure on II; adjudicated per-map, not by count alone.
- **PRUNING** — consistency conditions derive structure ON the space of admissible S
  (cutting, stratifying, bounding it) while the actual S remains a supplied point. Note:
  this is the F′ form applied to S-space itself — if pruning is the recurring outcome, the
  recursion is a finding and must be recorded as such, with its own trap (see conceptual
  pass) named.
- **RELOCATION** — the content moves to S′ undiminished or enlarged.
- **ILL-POSED** — the comparison cannot be honestly typed for this map; recorded, not
  forced.

## 3. Pre-registered outcomes (all deliverable)

(1) A genuine ELIMINATION → II fails; the program learns the most. (2) Every audited
candidate lands in PRUNING/RELOCATION (± type-paid COMPRESSION) → II strengthened at
S-level, and the F′-recursion (law derives admissible-S structure; the S-point stays
supplied) is documented at whatever strength the audits earn. (3) The comparison is
ill-posed beyond per-map form → recorded as a boundary of the question itself.
**Irreducibility of S is never asserted as a universal theorem** (C5/G-WEAK discipline):
at most per-audited-class irreducibility plus the conservation regularity, at recorded
strength.

## 4. Scope and prohibitions

No giant survey — bounded audits of named, strongest-available candidates, read-level,
verified citations. No new attempt to derive G, Λ, ħ, or the SM inside this program. No
GRUT. No ontology construction: if the audits converge, the output is the documented shape
of the regress, not a proposed substrate. The parked seven-row K-rerun remains parked as
control work for K-U/F, outside this phase. All RRP-01 discipline inherits (firewalls;
computed-independently; survival ≠ truth; per-map typing; traps named at birth).

## 5. Stops

Each audit is one bounded pass with a written verdict; the phase closes with a verdict
document assigning the S-question to its outcome at recorded strength — including
"irreducible at current evidence, per audited class," which closes it honorably; any drift
toward candidate-substrate construction stops the phase.
