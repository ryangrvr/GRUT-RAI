# RRP_02_CONCEPTUAL_PASS_01 — typing the S-question before auditing it

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Grade: CONCEPTUAL, UNCHALLENGED — nothing banks.** Inline, main
session. Purpose: type the question, name the candidates, define the audit shape, and name
the traps — before any instrument runs.

## 1. "Derive S" is three different claims

The record already shows the question splits by grade, and conflating the grades is the
K-U mistake one level up:

- **S-elimination**: a deeper theory forces S with strictly less specification of its own.
  No audited instance exists anywhere in the program's record; the class-4 gate has held
  through GRUT, RRP-00, RRP-01. This phase hunts it directly.
- **S-pruning**: consistency conditions derive structure *on the space of admissible
  specifications* — anomaly freedom already sits in the corpus in exactly this form
  ("constrains without uniquely fixing," Babu–Mohapatra leak); swampland-type and
  bootstrap programs have this shape by construction. Pruning is real derivation of
  *structure*, and derives no *point*.
- **S-relocation**: embeddings that move the content (gauge group from geometry →
  manifold/flux choice; SM from GUT group → breaking-sector content; vacuum from
  landscape → measure).

**The recursion observation** (this pass's central object, offered for attack): S-pruning
is F′ applied one level up. The law-side derives a stratified admissible-S-space
(anomaly-free chiral content; consistency cones; the tenfold way as the derived
stratification of admissible symmetry classes); the actual S is a supplied point in it. If
the audits confirm this shape across candidates, the program's answer will be: **S is
neither derived nor arbitrary — at every audited level, it is a supplied point in a
law-derived admissible space, and the regress itself exhibits conservation of
suppliedness.**

**The trap, named at birth:** "admissible-S-space" is dangerously reusable — any
specification can be post-hoc embedded in some space of alternatives, which is the
undetermined-by-the-flow smuggle again. A pruning claim counts only when the structure on
S-space is **forced by stated consistency requirements internal to the framework**
(anomaly freedom from unitarity+gauge invariance; positivity from causality+unitarity),
never by an invented parameterization of alternatives. Auditors must check the forcing,
not the existence, of the S-space structure.

## 2. The candidate inventory (strongest available, one bounded audit each)

- **A1 — Grand unification (SO(10) as the sharpest case).** The one candidate with a real
  COMPRESSION claim: one 16-spinor per generation replaces the SM's five rep choices +
  RH neutrino, with charge quantization and anomaly freedom automatic. The audit must
  ledger the payment side at source (breaking chain and Higgs-sector content,
  doublet–triplet splitting, threshold/tuning content) and adjudicate whether the
  compression survives type-honest comparison. **This is where the falsification pressure
  on II is highest on the current record.**
- **A2 — Kaluza–Klein / string compactification.** Gauge group from isometries/geometry:
  the canonical relocation exemplar (to manifold, flux, and moduli data). Audit for any
  residual genuine compression.
- **A3 — Landscape + measure.** S from vacuum selection; audit where the specification
  reappears (measure problem, vacuum-counting ambiguities).
- **A4 — Asymptotic-safety coupling predictions.** The one candidate claiming to derive a
  *coordinate of the point* (the Higgs-mass prediction, Shaposhnikov–Wetterich class):
  audit its assumption ledger and current empirical status — a verified success here would
  be a partial elimination of point-suppliedness, which II must face honestly.
- **A5 — Bootstrap / swampland pruning.** The purest S-pruning programs: what structure on
  theory-space is actually theorem-grade vs conjectural; the F′-recursion witness.
- **A6 — Dimension/content from dynamics.** CDT's spectral-dimension flow, causal-set
  dimension estimators, and emergent-Hilbert-space programs: what is genuinely derived
  (outputs) vs target-selected or supplied (the 4, the action class, the measure).

Each auditor returns: verdict class (charter §2), the typed per-map ledger, the regress
check (does S′ carry ≥ content, honestly typed?), and verified citations. No champion; no
substrate proposals.

## 3. What would close the phase

A single verified ELIMINATION (II fails — headline). Or: all six land in
PRUNING/RELOCATION with COMPRESSION at most type-paid → the recursion is documented, II is
strengthened at S-level, and the verdict document states per-audited-class irreducibility
at exactly that strength. Or: honest ILL-POSED beyond per-map form → the boundary of the
question is the finding. The phase does not propose what fixes S; it determines whether
anything on the audited record does.
