# K_ROUND_PROTOCOL — pre-registered before execution (RRP-01, kernel round)

**Date:** 2026-09-24. **Frozen at commit; execution may not alter it.** Scope per owner
ruling: K-round refutation → 48-item kernel-membership sweep → synthesis. **No corpus
broadening. No new candidate mechanisms. No substrate search. No "information theory
explains reality."** The single question: does the mathematical correspondence claimed by
K-U actually exist?

## The ordering discipline (binding; the C-I lesson carried forward)

The critical question is whether the kernel exists **independently of the list of things
already called priced inputs**. Therefore, in that order and with no back-edges:

1. **Identify the contraction from the physics** of each description.
2. **Compute its kernel without consulting the priced-input list.**
3. **Only then compare** the kernel with the inventory.

**Implementation — blinding, not exhortation:** kernel-computing agents receive
physics-only briefs (corpus fields: formalism, phenomena, derived_objects, limits,
domain_of_validity — with empirical_inputs and constants fields STRIPPED), are forbidden to
read any `rrp/` analysis document, and their prompts contain no mention of priced inputs,
K-U, the 48-item table, or this phase's question. Each kernel element must be justified
from the contraction's mathematics (verified at read level where load-bearing). A separate
comparator — the only agent shown both sides — then adjudicates membership per item in both
directions and checks the briefs did not leak.

## Pre-registered outcomes (all deliverable)

- **K-U FAILS:** the split is real in the surveyed cases but not explained by a common
  contraction/kernel mechanism; the deeper question stays open.
- **K-U PARTIAL** (flagged in advance as the likely most interesting): contraction explains
  a specific class of supplied information — that which becomes invisible under
  coarse-graining, RG flow, decoherence, or relaxation — but not everything; a mechanism
  for part of the law/state boundary is then identified, with its boundary drawn by the
  sweep.
- **K-U STRONG:** the correspondence holds across the sweep; the successor question ("why
  does physical reality possess these particular contraction kernels?") becomes live — and
  is NOT opened inside this round.

## Falsifiability guard

The flavor sector is the standing negative control (no contraction, no smallness). **If the
round finds that every priced item can be represented as the kernel of some suitably chosen
contraction, that is not victory — it is the diagnosis that K-U is unfalsifiable
bookkeeping, and the recorded verdict is exactly that.** A kernel claim counts only when
the contraction is independently motivated by the physics of the description, not selected
to contain the answer. Mismatches in BOTH directions are findings: priced-but-not-kernel
(counterexample to K-U) and kernel-but-not-priced (unexplained surplus — recorded, not
rationalized).
