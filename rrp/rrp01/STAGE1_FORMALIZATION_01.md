## 1. The five sharpest surviving examples, written down exactly

**E1 — Flavor invariants.** Space 𝒴 ≅ (ℂ^{3×3})³ of Yukawa tensors (36+18 real
parameters); compact group G_F = U(3)⁵ acting linearly. The object is the **orbit quotient
𝒴/G_F with its invariant ring** ℝ[𝒴]^{G_F}, generated by traces of words in
H_u = Y_uY_u†, H_d, H_e. Every basis-independent statement of the theory factors through
the quotient map π: 𝒴 → 𝒴/G_F; the supplied datum is **a point of 𝒴/G_F** (13 real
coordinates + θ̄). The theory derives dim(𝒴/G_F) (stabilizer computation) and the ring
structure; it does not derive the point. *Type: invariant theory of a compact linear
action — kinematic, exact at every scale.*

**E2 — Branch weights.** A CPTP map Λ that is a Schur multiplier in the pointer basis
(fixed by the commutant of H_int), with unit diagonal: Λ(ρ) = ρ ∘ M, M_nn = 1. The object
is the **fixed-point algebra of the channel — a commutative subalgebra 𝒜 ≅ ℂⁿ — together
with the conditional expectation onto it**. The theory (interaction structure) derives
*which* subalgebra; the supplied datum is **the state's restriction to 𝒜** — a point of
the simplex Δ^{n−1}. *Type: fixed-point algebra of a CP map — dynamical protection of a
kinematically-shaped value space.*

**E3 — R_K / Chern number.** Space 𝒢 of gapped, finite-range Hamiltonians with its
deformation topology. The object is the **locally constant map C: 𝒢 → ℤ** (constant on
π₀(𝒢); Hastings–Michalakis grade). The theory derives the partition into components and
the value map; the supplied datum is **which component the system occupies** — equivalently
a point of π₀(𝒢). *Type: deformation-class invariant — topology of an admissible-theory
space.*

**E4 — ζ / primordial amplitude.** Per-mode phase space with a flow whose forward
asymptotics (after horizon exit) annihilates one of two independent solutions; the frozen
variable ζ_k spans a one-dimensional slow manifold per mode, with a derivable basin
boundary (ultra-slow-roll). The object is the **asymptotic projection onto the slow
manifold**; the theory derives the projection, its rank (one function, not two — the
round's type-derivation), and where it fails; the supplied datum is **the point on the slow
manifold** (the function A_s, n_s). *Type: attractor/limit projection of a genuinely
dynamical flow — contractive only asymptotically.*

**E5 — Bath sufficient statistic.** Space ℰ of Gaussian environments within the declared
model class; the influence-functional map ℰ → (influence functionals) factors exactly
through (J(ω), β). The object is the **sufficient-statistic factorization** with fibres =
environments sharing (J, β). The theory (coupling structure + Gaussianity) derives the
factorization; the supplied datum is **the value (J, β)**. *Type: kinematic factorization
induced by coupling structure — quotient-like despite dynamical clothing.* (Noted: E5's
model-class restriction is itself supplied — flagged for Stage 2, route 2.)

## 2. The common denominator — what these actually share

After respecting the quotient/flow distinction, all five instantiate one shape:

> A space 𝒮 of upstream specifications; a map **Φ: 𝒮 → 𝒪** determined by the theory's
> own structure (symmetry action in E1, interaction commutant in E2, gap topology in E3,
> flow asymptotics in E4, coupling structure in E5); and the supplied datum of the
> downstream description is exactly **a point of im(Φ)**.

Stated without safeguards this is trivially universal — *any* input to *any* function is a
point in some theory-determined map's image. The non-trivial shared content is three
properties that every exemplar has and that the trivialization lacks:

- **(F1) Φ is forced**: it originates in a theorem-grade structure of the theory
  (symmetry, commutant, gap, asymptotics, coupling form), not in a modeling choice. Where
  it *is* partly a choice (E5's model class), that residue is priced — and flagged.
- **(F2) Φ is genuinely many-to-one**: an exhibitable fibre (26-dimensional in E1;
  off-diagonal environments in E2; deformation orbits in E3; the decaying mode in E4;
  (J,β)-equivalent environments in E5). This is what "the information was never there /
  does not survive" means, and it excludes the any-function trivialization.
- **(F3) im(Φ) has derivable structure**: its dimension (E1: 13; E4: rank one per mode),
  its topology (E3: ℤ), its convex geometry (E2: Δ^{n−1}), its function-space type
  (E5: (J,β)) are *outputs* of the theory. This is the class-A count phenomenon in
  general form.

**The candidate common denominator, at exactly this strength:** *each supplied datum in the
sharp examples is a point of a space whose existence, shape, and size the theory derives
via a forced many-to-one factorization — the theory derives the where-values-live; it does
not derive the value.* Label: **the factorization form (F)**. F is NOT an RRP candidate —
it is the formalized target Stage 2 exists to attack.

## 3. The traps, handed to Stage 2 explicitly

The geometry-vs-coordinates reading is now F's informal gloss, and F inherits its dangers,
sharpened into the owner's four routes:

1. **Coordinate dependence**: is "forced" (F1) well-defined across presentations? A
   reformulation might present the same physics with a different Φ — or none. The G-round
   precedent (same content as contour / saddle rule / boundary condition) is the standing
   warning that "the map the theory determines" may be presentation-relative.
2. **Law/state interchange**: can an equivalent formulation move Φ itself (or its model
   class, cf. E5) into the supplied side, or absorb the supplied point into the law
   (Λ as coupling vs integration constant is the recorded precedent)?
3. **Dynamical selection**: are some "supplied points" actually selected — an attractor
   picking the point, not merely the space? (BD as candidate attractor state; Hill
   quasi-fixed points compressing y_t; einselection is already law-side. If yes in some
   exemplar, F's clean split fails there — or F must absorb it as "the selection is part of
   Φ," which is route-4 territory.)
4. **Hybrids/triviality**: does the theory sometimes fix *coordinates of the point* as
   well as the space (partial determination), and can F state where the boundary falls
   without consulting the answer? If F can absorb every hybrid post hoc, it is taxonomy,
   not structure — the K-U lesson applied to F itself.

**Success criterion for Stage 2, fixed now:** F survives only if (a) at least one exemplar's
Φ is shown *canonical* — presentation-independent in a stated precise sense (e.g., E1's
quotient is category-theoretically forced by gauge-invariance of observables; E3's π₀ is
manifestly parameterization-free), AND (b) at least one attack route produces a genuine
failure or boundary somewhere else — because a hypothesis that survives everywhere
untouched, on this program's record, is one whose test was too weak. Partial survival with
a drawn boundary is, as before, the informative outcome.
