**Date:** 2026-09-23. **Authority:** owner opening ruling, following the RRP-00 requirements

## 0. The question

> Why does physical law determine dynamical possibilities and quantitative evolution while
> apparently leaving certain selections, asymmetries, and boundary/reference-state
> information to the state of the system?

Equivalently: **can the derive-or-price split itself be explained?** The subject is the
RRP-00 core's strongest cross-domain pattern — *laws fix magnitudes and kinds;
reference-state data fix asymmetries and selections* — with its audited instances: branch
weights from initial amplitudes; relaxation magnitude from dynamics with direction from
state data (Micadei-anchored); predictively contentful selections (SJ ≠ BD); effective
gravitational structure pinned while substrate structure stays open; Gleason moving the
probability premises upstream rather than discharging them.

## 1. Pre-registered outcome classes (none chosen in advance; all deliverable)

1. **ARTIFACT** — the split is an artifact of how current physics is formulated (e.g., of
   the (state space, dynamics, state) presentation or of the operational method itself).
2. **MATHEMATICAL** — the split is a consequence of deeper mathematical structure
   (symmetry/covariance theorems; the reconstruction-theorem regress).
3. **EMPIRICAL** — the split is an empirical feature of physical reality (the content of
   the supplied side is contingent preparation data).
4. **DERIVABLE** — a deeper theory could derive it (criterion below).
Mixed verdicts are legitimate — the existence, location, and content of the split may fall
in different classes — and so is "irreducible at current evidence": *if the split cannot be
explained, the divide between dynamics and supplied state information is itself an
irreducible feature of our best physical descriptions, and that is the finding.*

## 2. The inversion this phase carries

GRUT tried to explain state from dynamics. RRP-00's record suggests the deeper problem is
neither "how does dynamics generate everything" nor its reverse, but **what determines the
relationship between dynamics and state**. Born weights, subsystem decomposition, the arrow
of time, boundary conditions, vacuum choice, observer/reference structure, and G/Λ are not
one problem — but they may be manifestations of a common law/state interface. No ontology is
declared for that interface (not a vacuum, field, memory kernel, or information substrate);
whether it is even one thing is itself an open question of this phase.
