## 2. The core (outcome A), at exact strength

Any theory — any substrate, ontology, or formalism — that accounts for the corpus's verified
record must accomplish all of the following. Nothing here asserts what the theory *is*; each
item is stamped with the highest gate its evidence earned (M/D/P/O) and scoped to its
declared class.

**A. The meta-requirement (derive-or-price), R10.1 as stated + R10.2 narrowed — M/D.**
In every audited ontology class (Hilbert-space QM with tensor composition; AQFT type-III₁
nets; causal-set kinematics; CDT-class layered path integrals), a theory that closes a
quantitative description referring to subsystems, regions, or a distinguished state must
either **derive** that decomposition/selection within its own formalism or carry it as a
**declared, priced input** — and the price is physical, not notational: inequivalent
admissible selections yield different computed predictions where both are definable
(SJ ≠ BD on de Sitter, formalism-fixed). CPR's uniqueness-given-existence stands as the
recorded conditional alternative that keeps the form genuinely disjunctive. No universal
impossibility is asserted (C5).

**B. The one O-grade structural containment, R8.1′.** Any theory admitting a local effective
description of its long-range gravitational sector in the tested low-curvature regime, and
reproducing the five pinned data legs (MICROSCOPE WEP ~10⁻¹⁵; Cassini γ−1 = (2.1±2.3)×10⁻⁵;
GW170817 speed equality ~10⁻¹⁵ with two-TT-consistent polarization; PSR B1913+16 decay at
0.9983±0.0016 of the GR quadrupole; the GW150914 waveform), must contain in that effective
description a **single universally coupled helicity-2 sector with Einstein–Hilbert + Λ
dynamics at leading derivative order**. This is the only place in the whole record where
observation pins *structure* rather than bounding or pricing it — and it pins the effective
sector only, never the substrate.

**C. Consistency conditions that constrain without fixing — M/D, with O-grade data.**
(i) R6.1′: in 4D local unitary chiral gauge QFT demanded renormalizable, gauge-anomaly
freedom *by some declared mechanism* (content cancellation or Green–Schwarz/WZ-type added
structure) is a quantizability condition — it ties matter content to symmetry structure
without uniquely fixing charges; in EFT-organized classes, uncancelled anomalies price a
finite cutoff instead (Preskill), lower-bounded by the ~TeV record. The O-grade datum rides
alongside: **the observed fermion content cancels all gauge anomalies generation by

rendering the observed radiative sector as a massless spin-2 *quantum* inside a

named theorem hypothesis** — quantized GR pays via diffeomorphism invariance; emergent
substrata must name their exit. The observations themselves establish classical radiation

**D. The priced-input inventory — what every audited route consumes and none derives.**
(i) R8.2′: the two leading gravitational coefficients **G and Λ enter as underived empirical

**E. Scope bounds the core itself must respect.** R9-3′: over closed, measure-preserving,
