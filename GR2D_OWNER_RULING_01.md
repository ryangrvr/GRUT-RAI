## The ruling

**GR2-d: ACCEPTED at the recorded strength — `D-PARTIAL`, 22/25. No
status upgrade.** This is exactly the kind of result the hostile
charter was meant to expose.

The important distinction: the failed quantum leg is **not evidence
that the causal-cone idea is false**. It means the pre-registered
quantum demonstration did not establish the required distinct-cone
separation. Because V-2/V-3 were deliberately conjunctive, their
failure is mechanically inherited from Q-2.

The clean results are still substantial:

- Classical two-cone demonstration passed: v_B/v_A = 1.2968 versus the
  analytic 1.3.
- Exchange produces a composite cone rather than selecting a universal
  one.
- Equal supplied inputs produce a common cone; unequal inputs retain
  sector dependence.
- λ-relabeling leaves the earned frequency battery unchanged while
  changing front speed — particularly useful: **the existing earned
  spectral/influence data cannot recover the causal propagation
  speed.**
- Memory does not establish support.
- **0/7 different-cone systems were eliminated by the earned tests.**

So the central GR2-d question remains unresolved in the strongest
sense:

> **The earned core has not selected a universal causal cone.**

But because Q-2 did not pass, this is **not** upgraded to the stronger
statement that the quantum causal cone has been demonstrated
irreducible.

## GR2-d2 — AUTHORIZED, but narrowly

**GR2-d2 is a re-charter of Q-2 only.** The classical, exchange,
relabeling, and memory legs are **not** reopened: those are already
adjudicated. The purpose is exactly:

> **Can an earned GRUT constraint distinguish genuinely different
> quantum propagation cones?**

The quantum parameter sets must be selected **before** the measurement
procedure is run, and they must have demonstrably distinct
Lieb–Robinson velocities under the frozen microscopic construction.

**Three required pre-run gates:**

1. **Parameter separation gate.** Establish analytically/numerically,
   before the front measurement, that the selected sectors have
   sufficiently different expected LR speeds.
2. **Procedure calibration gate.** Calibrate the quantum-front
   estimator independently, analogous to the disclosed classical
   K = 1 calibration. The calibration must not use the two target
   sectors.
3. **Blind separation gate.** Freeze the required measured separation
   *before* looking at the target-sector result. No post-hoc threshold
   based on whatever separation happens to appear.

**And the same hard rule: if Q-2 fails again, record the failure. Do
not select parameters again.**

## What GR2-d2 can and cannot establish

A successful d2 establishes: *quantum causal-cone distinction is
demonstrable within the tested class*. It does **not**, by itself,
establish *the universal cone is derived* — for that an earned
discriminator eliminating the different-cone alternatives would still
be needed. Conversely, a clean d2 showing genuinely different
admissible quantum cones strengthens the irreducibility result
considerably:

> **locality + influence + geometry + memory + quantum dynamics ⇏
> c_universal**

— making the supplied status of causal structure considerably harder
to dispute.
