THE NEXT QUESTION

The omega^7 gravitational result must now be treated as a conditional construction, not as evidence that gravity follows from the microscopic core.

The relevant structure is approximately:

    J(omega) ~ omega^3 x omega^4 = omega^7

where the omega^3 phase-space factor is generic for the tested 3D acoustic setting, while the additional +4 comes from cancellation in the chosen stress-tensor/minimal-stress coupling.

Therefore the critical question is NOT:

    "Can GRUT produce omega^7?"

We already know that a specified coupling can.

The critical question is:

    "Does the GRUT generative core itself force the probe/coupling structure whose cancellation produces omega^7 and, more fundamentally, force an Einstein-like gravitational sector?"

THIS IS THE NEXT EXPERIMENT.

Do NOT assume the answer is yes.
Do NOT modify tests to obtain a desired result.
Do NOT reinterpret a conditional result as a derivation.
Preserve every failure.
