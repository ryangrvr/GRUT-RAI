## §0 The three outcome-relevant findings

1. **The "CL-3/CL-5 rank drop" route to DIRECT-CLASSICAL is closed at the earned retained site.**
   - With output at an **end** site of a tridiagonal chain, the Hermann–Krener map is a triangular
     polynomial automorphism with constant determinant.
   - So there are no rank drops anywhere and the retained site is globally observable.
   - A drop quadric does exist for an **interior** output, but interior outputs are not earned.
   - This is proof support for the owner's theorem task, carried out in
     `L0_ACCESS_RANKDROP_THEOREM_01.md`.
2. **"Every noncommutative lift needs ħ" has a counterexample: Koopman–von Neumann (KvN).**
   - Local algebras generated by xᵢ and λᵢ = −i∂ᵢ give [λᵢ,[L,x_j]] = −i·J(x)_{ji}, with no ħ
     and no dilation.
   - Its double-commutator graph is exactly supp J(x), i.e. CL-6.
   - So KvN is noncommutative but **representational of CL-6** for access. It is priced by
     unearned conjugate variables λᵢ and a wavefunction state.
3. **"A Hamiltonian dilation must come before any quantization" is contradicted by the draft's own
   LF-6.** The quasi-free Lindblad semigroup quantizes e^{−Kt} directly. The relevant fact is
   non-uniqueness of physical realization, not the necessity of a dilation.

## §3 C — Seed reduction

- **What is earned:** one collocated input/output pair. That is the retained **end** site x₁ of
  K_b, the impulse probe a·e₁, the identity readout, and k(τ) = e₁ᵀe^{−K_bτ}e₁ (plus L0-1e's
  correlation kernel as a declared extension). **Multi-site coordinate functionals are not
  earned.**
- **Counterexample.** A non-injective local output g(x₁) = x₁² on the *linear* flow gives
  codistribution rank 0 at x = 0 and 3 generically, with indistinguishability x₀′ ∈ ±x₀ + 𝒰.
  So output-map choice at a single site can change the CL-3/CL-5 structure even on the linear
  core.
- **Invariant case:** injective local reparametrizations g = φᵢ (K5's covariance group) change
  neither the rank nor the quotient.
- **Narrowed wording (adopting the owner's allowed version, sharpened):**

  > Within the declared Level-0 coordinate-observable class (the identity readout at the
  > retained site), the P-6-style within-site freedom is absent. If access at a site is the
  > full local function algebra, or any injective function of xᵢ, the seed reduces to "which
  > sites". A single **non-injective** local output is a within-site seed and can change
  > CL-3/CL-5 structure even on the linear core. It must be priced, not silently identified.

## §4 D — CL-3/CL-4/CL-5 definitions (the gate for the theorem task)

**Verdict: the definitions are SOUND** for the theorem task, provided it uses the following. With
ẋ = f(x), f(x) = −K_bx − 4βx^{∘3}, forward-complete by coercivity (β ≥ 0), and output
h_A = (x_a)_{a∈A}:

- **CL-3.** Observation space 𝒪_A = span{L_fʲ x_a}. Codistribution d𝒪_A(x).
  - Rank N at x₀ ⇒ local weak observability (Hermann–Krener 1977, IS). This is **sufficient, not
    necessary.**
  - A rank drop does **not** by itself make the indistinguishability class non-trivial.
  - For analytic systems the rank is constant on an open dense set.
- **CL-5.** x I_A x′ iff h_A(φ_t x) = h_A(φ_t x′) for all t ≥ 0. For analytic, forward-complete
  systems this holds iff every φ ∈ 𝒪_A agrees (IS). **Global observability needs Φ_A to be
  injective**, not merely full rank.
- **CL-4.** There is **no input** in the earned model. Accessibility needs an added g_i, **priced
  as a declared probe datum.** The minimal choice consistent with the record is g = e₁. Then apply
  the Sussmann–Jurdjevic rank conditions (IS).
- **Required of the theorem:**
  - state A explicitly;
  - separate "rank drop" from "non-trivial I_A-class";
  - restrict to identity readouts (or injective reparametrizations).
