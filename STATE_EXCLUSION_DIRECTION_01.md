## 1. What this route is — and what it is not (the owner's distinction, recorded verbatim)

**This is not a resurrection of GR2.** GR2 established that coupling,
spin, universal reach, and the universal cone **do not follow from the
current influence core** — that adjudication stands whole and is not
relitigated here. A route in which geometry/backreaction is produced by
the *organization of an excitation* is asking a **different question**:

> **excitation → substrate organization/deformation → effective
> relational geometry → response of other excitations.**

GR2 asked whether the influence structure *selects* gravitational
structure (a selection question over supplied candidates; answer: no).
This route asks whether the *occupancy* of the substrate by persistent
structure *produces* relational deformation representable as geometry
with backreaction (a production question, with no graviton vertex, no
probe representation, and no coupling class anywhere in its statement).
None of GR2's four closed coordinates is presupposed or reopened.

## 2. The mechanism sketch (from the Level-0 discussion, held as hypothesis)

1. Persistent distinguishable structures exist in the substrate.
2. Such structures necessarily occupy **relational** states.
3. Relational distances can emerge from distinguishability structure.
4. **State exclusion:** changing the state of one region changes the
   *available states* of neighboring regions — a capacity constraint,
   not a force.
5. The large-scale relational structure admits geometric
   representation; gradients of available-state capacity act back on
   other excitations.

Gravity, if it appears here, appears as *organization → relational
deformation → effective geometry* — counting-flavored, closer in genus
to entropic/thermodynamic routes than to the influence-kernel route,
but with the program's own machinery rather than borrowed holography.

- **The counting instruments already exist and are already earned:**
  the GR-1/L6 pair-counting construction (whose S∞ = 6.2142 result
  showed the counting construction carries intrinsic geometric

- **The relational-geometry recovery machinery exists** (CA-1/G-2

  from the occupancy side rather than the influence side.
- **The failure discipline exists:** per the u0 charter's standing
  rule, this is *a constrained classification problem with explicit
  failure states — never an ontology to defend.*

## 4. What the direction must settle BEFORE any charter (the design obligations)

- **D1 — a mechanical definition of "relational deformation":** an
  operational, instrument-computable quantity (e.g., a deficit measure
  on available-state counts of a region, conditioned on neighboring
  occupancy) with its background mathematics declared. Until this
  exists, every downstream statement is UNFORMULABLE-YET.
- **D2 — a probe-free backreaction observable:** GR2-b showed the
  probe representation is unselected; this route must not quietly
  re-supply a probe. The backreaction must be read off the excitations
  the substrate already contains, or the route inherits GR2's supplied
  quadruple through the back door.
- **D3 — the no-smuggling rules carried whole:** the empirical
  firewall (no universal attraction, light bending, or Eötvös input
  inside the machinery); no member of the supplied quadruple
  {coupling class, spin, reach, cone} may enter as an assumption; any
  appearance of one in a result must be *derived and labeled*, or the
  result is void.
- **D4 — failure states named first:** at minimum — (i) the exclusion
  deficit does not compose (no additivity → no field-like object);
  (ii) the deficit composes but is not representable as geometry
  (classification: organization without space); (iii) it is geometric
  but static (no backreaction → relational geography, not gravity);
  (iv) it reproduces the influence-kernel structure exactly
  (collapse: nothing new — the route is GR2 in disguise and closes).
- **D5 — the candidate first questions** (each a bounded fork when
  execution is lawful): (a) does occupancy of a region by a persistent
  excitation measurably reduce neighboring available-state counts in
  the already-tested substrates? — a pure counting question the
  existing instruments can pose; (b) if a deficit exists, does it
  compose additively across sources and fall off with relational
  distance in any lawful way? (c) only if (a) and (b) survive: can the
  deficit field be represented as an effective metric perturbation,
  and do *other* excitations respond to its gradients without any
  supplied coupling?
