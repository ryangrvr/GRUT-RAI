# GRUT RAI as a ToE research laboratory — charter

> **Owner direction, 2026-08-23.** Persistent Ox capacity, no token or session limit. The mission
> changes: Ox is no longer the executor of one rung-3 calculation. GRUT RAI becomes a
> general-purpose physics investigation environment, and GRUT becomes one candidate point inside it.
>
> **It does not have to end with GRUT being correct.** If GRUT fails, the machinery says where and
> why. If something survives, we know exactly why. If the whole thing becomes a framework others
> can use to audit ambitious ToE proposals, that is a worthwhile outcome on its own.
>
> **Governing sentence:** *Explore without restraint; conclude without mercy.*
>
> Nothing here overrides `CHARTER.md`. The bank gate, the adversarial pre-screen and the
> overseer relay apply to everything that enters the register, at every scale.

---

## 1. Two modes, and the boundary between them is the whole design

**MODE A — EXPLORATION.** Unrestricted. Alternative derivations, relaxed assumptions, toy models,
nearby theories, counterexamples, competitor frameworks, speculative mechanisms, dead ends.
Generate freely. **Volume is not a problem in Mode A and nothing in Mode A is a claim.**

**MODE B — REALITY.** Nothing enters the scientific ledger except through:

    exploration -> candidate -> reproduction -> adversarial attack -> reality classification

### [CONTROL 1] Mode B has a fixed intake rate. Mode A does not.

**This is the control that makes the separation real.** With unlimited generation, the failure is
not bad exploration — it is that Mode A out-produces Mode B's capacity to screen, at which point
the epistemic tags become the only quality signal, and **tags are self-assigned.**

So: **Mode B accepts a bounded number of promotions per cycle, declared before the cycle starts.**
Everything not promoted stays in the exploration log permanently — that is not a loss, it is the
log doing its job. Promotion is a **recorded decision with a stated reason**, never a default for
anything that looks interesting.

## 2. Epistemic tags — mandatory on every output, in both modes

    THEOREM · DERIVED · NUMERICALLY VERIFIED · SOURCE-VERIFIED · EMPIRICALLY SUPPORTED
    CONDITIONAL · CONJECTURE · TOY · HEURISTIC · UNRESOLVED · CONTRADICTED

**A tag is a claim about evidence and is itself auditable.** `THEOREM` means a proof exists and is
exhibited. `SOURCE-VERIFIED` means the source was opened, not cited. `NUMERICALLY VERIFIED` means
re-run, with the residual stated. Mis-tagging is a defect of the same class as mis-tiering.

**Mode A output is tagged too** — mostly `CONJECTURE`, `TOY`, `HEURISTIC`. That is what keeps
exploration free: an untagged interesting observation is the thing that gets quietly promoted.

## 3. [CONTROL 2] Survival-rate control — the scale hazard nobody has named

At 100 investigations, the **aggregate** over-claim rate matters more than any individual one. If
each carries even a small chance of a subtle over-claim, running hundreds produces several — and
they will be the interesting-looking ones, because interesting is what survives screening pressure.

**Pre-register the expected survival rate, and treat a high one as evidence of screening failure
rather than of GRUT being right.**

This program's own base rate is brutal and it is the honest prior: every empirical differentiator
died (energy-basis falsifier quiet-or-faint; GW dissipation invisible; GR borrowed; w(z) needs two
modes); both root novelty candidates dissolved (the founding ζ-hypothesis, the information
principle); the register holds **zero** nodes at tier `derived`.

**Expected survival is low single digits per hundred.** If a cycle promotes 40 of 100, the screen
broke — investigate the screen, not the physics. **This rule fires in the direction the program
does not want, which is the only direction worth pre-registering.**

## 4. [CONTROL 3] The death taxonomy — 99 deaths must aggregate into knowledge

"Preserve the 99 deaths" is right and it is already this program's strength. At scale it fails a
different way: 99 deaths per cycle over many cycles is a graveyard nobody reads.

**Index every death by WHAT KILLED IT, not by what it was.** If 60 of 99 died of the same cause,
**that cause is the finding** — and it is a bigger finding than any single survivor. Candidate
causes already visible in this program's record: Planck suppression; the assumed-background import;
scope creep past a narrow true statement; the object being a state property rather than a dynamical
one; clock/frame mismatch; a check that could not fail.

The death taxonomy is a **primary deliverable**, not an archive.

## 5. The comparative layer — "what is uniquely GRUT?"

For each GRUT claim, ask what else predicts it: open quantum systems · stochastic gravity ·
semiclassical gravity · EFT · hydrodynamic memory · non-Markovian channels · de Sitter thermal
physics · influence-functional methods · the open-EFT-of-gravity program.

Four outcomes, all first-class: **standard** (already ordinary physics) · **reduces-to** (GRUT is a
limit of something known) · **equivalent** (same content, different words) · **unique remainder**
(nobody else predicts it).

### [CONTROL 4] "The unique remainder is empty" must be a first-class result

Otherwise this becomes a search for uniqueness, and a search for uniqueness finds some.

**The honest prior is that the remainder is small or empty**, and the record says so: the
information principle dissolved into standard info-RG *by a theorem* (QFI = Kubo-Mori = FDT for KMS
Gaussian states); modular theory was screened as "= KMS = rung2"; the open-EFT program already
parameterizes the same object class with a named Γ_T slot. **An empty remainder is publishable and
it is the single most useful thing this layer could establish.** Write it plainly if it lands.

## 6. Bottom-up — the constraint space, not "how do we make GRUT explain X"

Treat causality, unitarity, diffeomorphism covariance, KMS/FDT, decoherence, Lorentz symmetry,
quantum statistics and observed cosmology as **constraints on a space of theories**, and locate
GRUT as one point in it. Then ask which GRUT ingredients are **necessary** and which are **one
possible construction**.

### [CONTROL 5] Read the existing program before reinventing it

This is reverse mathematics for physics, and **it already exists**: Carcassi & Aidala, *Reverse
Physics* / Assumptions of Physics (Found. Phys. 52:40, 2022) — already flagged in this repo as
prior art `method_novelty` has **never been screened against**, with a published two-axis tier
vocabulary for underived physical inputs.

**Start this track by reading it. Not by rebuilding it.** Report what it already does, what it does
not, and what is left for us. Reverse mathematics itself (Friedman/Simpson, the Big Five) is the
mathematical parent and does the price-of-a-theorem job *better than a blind ledger sum*, because a
reversal proves the price is exact.

## 7. The Generic ToE Lab — mostly already built, and unbooked

    GRUT RAI
      |- theory-specific: GRUT ontology / claims / equations
      |- generic lab: claim registry · derivation engine · source verification ·
         numerical validation · adversarial screening · dependency graph ·
         reality audit · experimental bridge · comparative-theory layer

### [CONTROL 6] This is a FINISHING job, not a building job — and it owes a screen first

`../claimledger` **already is** the domain-free engine, MIT, pip-installable, with **two**
foreign-corpus runs — and this register books only the weaker one. The stronger (Nowak,
arXiv:2608.06147, q-bio.PE) had its selection rule pre-committed before fetch, the encoder's first
read was the encoding read, and no substitutions were permitted. **It is unbooked.**

And `method_novelty`'s falsifier is **armed** for exactly this: *"REFUTED as even WEAKLY-novel IF
the full assembly … is shown to be an existing named/deployed system."* The screen against formal
methods — Metamath set.mm (47,736 theorems, CI-enforced axiom ledgers since 1992), Lean's
`#print axioms`, reverse mathematics, the RM Zoo, and the Douglas stack (contemporaneous, in
physics, all five components) — has never been run.

**Run that screen before building generic-lab software.** Building a generic ToE lab without it
risks rebuilding something that has existed for three decades.

## 8. Order of work

1. **The reality audit** (`REALITY_AUDIT_BRIEF.md`) — you cannot map a landscape from an unmapped
   starting point, and its load-bearing map tells you which claims are worth comparative work.
2. **The expensive things, while capacity is free.** Wall A (graviton-probe assembly) and wall B
   (the RG half). **Computed and reported, NOT banked** — `CHARTER.md` §3 forbids banking an
   in-house resolution of the frontier; it does not forbid attempting one. That distinction is now
   load-bearing. Documents stay cheap forever; a wall attempt does not.
3. **The comparative layer** — because "what is uniquely GRUT" is the biggest unanswered question
   in the program and it needs no new physics.
4. **The `derived` column** — twenty `to-derive` nodes, zero at `derived`. Attempt them. Most will
   die; deaths feed §4.
5. **The generic lab**, after §7's screen.

## 9. The question every investigation ends with

> **What did we learn about nature, and what did we merely learn about our model?**

Answered explicitly, every time. Most investigations will honestly answer "about our model" — and
that is the sentence that keeps a laboratory from becoming an echo.

## 10. Standing fences

- Every claim entering the register still passes the bank gate, the adversarial pre-screen and the
  overseer relay. **Scale does not relax this; it is what scale is most likely to erode.**
- CHARTER §1.4 applies hardest where capacity is largest.
- Self-audit cannot discharge the external-validation debt. `claims.json` records that no outside
  human has ever been contacted by this program. That remains true no matter how many tokens are
  spent, and it should appear in any deposit.
- Do not conclude GRUT is correct because many things survive, or wrong because many die. **The
  deliverable is the map.**
