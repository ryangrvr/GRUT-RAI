## 0. The question (the owner's, frozen verbatim)

> **Can a universal causal cone emerge from the earned
> influence/response/access structure, or must the cone itself be
> supplied as an additional primitive?**

Equivalently, the stronger internal form:

> **Does the GRUT core itself produce the causal structure, or does it
> require causal structure as an input?**

Bindings from the ruling:

1. *The critical distinction:* **causal propagation exists ≠ a
   universal causal cone is selected.** Exhibiting fronts, supports,
   or cross-sector signal transfer is never by itself evidence of cone
   selection.
2. *The most important gate:* **if two earned-admissible systems have
   different causal cones, then universal causality is not derived.**
3. *The converse discipline:* a common cone must **never** be accepted
   as selected merely because the tested family happened to share one —
   a SELECTED outcome requires an earned discriminator that rejects the
   alternatives (implemented in the outcome rule: D-CONE-SELECTED
   demands that **every** different-cone row be eliminated by an earned
   test).
4. *The empirical firewall:* the light-speed/Lorentz eliminators
   (Michelson–Morley-type constancy, observed Lorentz invariance) stay
   **outside** the earned result — recorded as external constraints
   that would eliminate branches, never used to manufacture an internal
   derivation.

## 2. Elimination discipline (frozen)

A system is **eliminated** only if an earned *admissibility* test fails
on it: pair-kernel positivity $J(w) \ge 0$, ground-state two-time Gram
PSD, interaction-graph consistency, the memory battery (positivity +
decay). Measured front speeds, arrival times, and supports are
*labels*, never eliminators. Empirical light-speed/Lorentz data are
outside the machinery entirely (§0.4).

  any of them; exchange composes supplied cones into averages without

  consequences of the generative core. M-3's missing link is confirmed

  eliminated by an earned test — the owner's converse condition: family
  coincidence is never selection; only an earned discriminator that
  rejects the alternatives can select.

  never physics; no verdict) on any RM/RL/RN/QI control miss.
