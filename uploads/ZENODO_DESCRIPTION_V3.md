# Grand Responsive Universe Theory (GRUT) — Theory of Everything, Version 3

## What GRUT v3 Is

The Grand Responsive Universe Theory (GRUT) treats the gravitational vacuum as a responsive medium rather than an inert stage. Version 3 reduces the framework to its load-bearing core and states every result at the standing it has actually earned. In one line: **GRUT is general relativity's long-wavelength rescaling redundancy, broken in a controlled way by exactly one finite memory scale.** A theory is defined by *which* symmetry it breaks and by *how much*; GRUT breaks the adiabatic spatial-dilatation redundancy of general relativity by a single memory length L₀ = c·τ₀ ≈ 12.85 Mpc (τ₀ ≈ 41.9 Myr) — the same shape by which a particle mass breaks scale invariance.

The framework rests on three organizing structures. **Q** is closed-time-path (CTP / in-in) unitarity: physics is the response to *realized differences*, and the influence action vanishes on the diagonal, S_IF[φ₊ = φ₋] = 0 (proven — a theorem of the formalism). **F** is a finite single-pole vacuum memory, χ(ω) = 1/(1 − iωτ₀) — causal, bounded, and general-relativity-recovering at high frequency (postulated; one scale, τ₀). **D** is the adiabatic-dilatation redundancy of general relativity. The organizing principle is that **F breaks D**, in a controlled way at order (L₀k)² and non-anomalously, and that breaking is made observable through Q.

Above the two pillars sits exactly one dimensionless input: the vacuum impedance **α = 1/3**. v3 is explicit that this is an *adopted axiom*, not a first-principles result. There is a proven conditional theorem behind it — if the gravitational conformal mode is the infrared carrier of the vacuum's response, then the central-charge ratio a/c = 1/3 follows rigorously (from a = 1/360 and c = 1/120, via Komargodski–Schwimmer) — but the *antecedent* of that theorem, the Riegert/Paneitz identification, remains open. The framework states the wager plainly and names its closure condition.

From this single broken symmetry, the universe's large-scale behaviour follows as a *requirement* rather than a fit. Linear cosmology is forced to be exactly ΛCDM: the vacuum cannot respond to a pure long-wavelength rescaling, so the linear growth modification collapses to μ_linear = 1 (a no-go theorem, not an approximation). The once-hoped linear modified-gravity signal (μ → 4/3) is therefore forbidden — and is independently excluded by CMB–ISW data at 32σ. The cosmological constant appears as a *terminal velocity*, the balance of a Gibbons–Hawking instability drive against memory-kernel friction, giving a dark-energy fraction Ω_Λ = 0.6886 (anchored; Planck 2018: 0.6889).

v3 is built to be killed. Its sharpest test is a tabletop one: a gravitational-decoherence plateau near **689 Hz**, fixed by the theory and measurable in a nanoparticle interferometer. It predicts the **normal neutrino mass ordering** (forced by a Z₃ flavour structure anchored to the Koide relation K = 2/3), so a confirmed inverted ordering would refute it. The framework also *recovers* physics it does not assume: standard quantum mechanics emerges from the same constitutive law in the zero-memory limit (the Schrödinger equation as the τ → 0 face of the responsive vacuum), and the arrow of time follows from a non-negative constitutive entropy-production rate — the Second Law as a consequence of the CTP retarded-kernel structure rather than a separate postulate.

The defining result of v3 is a *negative* one, honestly reported. **GRUT has no derived dark-matter mechanism.** The most sophisticated candidate — the second-order Weyl-squared (W²) response — clears every test but one: it has the right magnitude at galactic scales but a radial profile that falls far too steeply (1/r⁴ steepening to 1/r⁶) to reproduce the flat 1/r² rotation curves galaxies show. This is not an artefact of approximation but a *locality theorem*: flattening the profile would require an inverse-Laplacian (1/∇²) operator that a local, causal kernel cannot contain — the same locality that fixes the scale L₀ forbids it. Dark matter is therefore recorded as a *hosted input*, an empirical boundary condition the theory accepts, not a success it claims.

Every statement in v3 is carried by **GRUT-RAI**, the "Responsive AI" research platform that implements the theory as runnable code rather than prose alone. Each quantitative claim is a structured record in a machine-checked registry (116 claims): the physical statement, the tier it has earned, the code that computes it, the tests that check it, its dependencies, and the single observation that would falsify it. The framework is held to account by **3,227 passing tests**, with enforcement tests guaranteeing that no claim is left unregistered, untested, or undocumented; every gap is a live entry in an open-question ledger (**28 open negatives**), each with an explicit closure condition. Claims are graded on a transparent five-tier scale — *proven, postulated, anchored, conjectural, open* — and the registry, tests, and ledger are a single source of truth from which the editions and the live platform all draw.

v3 is best understood as a *foundation*, not a finished theory of everything. It is deliberately defined more by what it forbids than by what it predicts: each refutation is a constraint the framework imposed on itself, and the surviving core is small and hard. The recurring signature of the edition is that **the mathematics usually survives while the ontology changes.** The honest cost — fewer claimed successes than earlier work — is repaid in falsifiability: GRUT v3 makes precise, refutable statements across laboratory, particle-physics, and cosmological scales, and says, on every line, exactly how confident it is.

## GRUT v2 → v3: What Changed

GRUT **Version 2** (June 2026) was an ambitious, wide-scope synthesis. **Version 3** is the result of a full *adversarial re-audit* of that work: roughly seventy inherited claims were re-examined and re-tiered, and wherever v2 over-claimed, v3 demotes and says so. The mathematics usually survived the audit; what changed was the ontology — and the honesty of the claims. The cost of honesty is the gain in falsifiability.

**What v3 retracts or demotes from v2:**

- **Linear "dielectric" dark matter / Ω_dm = 1/3** — *refuted.*
- **The linear modified-gravity enhancement (μ → 4/3)** — *refuted* (excluded at 32σ by CMB–ISW).
- **A derived dark-matter mechanism** — *withdrawn.* v3 proves by a locality theorem that no local, causal mechanism of this kind exists, and records dark matter as a hosted input.
- **"The Standard Model is the unique minimal theory"** — *demoted* to "the SM is *consistent* with five GRUT-derived structural constraints" (necessary, not sufficient).
- **α = 1/3 as a derived quantity** — *reclassified* as an adopted axiom with a proven conditional theorem and an open antecedent.
- **Black-hole curvature saturation** — *demoted to conjectural* (the values follow arithmetically from {α, τ₀}; the saturation mechanism is unproven beyond linear order).

**What v3 adds:**

- An explicit **five-tier discipline** (proven / postulated / anchored / conjectural / open) applied to every claim.
- A **machine-checked claim registry** (116 claims) and a 1:1 **open-question ledger** (28 open negatives), each with stated closure conditions.
- The **locality theorem** that turns the dark-matter result from "not found yet" into "provably absent for any local, causal kernel."
- **Recovered-physics sections** — quantum mechanics from the constitutive law, and the arrow of time from entropy production.

**Net effect:** v3 claims fewer successes than v2 but is far more falsifiable and internally consistent. It is a foundation defined by what it forbids — the smaller, harder core on which the full theory is meant to be built.
