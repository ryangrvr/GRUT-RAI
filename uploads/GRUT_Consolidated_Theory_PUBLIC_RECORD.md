## Abstract

The Grand Responsive Universe Theory (GRUT) is a research program. It asks
whether a bottom-up class of local microscopic models can generate continuum,
influence, geometric, and gravitationally relevant effective structures,
without taking those structures as primitive.

Its central surviving result is a dependency-resolved hierarchy:

$$
\mathcal O_{\rm local}\longrightarrow\mathcal C_{\rm continuum}\longrightarrow\mathcal I\longrightarrow\mathfrak A\longrightarrow\mathcal G\longrightarrow\mathcal F_{\rm eff}\longrightarrow\mathcal G_{\rm grav}
$$

Here the access structure $\mathfrak A$ is not just one link in the chain. It
is a cross-cutting condition, consumed in three places:

- upstream, in subsystem selection;
- throughout reconstruction and decidability;
- at access-boundary events, where otherwise hidden distinctions become
  observable.

**The continuum and memory result.** The strongest constructive result holds
within the admitted model classes. There, strictly local finite microscopic
dynamics generate continuous spectral structure, persistent memory, and
effective irreversible response under stated limits. This demonstrates a
mechanism within the tested class. It does not establish that the physical
universe is uniquely described by one such substrate.

Finite elimination has an exact spectral representation:

$$
K(t)=\sum_k (v_{1k})^2e^{\lambda_k t},\qquad \operatorname{Re}\lambda_k<0 .
$$

In the passive stationary class, the infinite-volume limit gives a positive
spectral measure:

$$
K(t)=\int e^{-t/\tau}\,d\rho(\tau).
$$

The continuum-origin computation passed all six of its checks. It recorded
the verdict `continuum_emergent_from_local_finite_ontology`, and it showed
that substrate dimensionality selects the late-time memory class.

**The influence result.** The effective interface is not a single response
kernel. In the declared Gaussian class, realizable two-point influence data
obey

$$
\mathfrak C_{\rm Gauss}=\{(J,\nu):J(\omega)\ge0,\ \nu(\omega)\ge\hbar J(\omega)/2\}.
$$

However, $(K,N)$ is not the complete interface. Higher connected cumulants
distinguish systems that have identical two-point data. The full influence
moment hierarchy is complete as an interface within the declared class and
access assumptions. Its positivity reduces to ordinary state positivity, so
this is not a new derivation of quantum mechanics.

**The geometry result.** Geometry is reconstructed, not primitive.
Sufficiently rich influence and access data recover dimension, hop metrics,
and metric density. They can also eliminate genuinely different geometries,
including isospectral ones. Dynamic influence data separate geometries that
static data cannot. Under restricted access, geometry is underdetermined; the
single-site degeneracy and the topology horizon are structural limits.

**The gravity result.** The route from the generative core to a
gravitational identification has been attacked link by link. The resulting
branch is sharply typed but conditional. The following remain supplied:

- a massless, gauge/Lorentz-redundant probe structure;
- universal reach;
- a co-stretch declaration;
- a cross-sector causal (light-cone) relation.

Within this conditional branch, the retained gravitational-side sector is
constrained to a gapless, $z=1$, locally accessible Goldstone-like class; the
exact member and its dispersion curvature remain open. The within-class
influence exponent $\omega^7$, in the program's declared convention, has a
two-sided analytic-versus-instrument confirmation. It is explicitly **not**
promoted to a theory-wide prediction, and the Class-4 (forcing) derivation
remains open.

**The achievement, and its scope.** GRUT does not claim to have derived all
of physics. Its present achievement is narrower and more exact: repeated,
counterexample-driven attacks have reduced a diffuse set of assumptions to a
small inventory of named primitive inputs, domain data, unresolved seams, and
preserved red gates. The completion criterion lets a primitive remain
primitive, provided a principled irreducibility result certifies that status.

**The latest result: the stationarity seam.**

- **C1-a** extended the microscopic construction to finite stepped
  nonstationary dynamics. The reduction is exact to $10^{-12}$ and reduces
  continuously to the stationary case.
- **C1-a2** re-tested the packaging boundary under a defensible
  normalization and passed 7/7. At that scope, no single spectral measure
  and no $\Delta t$-only kernel packages the generated $K(t,s)$. The
  replacement is a two-time spectral datum.
- This closes the finite stepped packaging item of seam S1. The
  infinite-volume and smooth-modulation problems remain open.

# 1. Scope, epistemic discipline, and status vocabulary

GRUT is maintained under a source-first rule: **computational checks outrank
narrative prose**. A claim does not become stronger because a later document
describes it more confidently.

The binding status vocabulary:

- **[IDENTITY]** — an exact mathematical identity. It carries content only as a classification.
- **[DERIVED-IN-CLASS]** — established by a frozen instrument within a
  declared model class, at stated tolerances.
- **[FAMILY-FACT]** — demonstrated across a tested family, not promoted to a
  general theorem.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** — restricted by the evidence, but not uniquely selected.
- **[SUPPLIED]** — a primitive input or boundary datum the program does not derive.
- **[DOMAIN DATUM]** — state, boundary, or regime information needed to define the problem.
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — open.
- **[RED]** — a frozen gate failed. It stays failed until a separately
  chartered measurement changes it.
- **[NULL-AS-NEW-PRINCIPLE]** — a proposed foundational principle that
  reduced to an existing identity or positivity condition.
- **Owner ruling** — a project-level adjudication recording how a result is
  admitted, demoted, fenced, or incorporated. It is not a theorem, a
  peer-review finding, or an experimental confirmation.

The central methodological rule:

> A failure is a result. It is never repaired by changing the interpretation after the run.

A conditional result also stays conditional. A successful calculation does
not discharge the assumptions that made it possible.

Numerical values and gate outcomes should be read against their repository
artifacts. Appendix G indexes every campaign fork by commit and battery
score. The repository remains the authoritative location for executable code,
frozen thresholds, raw outputs, diagnostics, and the issue-level adjudication
record (Issue #2).

3. **The choice between medium and relational language is not fundamental.**
   At fixed influence data and restricted access, different microscopic
   ontologies are representationally equivalent.

What remains is a **dependency-resolved theory of generative structure**, not
a single guessed field equation.

# 3. The formal architecture

## 3.1 The seven principal objects

1. **$\mathcal O_{\rm local}$ — local microscopic dynamics.** Admitted
   classes of local finite dynamical systems. No unique substrate ontology is
   selected.
2. **$\mathcal C_{\rm continuum}$ — continuum structure.** The spectral and
   memory structure obtained by eliminating hidden modes and taking limits.
3. **$\mathcal I$ — the influence hierarchy.** Response, noise, and higher
   connected influence cumulants of the retained sector.
4. **$\mathfrak A$ — access.** A seed subalgebra, its dynamically generated
   closure, and the events that change the access boundary. Access is
   cross-cutting, not a stage.
5. **$\mathcal G$ — geometry.** Geometry reconstructed from influence and
   access data by spectral, resistance, metric-density, and walk-count
   functionals: geometry *as far as access reaches*.
6. **$\mathcal F_{\rm eff}$ — effective sectors and probes.** Sector classes
   inside the influence cone, retained-sector candidates, and the probe
   structures that couple to them.
7. **$\mathcal G_{\rm grav}$ — gravitational response.** The conditional
   gravitational influence point, the TT response, channel conditions, and
   the exponent class.

## 3.2 The cross-cutting role of access

A more accurate drawing is the chain

$$
\mathcal O_{\rm local}\rightarrow\mathcal C_{\rm continuum}\rightarrow\mathcal I\rightarrow\mathcal G\rightarrow\mathcal F_{\rm eff}\rightarrow\mathcal G_{\rm grav},
$$

with $\mathfrak A$ crossing all of it. Access is consumed in three roles:

1. **Partition** — it determines which subsystem or retained sector exists.
2. **Reach** — it determines which distinctions can be reconstructed.
3. **Boundary events** — changing access turns hidden differences into
   observable ones.

In the tested class, the closure of an access seed is canonical, but the seed
itself is not dynamically selected.

In summary:

- the **form** of the representation is derived in class;
- the **content** of $\rho$ is supplied, including its support and
  macroscopic scales such as $\tau_0$.

# 8. Layer III — access as a physical interface

## 8.4 Medium versus relational descriptions (D-1; 13/13)

11-dimensional chain bath with hidden decoupled modes. Their influence data
are identical, and every S-local observable agrees to $4.5\times10^{-15}$,

Conversely, two theories with the same kernel but different noise data split
observably, by 0.91. At fixed accessible influence data, the question
"medium or relational?" is therefore representational. What is physical is
the constraint structure on realizable influence data.
