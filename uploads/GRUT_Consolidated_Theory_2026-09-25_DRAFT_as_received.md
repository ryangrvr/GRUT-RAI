## Abstract

The Grand Responsive Universe Theory (GRUT) is a research program investigating whether a bottom-up class of local microscopic models can generate continuum, influence, geometric, and gravitationally relevant effective structures without taking those structures as primitive. Its central surviving result is a dependency-resolved hierarchy:

\[
\mathcal O_{\rm local}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal C_{\rm continuum}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal I
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak A
\longrightarrow
\mathcal G
\longrightarrow
\mathcal F_{\rm eff}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal G_{\rm grav},
\]

with an important qualification: the access structure \(\mathfrak A\) is not merely one link in the chain. It is a cross-cutting condition consumed upstream in subsystem selection, throughout reconstruction and decidability, and at access-boundary events where otherwise hidden distinctions become physically observable.

The strongest constructive result is that, within the admitted model classes, strictly local finite microscopic dynamics can generate, under stated limits, continuous spectral structure, persistent memory, and effective irreversible response. This demonstrates a constructive mechanism within the tested class; it does not establish that the physical universe is uniquely described by one such microscopic substrate. The finite microscopic elimination problem has an exact spectral representation,

\[
K(t)=\sum_k (v_{1k})^2e^{\lambda_k t},
\]

and the infinite-volume limit produces a positive spectral measure,

\[
K(t)=\int e^{-t/\tau}\,d\rho(\tau),
\]

within the demonstrated passive stationary class. Continuum and memory were also obtained from strictly local nearest-neighbor substrates, with dimensionality controlling the late-time memory class. The final continuum-origin campaign passed its four principal branches and was recorded as `continuum_emergent_from_local_finite_ontology`.

The second major result is that the effective interface is not a single response kernel. In the declared Gaussian class, realizable two-point influence data obey

\[
\mathfrak C_{\rm Gauss}
=
\{(J,\nu):J(\omega)\ge0,\ \nu(\omega)\ge\hbar J(\omega)/2\},
\]

but \((K,N)\) is not the complete physical interface: higher connected influence cumulants can distinguish systems with identical two-point data. The full influence moment hierarchy is complete as an interface **within the declared class and access assumptions**: matching the hierarchy certifies representational equivalence in that class. Its positivity reduces to ordinary state positivity, so this completeness result does not constitute a new derivation of quantum mechanics.

Geometry is likewise not primitive. Sufficiently rich influence and access data can recover dimension, hop metrics, metric density, and distinguish genuinely different geometries within access reach. Dynamic influence data can distinguish geometries that static geometry cannot. At restricted access, however, geometry becomes underdetermined; one-site and sub-circumference topology horizons are structural limits, not mere numerical failures. Thus GRUT presently supports geometry as an access-relative reconstruction.

The campaign also systematically attacked the route from the generative core to a gravitational identification. The gravitational branch is now sharply typed, but remains conditional. A massless gauge/Lorentz-redundant probe structure, universal reach, a co-stretch convention, and a cross-sector causal/light-cone relation remain supplied or unresolved inputs. Within the resulting conditional branch, the retained gravitational-side sector is constrained to a gapless, \(z=1\), locally accessible Goldstone-like class in the tested family; the exact member and dispersion curvature remain open. The non-aligned acoustic channel is open under the tested conditions, and the within-class influence exponent \(\omega^7\) has strong occupancy evidence and conditional derivation within the tested coupling class, but it is explicitly **not** promoted to an absolute theory-wide prediction and the Class-4 derivation remains open.

The theory therefore does not claim that all of physics has been derived. Its current achievement is more precise: repeated counterexample-driven attacks have reduced a diffuse collection of assumptions to a small set of explicitly identified primitive inputs, domain data, unresolved seams, and preserved red gates. GRUT's completion criterion permits a primitive to remain primitive when a principled irreducibility result establishes that status.

The latest stationarity result is especially important. C1-a established the finite stepped extension of the microscopic construction. C1-a2 then independently re-tested the packaging boundary using a defensible local-scale normalization. It passed 7/7 with zero failures and zero halts. At finite stepped level and within the declared model class, no single \(\Delta t\)-only kernel or single spectral measure packages the generated nonstationary \(K(t,s)\). The replacement is a two-time spectral datum consisting of per-epoch spectral information together with mixing data between epoch eigenspaces. This closes the **finite stepped packaging item of S1**, while leaving the infinite-volume nonstationary limit and smooth-modulation problems open.

This document is a consolidated research record, not a claim of completed empirical validation. Every result is scoped to its demonstrated class, and every failed or unresolved gate remains visible.

# 1. Scope, epistemic discipline, and status vocabulary

GRUT is maintained under a strict source-first rule. Computational checks outrank narrative prose. A claim cannot become stronger merely because a later document describes it more confidently.

The binding status vocabulary is:

- **[IDENTITY]** — exact mathematical identity.
- **[DERIVED-IN-CLASS]** — established constructively within a frozen model class and stated tolerance.
- **[FAMILY-FACT]** — demonstrated across the tested family but not promoted to a universal theorem.
- **[CONSTRAINED]** — restricted by the evidence but not uniquely selected.
- **[SUPPLIED]** — an explicit primitive or boundary datum not derived by the present program.
- **[DOMAIN DATUM]** — state, boundary, or regime information required to define the problem.
- **[UNRESOLVED]** — currently open.
- **[RED]** — a frozen gate failed and remains failed until a separately chartered measurement changes it.
- **[NULL-AS-NEW-PRINCIPLE]** — a proposed foundational principle reduced to an existing identity, positivity condition, or representational statement rather than yielding new physics.
- **Owner ruling** — a project-level adjudication recording how a result is admitted, demoted, fenced, or incorporated into the theory record; it is not a mathematical theorem, peer-review finding, or experimental confirmation.

A central methodological rule is:

> A failure is a result. It is never repaired by changing the interpretation after the run.

Likewise, a conditional result remains conditional. The existence of a successful numerical calculation does not discharge the assumptions that made that calculation possible.


For public-record use, numerical values, gate outcomes, and status changes should be read against their underlying repository artifacts. This consolidation names the relevant campaign, charter, result artifact, or formalization where practical; the repository remains the authoritative location for executable code, frozen thresholds, raw numerical outputs, diagnostics, and issue-level adjudication. A project-level owner ruling records the program's disposition of an evidence item; it does not convert that item into an externally established scientific fact.

# 2. What GRUT currently claims

The present GRUT proposal is best stated as follows:

> **The present GRUT result is a dependency-resolved construction: within the admitted microscopic classes, local dynamics can generate continuum spectral structure, memory, effective dissipation, and a representation-complete influence hierarchy within the declared model and access class; declared access determines which distinctions are recoverable, and sufficiently rich access can reconstruct tested graph/substrate geometry from the declared accessible influence functionals. The program has not established a unique microscopic ontology or shown that every physical sector of the universe must instantiate this construction.**

This is deliberately weaker and more precise than early GRUT formulations.

Three major demotions were forced by the campaign:

1. **The constitutive kernel is not fundamental.**
   A kernel is downstream of subsystem/sector selection, state information, and access. The full influence hierarchy is richer than \((K,N)\), and a single kernel therefore cannot serve as the foundation.

2. **Stationarity is not fundamental.**
   Exact nonstationary calculations produce genuinely two-time kernels. A stationary \(K(t-t')\) is therefore a regime-specific representation rather than the universal primitive.

3. **Medium versus relational language is not fundamental.**
   At fixed influence data and restricted access, different microscopic ontologies can be representationally equivalent. What becomes physical is the difference in accessible influence data, particularly when access boundaries change.

The theory that remains is therefore a **dependency-resolved theory of generative structure**, not a single guessed field equation.
