Im Σ_R oddness lives in the **sgn(ω) continuation, not in the power** — an H·ω³ absorptive term
with the odd continuation is parity-allowed. Parity forbids only a *real ω³ local*, which is

## The strongest candidate: curvature evenness

The background enters covariant organization through **curvature, and dS curvature is O(H²)**
(R = 12H²): every covariant local invariant is even in H. This is **registered-adjacent** — the
D5/1b counterterm basis is {ω⁰, ω², ω⁴}, with no odd slot. What is **unproven** is the nonlocal
half: that the full kernel organizes covariantly is exactly the kind of assumption the record's
F7 discipline forbids assuming.

## Proof obligation (constructed, not discharged)

Show that through first order in H the assembled non-TT self-energy admits a
covariant-in-curvature organization, **and** that the declared adiabatic state contributes no
O(H) term. Then H¹ = 0 follows from curvature evenness. Both halves are open; the second is
where the counterexample lives.

in H, an initial-time/boundary term at u_b, or a chart-dependent subtraction — would
