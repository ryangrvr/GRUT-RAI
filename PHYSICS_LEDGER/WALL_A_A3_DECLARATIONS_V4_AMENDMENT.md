## The exact defect (exhibited by the a1282af diagnostic, computed not asserted)

The literal wording has **one-sided false negatives**: it classifies genuinely LOCAL
finite-derivative kernels as nonlocal. On the diagnostic's 7-structure census:

- a local kernel containing the mixed-odd structure ω·k (the Fourier transform of
  ∂_t∂_z δ — support at coincidence only, local by definition) FAILS the literal
  (ω², k²) wording;
- a local kernel with a single odd power ω (the transform of i∂_t δ) likewise FAILS;
- every tested NONLOCAL structure (log branch cut, ω⁴/k², arctan, ωk·log(ω²+k²)) fails
  BOTH the literal and the corrected predicate — the literal rule's error direction is
  "too narrow, never too permissive" (0 false positives).
The defect bites on the real object: the Phase-12 pole census found mixed-odd monomials
(ωk, ωk³, ω³k) in 28/112 terms at H⁰, 40/40 at H¹, 8/56 at H² — all of them
reflection-covariant term by term (0 violations) and generated by the validated
local-kernel E-transform ((−iω)^q for any q) of the frozen basis construction itself.
The literal reading would forbid subtracting real counterterms — it is the frozen
basis's OWN local kernel structures that it excludes.

## The mathematical locality definition (fixed independently of any pole output)

A momentum-space kernel is LOCAL iff its position-space preimage is a finite sum of
derivatives of the delta distribution (support at coincidence only). The Fourier
algebra: that class is EXACTLY the polynomials in the momentum components — each
monomial ω^a k^b is the transform of (i∂_t)^a (i∂_z)^b δ — with **no parity
restriction anywhere**. Parity enters only through the background: on a parity-even,
Lorentz-invariant flat background only even powers arise, which is why (ω², k²)
suffices THERE and why the narrow form was written (a checker defect made at the A3
freeze gate, 2026-08-25, recorded as such at a1282af). A background with a preferred
time direction and a reference slice admits ∂_t∂_z structures; the restriction is not
general.

## The corrected predicate (v4, binding from this date)

**Π_local^scheme:** local terms are **finite polynomials in the external momentum
components (ω, k)** — non-negative integer exponents, no logs, denominators, or branch
functions of (ω, k) — with coefficients arbitrary FINITE functions of (m², H², μ).
Equivalently: position-space finite sums of derivatives of delta functions. Everything
not so expressible is nonlocal by declaration and may not be subtracted. The
coefficient-function clause of v1 (including log(m²/μ²)-type coefficient logs being
LOCAL) is unchanged.

## Why the correction does not absorb nonlocal structures by construction

The correction widens only the LOCAL side (odd powers of ω, k are now admissible
derivative structures). Every nonlocality witness fails BOTH wordings, because a
nonlocal object carries a log, an inverse power, or a branch function **of (ω, k)**
itself: ω²·log k² and ω⁴/k² (the hostile cases named in v1's own frozen text),
log(ω²+m²), log(−ω²+k²+m²) (branch cut), atan-type thresholds. No structure lacking
derivative-of-delta position-space support gains local status under the corrected
predicate: the class remains exactly the finite-derivative delta-kernels.
