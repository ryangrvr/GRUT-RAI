## §2 The predicate registry (the anti-instrument-artifact rule)

The sweep certifies necessity **per property, never per instrument**.
Declared emergence-class predicates, each with an instrument-independent
operationalization required at freeze time:

- **P_continuum** — the finite→continuum limiting behavior (the C1
  class; the L6 finite-size-law grade).
- **P_memory** — finite-memory-grade decay of the retained-site kernel
  (decay-grade classification on a declared τ window: exponential vs
  algebraic, by a frozen fit-discrimination rule — never a threshold on
  a single number).
- **P_positivity** — the earned cone/Gram structure (J(w) ≥ 0;
  ground-state two-time Gram PSD).
- **P_geometry** — recoverable spatial structure (graph/distance
  recovery at the CA-1/G-2 grade).
- **P_exact-reduction** — the eigen-reduction machinery itself.

**Frozen rule (the owner's):** P_exact-reduction ≠ P_emergence. An
assumption failing only because the *chosen measurement instrument*
cannot operate without it certifies nothing about the world. Every
deletion member is evaluated by phenomenon batteries that do not
presuppose the deleted structure; where no such battery exists yet, the
verdict is UNFORMULABLE-YET, never NECESSARY.

**Outcome vocabulary (per assumption × property):**
NECESSITY-CERTIFIED · REMOVABLE · SPLIT (property-dependent) ·
UNFORMULABLE-YET. Plus the standing scope line on every record:
*within declared background mathematics and the tested construction
class* — removal of aᵢ destroys property P, or does not; never "the
axioms of reality."

  > destroys finite-memory-grade decay, while removal of passivity

  is presupposed in the other's instrument.

  does not certify for P_memory/P_positivity, once the phenomenon
  batteries are instrument-separated per §2.

## §4 The deletion-instrument designs (sketches; frozen only at reopening)

**D-GAP (priority 1a).** The C1-a chain with the pin scanned toward
deletion: PIN ∈ {0.3 (replication anchor: the sealed kernels
0.1594754/0.3579003…), 0.1, 0.03, 0.01, 0}. Analytic structure already
readable from the committed `build_K`: the bath spectrum's floor is set
by the pin, so the kernel's decay grade is the exposed quantity.
Battery: P_memory decay-grade classification on a declared τ window;

the deletion; the scan is the approach (the L-g precedent: watch the
property degrade continuously or fail structurally, and say which).

**D-PASS (priority 1b, separate).** Passivity deletion without gap
deletion: one interior coupling driven negative at declared magnitudes,
keeping the pin. Battery: P_positivity (J(w), Gram PSD) — predicted by
H1 to fail; P_memory evaluated independently. Halt-grade identity: the
member's spectrum computed and its non-passive eigenvalue disclosed on
the face (an unstable substrate is a *finding surface*, not an error).

**D-LOC (priority 2).** Nonlocal members at fixed total coupling norm:
(i) power-law K_ij ∝ 1/d^α with α scanned from short-range to
effectively all-to-all; (ii) a sparse random long-range admixture.
Batteries: P_memory and P_positivity (H2: survive); P_geometry at the
CA-1/G-2 grade (H2: fails or trivializes as the graph completes — the
recovered geometry *is* the interaction structure). The design must
pre-commit the geometry-failure criterion (e.g., recovered-distance
degeneracy measure) so "space disappears" is a gate, not a narrative.

**D-LIN (priority 3, two instruments by construction).** An anharmonic
on-site term (βq⁴) added to the C1 chain at declared β. Instrument A:
the eigen-reduction — expected inapplicable (that is the
P_exact-reduction result, stated as such). Instrument B: the
phenomenon battery — direct time-domain two-time correlation of the
retained site, decay-grade classified without any eigen machinery.
H3 lives or dies on Instrument B alone.

**D-ORD / D-HERM / D-DET (priority 4 — formulability first).** These
are not yet deletions; they are *formulability obligations*, each its
own design fork before any charter:
- **D-ORD:** a t-free presentation of a substrate (constraint/fixed-point

  any ordering appearing in a Layer-0 instrument must be derived and
