## 1. The label glossary (frozen; the owner's nine grades)

These grades are the campaign's finding — more informative than a
binary "gravity works/doesn't work":

- **derived** — forced by the earned core alone.
- **derived-in-class** — forced within a stated model class, from earned
  structure alone.
- **conditionally derived** — forced once named supplied structure is
  granted (the condition is part of the statement).
- **supplied** — an input the record grants and does not obtain.
- **compatible** — passes every earned test; passing is not selection.
- **not selected** — an earned survivor count over alternatives came
  back with zero eliminations at the tested coordinate.
- **not forced** — same, for a constraint (equality/universality) rather
  than a choice.
- **demonstrated counterexample** — an explicit earned-admissible system
  realizing the alternative.
- **open** — not adjudicated either way.

  non-universal assignments eliminated; forcing exists only in the

  component membership is itself supplied. *Not forced.*

  ω⁷ = conditional occupancy evidence, not selected by the generative

## 8. What this synthesis does NOT say (frozen; the two forbidden inferences)

Per the authorizing ruling, this synthesis does **not** convert "the
core does not force universal causality" into either:

1. *"therefore GRUT predicts no universal causality"* — the core is
   **compatible** with a universal cone; it simply does not select
   one; or
2. *"therefore universal causality can simply be supplied"* — whether
   supplying it is legitimate, economical, or terminal for the program
   is not a question these experiments answered.

**Both would exceed the experiments.** Likewise: the demonstrated
distinct quantum cones do **not** disprove the universal cone as an
empirical law — the empirical light-speed/Lorentz constraints stayed
outside the earned machinery throughout the campaign, by explicit
owner instruction, and remain external data.
