## ADDED FREEZE CONDITION (owner, 2026-09-21) — the IRREDUCIBLY_TWO_TIME gate

The protocol MUST distinguish:

- **(A) "no stationary reduction was found"** — a search failed to produce one; and
- **(B) "the declared stationary reduction class is mathematically incompatible
  with the response"** — an exact obstruction, of the kind T3-07 established for
  its tested dressed-stationary class.

`IRREDUCIBLY_TWO_TIME` may be declared **only if BOTH**:

1. the pre-registered reduction tests fail **within their stated error bounds**
   (an exact/bounded incompatibility, i.e. case B — not merely an unsuccessful
   search, case A); **and**
2. the failure **cannot be attributed to a known incompleteness** — specifically
   not to the missing H⁵ PV contribution, not to the omitted H⁷/H⁸ terms, and
   not to the undefined `Re Σ_R` or any normalization ambiguity.

If the failure is of type (A), or is attributable to any listed incompleteness,
the outcome is `REDUCES_UNDER_DECLARED_APPROXIMATION` (if a bounded reduction
survives), `APPARATUS_INSUFFICIENT_WITHOUT_RE_SIGMA` (if the obstruction traces
to the deferred/absent pieces), or `UNEXPECTED_STRUCTURE`. **The narrative that
the response "should" be two-time is not evidence; the bounds and the
attribution check decide.**
