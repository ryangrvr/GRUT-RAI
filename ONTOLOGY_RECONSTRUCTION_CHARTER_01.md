## THE OWNER'S INSTRUCTION (verbatim; this is the working charter)

> Claude — we are changing the objective of the GRUT program.
> Do NOT open another prediction campaign, do NOT try to rescue retired
> discriminators, and do NOT assume the present GRUT architecture is
> correct.
> I want you to work with me on the actual theory of reality.
> Starting from the committed GRUT results, reconstruct the minimum
> ontology required by everything that has survived testing.
> The central question is:
> **What mathematical ontology can generate the reality we experience,
> rather than merely parameterize it?**
> Use the strongest surviving chain as the starting point:
> finite strictly local dynamics → infinite-volume continuum → continuous
> spectral degrees of freedom → persistent memory → emergent
> dissipation/irreversibility → general two-time kernel K(t,t′) →
> stationary K(Δt) only when a symmetry/state actually warrants it.
> Now build a dependency graph for the entire theory.
> For every rung, distinguish rigorously between:
> **DERIVED** — follows from existing calculations;
> **CONDITIONALLY DERIVED** — follows only after an explicitly stated
> additional assumption;
> **EMPIRICAL INPUT** — must be measured;
> **OPEN** — mathematically unresolved;
> **DISDERIVED** — tested and ruled out.
> Investigate, in order:
> 1. **Primitive ontology:** what are the actual microscopic degrees of
>    freedom? What locality means mathematically. What dynamics they obey.
>    Whether finite-dimensional local state spaces are sufficient.
> 2. **Continuum:** reconstruct exactly how continuum spacetime-like
>    variables arise from the microscopic system. Identify which continuum
>    structures are universal and which depend on microscopic details.
> 3. **Spectral/memory structure:** treat K(t,t′), not K(t−t′), as the
>    general object. Determine what structure produces the kernel.
>    Determine when stationarity, time-translation invariance, KMS
>    structure, and a relaxation-time description emerge.
> 4. **Time:** determine whether physical time is primitive or an emergent
>    ordering parameter. Do not assume the answer. Relate this explicitly
>    to the emergence of irreversibility and the thermodynamic limit.
> 5. **Geometry:** determine whether metric geometry can emerge from
>    propagation, correlations, causal structure, spectral data, or an
>    effective response tensor. Identify the minimum additional structure
>    required. Do not merely relabel the existing metric as emergent.
> 6. **Quantum mechanics:** start from the known result that ℏ did not
>    emerge from the tested GRUT construction. Determine whether quantum
>    structure can nevertheless arise from a deeper ontology. Explicitly
>    identify the missing ingredient if not. Separate emergence of complex
>    amplitudes, noncommutativity, interference, and Born probabilities;
>    they need not have the same origin.
> 7. **Matter:** determine whether particles and quantum fields can be
>    identified with stable collective modes, defects, topological
>    sectors, resonances, or other excitations of the underlying
>    substrate. Do not assume one mechanism.
> 8. **Gravity:** determine whether gravitational response is an emergent
>    constitutive/collective response of the same underlying system. The
>    gravity-as-bath result is currently only spectrally viable, and the
>    acoustic Cherenkov channel was disderived. Preserve those
>    constraints. Find physically admissible channels rather than forcing
>    the acoustic one.
> 9. **Gauge structure:** determine whether gauge symmetry is fundamental,
>    emergent, or a redundancy of the effective description. Explain what
>    mathematical mechanism would produce SU(2)×U(1), SU(3), etc., if the
>    ontology can produce them at all.
> 10. **Classicality:** determine how macroscopic persistent classical
>     structures arise from the microscopic theory.
> 11. **Observers:** determine whether an observer can be defined as a
>     persistent information-processing subsystem of the same ontology. Do
>     not introduce consciousness as a primitive unless the mathematics
>     forces it.
> 12. **Reality:** at the end, write the smallest candidate axiomatic
>     system from which the above structures could in principle be derived.
> For every proposed connection, ask: **What calculation would distinguish
> this from an arbitrary philosophical story?**
> The final deliverable should NOT be a polished theory claiming success.
> It should be:
> **A. ONTOLOGY** — what exists;
> **B. DYNAMICS** — what it does;
> **C. EMERGENCE MAP** — how higher-level structures arise;
> **D. FREE INPUTS** — what cannot currently be derived;
> **E. DISDERIVED STRUCTURES** — what the program has ruled out;
> **F. OPEN DERIVATIONS** — the smallest number of mathematical problems
> that must be solved;
> **G. MINIMUM CORE** — the shortest candidate mathematical statement of
> GRUT as a theory of reality.
> Most importantly: **do not optimize for making GRUT work. Optimize for
> discovering what the calculations force reality to be.**
> If the result requires replacing the current GRUT ontology, do it.
> If it shows that GRUT cannot be a complete theory without additional
> primitives, say so.
> If it reveals a much simpler underlying structure than the current
> theory, prefer the simpler structure.
