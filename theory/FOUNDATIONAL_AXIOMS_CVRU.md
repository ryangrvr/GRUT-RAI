## Axiom Set (A1–A6)

**A1. Closedness:**
The universe is a closed system (no external degrees of freedom).

**A2. Responsiveness:**
The substrate responds to all admissible probes (no hidden sectors).

**A3. Viscoelasticity:**
The substrate exhibits viscoelastic response (combines elastic and dissipative features; supports Kramers-Kronig relations).

**A4. Geometric Selection:**
Operator selection is determined by geometric/topological constraints (e.g., Euler-Gauss-Bonnet uniqueness on S⁴).

**A5. Convention Closure:**
All conventions (prescriptions, criteria, protocols) are pre-registered and closed before execution (no post-hoc tuning).

**A6. Epistemic Honesty:**
All results, failures, and regime boundaries are documented honestly; negative results are regime characterizations, not deficiencies.
