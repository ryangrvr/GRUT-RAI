## 1. DC-1: CONFIRMED, kept exactly as frozen

> Coupling a declared bath to the C-B system through a coupling that is not already declared in the
> record counts as **new structure**.

Such a construction:
- does **not** satisfy O-2 (FORMULABLE-PHYSICAL-BATH-TEST);
- receives the sub-label **FORMULABLE-ONLY-WITH-NEW-COUPLING** if the missing coupling is the only
  obstacle;
- may not be upgraded merely because a standard Caldeira–Leggett/FKM/oscillator-bath coupling could be
  written down.

**Reason:** S2-HB audits what the current record already supplies. It is not an invitation to design
the missing bath after seeing the target discriminator. A new system–bath coupling is theorem-bearing
physical structure. It may be the subject of a later campaign, but it cannot be smuggled into O-2 as
bookkeeping.

## 2. O-4 versus O-5: apply the frozen predicates literally

Suppose a candidate:
- passes P-1 … P-8 (so it is HB-P-admissible);
- reaches only R-2;
- has no declared route to R-DISC, and is not formulable for one without new structure.

Then **O-4 SECOND-ORDER-ONLY is true** and **O-5 NO-PHYSICAL-BATH-CANDIDATE is false**. "Cannot
formulate C-B without new coupling" must not erase an otherwise HB-P-admissible R-2 bath.

**O-5 is read narrowly:**

> **There is no HB-P-admissible physical-bath candidate at all at the frozen scope.**

A candidate may carry FORMULABLE-ONLY-WITH-NEW-COUPLING while O-4 is true.
