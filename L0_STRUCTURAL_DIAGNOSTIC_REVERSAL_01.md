## 2. Two cautions recorded before the criteria (operator)

1. **One arm of the apparent inversion is an identity.** Additive noise
   leaves the mean response exactly unchanged (F-5, theorem), so
   "noise change → response untouched" is definitional. Any
   response-vs-correlation asymmetry that rests on it is partly forced,
   and it must not count as emergent structure.
2. **Response ↔ correlation already has a textbook duality:** the
   fluctuation–dissipation relation, −C′(τ) = T·k_resp(τ), which holds
   exactly at the FDT point (L0-1e §3.2). Part of what reads as a
   reversal may be FDT and its controlled breakdown (the cone). **A
   reversal counts as new only if it is not reducible to FDT or to any
   other identity already on the record.**
3. **The observed contrast is confounded on three axes at once:**
   object (response vs correlation), route (generator K vs noise Q),
   and substrate (ring vs chain). To tell "response ↔ correlation"
   apart from "K ↔ Q" would need the **crossed cells**: generator
   affinity's effect on *correlation* CM, with the noise held FDT-like.
   (The other crossed cell, noise on *response*, is empty by F-5.) That
   cell is outside the closed floor list and belongs on the successor
   list. This diagnostic records the need; it charters nothing.

## 3. The pre-registered criteria (frozen here)

**The graph 𝒢.** Nodes are of two kinds:
- **ingredients:** every deleted or held structural axiom on the
  record (gap, passivity/accretivity, locality, linearity, cycle
  affinity, noise–dissipation match, determinism, ordering, …);
- **properties:** every earned predicate or component (P_memory,
  P_positivity (a)/(b)/(c), P_geometry, P_exact-reduction, P^resp,
  P^corr, …).

An edge exists **only** where an accepted owner ruling certifies it:
- **ingredient → property:** a NECESSITY-CERTIFIED or LOAD-BEARING
  line, citing the ruling;
- **property → ingredient:** a certified record showing that a property
  earned at one level **constructs, implies, or is required as** an
  ingredient at another level. For example, an earned relational
  geometry proven to fix a locality structure that another fork takes
  as input.

Interpretations, readings, owner or operator glosses, and "suggests"
statements create **no** edges.

**Nontrivial reversal (the diagnostic's positive outcome):** a
directed cycle in 𝒢 that
1. contains at least one property → ingredient edge;
2. has every edge certified per the rule above;
3. has no edge that is a definitional identity (F-5-type) or reducible
   to FDT or to another identity already recorded.

**Self-duality (the stronger positive outcome):** an explicit,
record-certified map exchanging two subgraphs of 𝒢 that preserves
their edges, and that is **not** FDT or its equilibrium restriction.

**Death criterion (the owner's "dies cleanly"):** at synthesis time, if
𝒢 is acyclic, or every cycle fails condition 2 or 3, and no qualifying
self-duality exists, the hypothesis is recorded as **NOT SUPPORTED at
recorded scope.** It is closed with no re-framing and no search for an
alternative diagram.

## 4. The current state of 𝒢 (for the record, not an evaluation)

Every certified edge so far runs ingredient → property: gap → P_memory;
passivity → P_positivity; locality → P_geometry; linearity →
P_exact-reduction; cycle affinity → loss of P_positivity (c) of the
response. **No property → ingredient edge is certified anywhere on the
record.** 𝒢 is therefore currently bipartite and acyclic, so no
reversal exists yet. That is expected and not a verdict: the descent
has so far only *deleted* ingredients. A property → ingredient edge
could only come from work that constructs an ingredient out of an
earned property. The state-exclusion route and the "geometry from
response" questions are where such edges would first become possible.
